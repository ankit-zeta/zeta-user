💰 Budget Calculation Formula

Monthly Ad Budget Formula:

(Target Revenue × Customer Acquisition Cost) ÷ Average Order Value = Monthly Budget

📈 ROI Tracking

Metric Formula Target
ROAS Revenue ÷ Ad Spend 4x minimum
CAC Total Spend ÷ Customers < 30% of AOV
CTR Clicks ÷ Impressions × 100 > 2%
Conversion Rate Conversions ÷ Clicks × 100 > 2%

💡 Budget Tips

• Start with ₹500/day, scale based on ROAS
• Never spend more than 30% of revenue on ads
• Allocate 20% of budget to testing new creatives
• Review and optimize weekly — kill underperformers fast
• Factor in ₹2-5 per click for Indian audiences
