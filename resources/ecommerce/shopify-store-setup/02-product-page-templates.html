📄 Product Page Structure

High-Converting Product Page Layout

1. HERO IMAGE (High-quality, white background) • 4-6 product photos from different angles • Zoom functionality • Lifestyle images showing product in use 2. PRODUCT TITLE + PRICE • Clear, descriptive title (include keywords) • Price with strikethrough for discounts • "Save ₹X" badge 3. BUY BUTTON (Above the fold) • Prominent, contrasting color • "Add to Cart" or "Buy Now" • Quantity selector 4. KEY BENEFITS (3-4 bullet points) • What problem does this solve? • Why should they buy from you? • What makes it different? 5. DETAILED DESCRIPTION • Features with icons • Specifications • Materials/Care instructions 6. SOCIAL PROOF • Star ratings • Customer reviews with photos • "X people bought this" 7. FAQ SECTION • Shipping info • Return policy • Size guide

💡 Product Page Tips

• Mobile-first design — 70%+ Indian shoppers browse on mobile
• Fast loading — every second delay costs 7% conversions
• Clear CTA above the fold
• Use urgency: "Only X left in stock"
• Show trust badges: Secure payment, Easy returns
