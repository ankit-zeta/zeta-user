Theme Customization Guide

Customize your Shopify theme for conversions — not just good looks

🎨 Customization Checklist

  • Upload logo + favicon
    High-res logo (PNG with transparent background). Favicon = brand recognition.
  • Set brand colors
    Primary, secondary, and accent colors. Use color psychology.
  • Choose fonts
    Max 2 fonts. Readable on mobile. Match brand personality.
  • Configure homepage sections
    Hero banner, featured products, testimonials, newsletter signup.
  • Set up navigation menu
    Clear categories, search bar, cart icon. Keep it simple.
  • Footer setup
    Contact info, social links, payment icons, policy links.

💡 Design Principles

• White space is your friend — don't overcrowd
• High-contrast CTA buttons — make them impossible to miss
• Mobile-first — design for phones, enhance for desktop
• Consistent branding — every page should feel like the same store
• Fast loading — minimize apps and heavy images
