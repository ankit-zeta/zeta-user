Store Launch Checklist

Launch your Shopify store in 7 days — step-by-step

📦 Day 1-2: Foundation

  • Choose and register domain name
    Keep it short, memorable, and easy to spell. Avoid hyphens.
  • Set up Shopify account
    Start with Basic plan (₹1,499/month). Upgrade later.
  • Choose and customize theme
    Use free themes like Dawn. Focus on mobile-first design.
  • Set up payment gateway
    Enable Razorpay, PhonePe, and COD for Indian customers.

📋 Day 3-4: Products

  • Add first 10-20 products
    High-quality photos, detailed descriptions, SEO-friendly titles.
  • Set up collections/categories
    Organize products logically. Create featured collections.
  • Configure shipping rates
    Free shipping threshold (₹999+), flat rate, or weight-based.
  • Set up tax settings
    Configure GST. Most products = 18% GST in India.

🚀 Day 5-7: Launch

  • Create essential pages
    About Us, Contact, FAQ, Privacy Policy, Terms of Service.
  • Set up Google Analytics
    Track visitor behavior, conversions, and marketing effectiveness.
  • Test checkout process
    Place test orders. Verify emails work. Check mobile experience.
  • Launch with a promotion
    10-20% off first order, free shipping, or gift with purchase.

💡 Launch Day Checklist

• Test on multiple devices (phone, tablet, desktop)
• Have customer support ready (WhatsApp/Email)
• Prepare social media posts for launch
• Send launch email to your list
• Monitor for any issues in first 24 hours
