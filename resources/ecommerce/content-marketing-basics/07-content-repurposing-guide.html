Content Repurposing Guide

Create once, distribute everywhere — because creating content from scratch every time burns you out

🔄 Repurposing Framework

Blog Post → 10+ Pieces

STARTING POINT: 1 Blog Post (2000 words) REPURPOSE INTO: 1. Twitter/X thread (10-15 tweets) 2. LinkedIn article (condensed version) 3. Instagram carousel (8-10 slides) 4. Instagram Reel (60-second summary) 5. YouTube video (full tutorial) 6. Podcast episode (discuss key points) 7. Email newsletter (subscriber-only) 8. Quora answers (link back to post) 9. Pinterest pins (infographics) 10. TikTok video (quick tips)

📱 Platform-Specific Tips

Adaptation Rules

INSTAGRAM: Visual-first, short captions, hashtags TWITTER/X: Concise, thread format, engagement hooks LINKEDIN: Professional tone, longer posts, industry insights YOUTUBE: In-depth, tutorial-style, SEO titles PODCAST: Conversational, storytelling, interviews EMAIL: Personal, exclusive, direct CTA

💡 Repurposing Tips

• Always link back to original content (drives traffic)
• Adapt tone for each platform (don't just copy-paste)
• Use Canva to quickly create platform-specific visuals
• Schedule repurposed content across the week
• Track which platforms drive the most traffic
