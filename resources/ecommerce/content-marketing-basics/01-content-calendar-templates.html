📝 Content Mix Formula

80/20 Content Rule

80% VALUE CONTENT: • Educational posts (teach something) • Entertaining content (memes, stories) • Inspiring content (success stories) • Engaging content (polls, questions) 20% PROMOTIONAL: • Product/service mentions • Sales/launches • Testimonials • Case studies

💡 Content Tips

• Batch create — write 4 posts in one sitting
• Repurpose everywhere — one blog = 5 social posts
• Schedule in advance using Buffer/Hootsuite
• Track what works and double down on it
• Quality > Quantity — one great post beats five mediocre ones
