📊 Key Metrics to Track

Metric Where to Track Good Benchmark

💡 Analytics Tips

• Set up goals in Google Analytics (purchases, signups, etc.)
• Review weekly, not daily (daily fluctuations are noise)
• Compare month-over-month and year-over-year
• Create a simple spreadsheet dashboard
• Focus on trends, not individual data points
