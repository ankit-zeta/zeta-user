Blog Post Templates

Write blog posts that rank and convert — because blogging is still the #1 SEO strategy

📝 How-To Post Template

Structure (1500-2500 words)

H1: How to [Achieve Result] in [Timeframe] (Without [Common Pain Point]) INTRO (100 words) • Hook: Start with the problem • Promise: What they'll learn • Proof: Your credibility H2: Why [Topic] Matters • Stats/facts • Common mistakes H2: Step-by-Step Guide • H3: Step 1 — [Action] • H3: Step 2 — [Action] • H3: Step 3 — [Action] • H3: Step 4 — [Action] H2: Pro Tips • Advanced strategies • Common pitfalls to avoid H2: FAQ Section • 3-5 related questions H2: Conclusion • Summary • CTA (what to do next)

📊 List Post Template

Structure (1500-2000 words)

H1: [Number] [Topic] Tips/Strategies/Tools for [Year] INTRO • What this list covers • Who it's for BODY • H2: #1 — [Item Name] • What it is • Why it matters • How to implement • H2: #2 — [Item Name] • What it is • Why it matters • How to implement [Repeat for each item] H2: Conclusion • Quick recap • CTA

💡 Blog Tips

• Target long-tail keywords (lower competition, higher intent)
• Use headers (H2, H3) for readability and SEO
• Include images, infographics, or videos
• Internal link to your products/services
• End every post with a clear CTA
