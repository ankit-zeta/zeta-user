Email Marketing Templates

Build an email list that converts — because you own your email list (unlike social followers)

📧 Welcome Sequence (5 emails)

Email 1: Welcome (Day 0)

Subject: Welcome to [Brand] — here's what to expect Hi [Name], Thanks for joining [Brand]! Here's what you'll get: • [Benefit 1] • [Benefit 2] • [Benefit 3] As a welcome gift, here's [free resource]. Talk soon, [Your Name]

Email 2: Value (Day 2)

Subject: The [topic] mistake most people make Hi [Name], Most people struggle with [problem]. Here's the fix: [Solution in 2-3 sentences] [Action step] See you next time, [Your Name]

Email 3: Story (Day 4)

Subject: How I [achieved result] in [timeframe] Hi [Name], I used to struggle with [problem]. Then I discovered [solution]. Here's what happened: [Result] If you want the same result: [CTA] Best, [Your Name]

Email 4: Social Proof (Day 6)

Subject: "[Testimonial quote]" — [Customer Name] Hi [Name], [Customer] was struggling with [problem]. After using [your solution], they [result]. Here's what they said: "[Full testimonial]" Want similar results? [CTA] [Your Name]

Email 5: Soft Sell (Day 8)

Subject: Quick question, [Name] Hi [Name], Over the past week, I've shared: • [Tip 1] • [Tip 2] • [Tip 3] If you want to go deeper, I created [product/service]. It's designed to help you [benefit]. Here's the link: [CTA] No pressure — just wanted to let you know. [Your Name]

💡 Email Tips

• Send 1-2 emails per week (not more)
• Always provide value before selling
• Personalize with first name
• Test subject lines (A/B test for 20% open rate lift)
• Clean your list quarterly (remove inactive subscribers)
