SEO Keyword Research Guide

Find keywords people actually search — because writing without keywords is like fishing without bait

🔍 Keyword Research Process

5-Step Process

1. BRAINSTORM SEED KEYWORDS • What does your audience search for? • What problems do they have? • What solutions do they need? 2. USE FREE TOOLS • Google Keyword Planner • Ubersuggest (free tier) • AnswerThePublic • Google "People Also Ask" 3. EVALUATE KEYWORDS • Search volume (monthly searches) • Competition (high/medium/low) • Search intent (informational/commercial/transactional) 4. GROUP BY INTENT • Informational: "how to", "what is", "guide" • Commercial: "best", "top", "review", "vs" • Transactional: "buy", "price", "discount", "near me" 5. PICK YOUR TARGETS • Start with low competition, medium volume • Create content for each intent type • Map keywords to content pieces

🇮🇳 India-Specific Tips

Indian SEO Considerations

• Include Hindi keywords (even if content is English) • Target "near me" searches (local SEO) • Use Indian English terms ("shop" not "store") • Consider regional language content • Target tier 2/3 cities (less competition) • Use Indian price format (₹ not $)

💡 Keyword Tips

• Target long-tail keywords (3+ words) — easier to rank
• One primary keyword per blog post
• Include keyword in title, first paragraph, and headers
• Don't keyword stuff — write naturally
• Update old content with new keywords quarterly
