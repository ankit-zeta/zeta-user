Video Script Framework

Write scripts that hold attention — because viewers decide in 3 seconds whether to stay

🎬 Short-Form Video Script (Reels/TikTok)

30-60 Second Script Template

HOOK (0-3 seconds) "The biggest mistake [audience] makes with [topic]..." PROBLEM (3-10 seconds) "Most people think [common belief]. But actually..." SOLUTION (10-40 seconds) "Here's what works instead: 1. [Step 1] 2. [Step 2] 3. [Step 3]" CTA (40-60 seconds) "Follow for more [topic] tips. Save this for later."

🎥 Long-Form Video Script (YouTube)

8-15 Minute Script Template

INTRO (0-30 seconds) • Hook: Bold promise or shocking fact • What they'll learn • Why it matters PROBLEM (30-90 seconds) • Pain point elaboration • Why most people fail • Common mistakes MAIN CONTENT (90% of video) • H2: Key Point 1 • Explanation • Example • Demonstration • H2: Key Point 2 • Explanation • Example • Demonstration • H2: Key Point 3 • Explanation • Example • Demonstration CONCLUSION (last 30 seconds) • Quick recap • CTA (subscribe, like, comment) • Teaser for next video

💡 Video Tips

• Hook in first 3 seconds — or they're gone
• Use captions — 85% watch without sound
• Keep energy high — speak with enthusiasm
• End with a question to drive comments
• Batch record — film multiple videos in one session
