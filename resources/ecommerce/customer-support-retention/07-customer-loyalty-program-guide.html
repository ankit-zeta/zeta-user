Customer Loyalty Program Guide

Reward your best customers — because loyal customers are worth 10x more than new ones

🏆 Loyalty Program Models

Model How It Works Best For
Points System Earn points for purchases, redeem for rewards E-commerce, frequent purchases
Tier System Unlock tiers based on spending (Silver/Gold/Platinum) High-value customers, status-driven
Cashback Get percentage back on purchases Price-sensitive markets (India!)
Referral Program Reward for bringing new customers Growth-focused businesses
Subscription Monthly fee for exclusive benefits Content, SaaS, membership sites

🇮🇳 India-Specific Ideas

What Works in India

• UPI cashback rewards (familiar and loved) • Festival season bonuses (Diwali, New Year) • Free shipping on loyalty members • Early access to sales/new products • Exclusive WhatsApp group for VIPs • Birthday/anniversary rewards

💡 Loyalty Tips

• Keep it simple — if it's complicated, people won't join
• Make rewards attainable (not just for whales)
• Communicate program benefits clearly
• Surprise loyal customers with unexpected perks
• Track program ROI — it should increase LTV
