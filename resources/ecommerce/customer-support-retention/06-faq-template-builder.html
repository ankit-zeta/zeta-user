✍️ Writing Good FAQ Answers

FAQ Answer Formula

SHORT ANSWER (1-2 sentences): Direct answer to the question. DETAILED EXPLANATION (if needed): More context, steps, or examples. LINK/CTA (if applicable): "Learn more: [link]" or "Contact us: [link]" Example: Q: "How do I get a refund?" A: "You can request a refund within 7 days of purchase. Go to Settings > Purchases > Request Refund. Need help? Email support@zetagrow.in"

💡 FAQ Tips

• Track support tickets — top questions become FAQs
• Update FAQs monthly based on new questions
• Use search-friendly language (match how people ask)
• Include screenshots for technical questions
• Place FAQs on checkout page to reduce cart abandonment
