Ticket Resolution Framework

Resolve issues fast — because a happy customer tells 3 people, an unhappy one tells 30

📋 Resolution Process

5-Step Resolution Framework

STEP 1: ACKNOWLEDGE (within 1 hour) "I received your message. I'm looking into this right now." STEP 2: DIAGNOSE (within 4 hours) Ask clarifying questions. Identify root cause. STEP 3: SOLVE (within 24 hours) Provide clear solution or workaround. STEP 4: FOLLOW UP (within 48 hours) "Is everything working now? Anything else I can help with?" STEP 5: DOCUMENT Log the issue and solution for future reference.

🎯 Ticket Priority Matrix

Priority Response Time Resolution Time Example
Critical 15 minutes 4 hours Payment issue, account locked
High 1 hour 24 hours Course not accessible, login problem
Medium 4 hours 48 hours Content question, feature request
Low 24 hours 72 hours General inquiry, feedback

💡 Resolution Tips

• Always acknowledge first — silence is the worst response
• Use templates for common issues (but personalize them)
• Escalate when you're stuck — don't let tickets sit
• Track resolution times — aim to improve weekly
• Turn resolved tickets into knowledge base articles
