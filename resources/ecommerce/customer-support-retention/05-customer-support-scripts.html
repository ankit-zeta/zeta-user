Customer Support Scripts

Handle any situation professionally — because how you handle problems defines your brand

💬 Common Scenarios

Refund Request

CUSTOMER: "I want a refund." YOU: "I'm sorry to hear you're not satisfied. Could you tell me what went wrong so I can help?" [Listen to reason] IF valid: "I understand. I've processed your refund. You'll see it in 3-5 business days. Is there anything else I can help with?" IF not valid per policy: "I appreciate your feedback. Unfortunately, our refund policy states [policy]. However, I can offer [alternative: discount, support, etc.]" ALWAYS: End with "Is there anything else I can help with?"

Technical Issue

CUSTOMER: "Something isn't working." YOU: "I'm sorry about that. Let me help fix this. Could you tell me: 1. What device/browser are you using? 2. What exactly happens when you try? 3. Can you share a screenshot?" [Provide solution or escalate] "I've [fixed the issue / escalated to our team]. You should see it working within [timeframe]. I'll follow up to make sure."

Angry Customer

CUSTOMER: [Frustrated/angry message] YOU: "I completely understand your frustration, and I'm sorry for the experience you've had. This isn't the standard we aim for." "Let me take care of this right away. Here's what I'm going to do: [specific action]." "I'll personally make sure this is resolved by [timeframe]. Can I reach you at this email/number?" ALWAYS: Stay calm, empathize, take ownership, provide specific next steps.

💡 Script Tips

• Always acknowledge feelings first, then solve problem
• Use "I" not "we" — take personal ownership
• Be specific about next steps and timelines
• Follow up even after resolution — it builds trust
• Document everything for future reference
