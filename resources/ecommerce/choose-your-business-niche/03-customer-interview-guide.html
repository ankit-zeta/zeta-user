Customer Interview Guide

Talk to real customers — because assumptions are the mother of all failures

🎤 Interview Questions

Problem Discovery Questions

1. "Tell me about the last time you faced [problem]." 2. "What was happening right before that?" 3. "How did you try to solve it?" 4. "What was frustrating about those solutions?" 5. "If you had a magic wand, what would the perfect solution look like?"

Buying Behavior Questions

1. "What have you already tried to buy for this?" 2. "What made you choose that solution?" 3. "What almost stopped you from buying?" 4. "How do you research before buying?" 5. "What would make you switch from your current solution?"

Pricing Questions

1. "What have you paid for similar solutions before?" 2. "What's your budget for solving this problem?" 3. "What would be too expensive? What would be too cheap?" 4. "Would you prefer one-time payment or subscription?"

💡 Interview Tips

• Talk to 10-15 people before making decisions
• Listen more than you talk (80/20 rule)
• Don't pitch your idea — just understand their problems
• Ask "why" at least 3 times to get to root causes
• Record interviews (with permission) for later analysis
