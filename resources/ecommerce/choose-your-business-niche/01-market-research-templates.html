🔍 Niche Research Framework

Niche Evaluation Scorecard

Rate each criterion 1-10: PASSION & KNOWLEDGE (Score: __/10) • Do you enjoy this topic? • Do you have experience/expertise? • Can you create content about it? MARKET DEMAND (Score: __/10) • Are people searching for this? (Google Trends) • Are there active communities? (Facebook, Reddit) • Are people spending money? (Courses, products) COMPETITION (Score: __/10) • How many competitors exist? • Can you differentiate? • Is there room for a new player? MONETIZATION (Score: __/10) • Can you sell products/services? • Are there affiliate programs? • Can you create digital products? TOTAL SCORE: __/40 30-40: Strong niche — pursue it 20-29: Decent — refine your angle Below 20: Consider other options

🇮🇳 India-Specific Research

Indian Market Research Checklist

✓ Google Trends India (filter by country) ✓ Check Amazon.in bestsellers in category ✓ Browse Indian subreddits (r/india, r/IndianEntrepreneurs) ✓ Check Indian Facebook groups for activity ✓ Research Indian competitors (not just global) ✓ Understand Indian pricing (₹ vs $) ✓ Consider regional language opportunities ✓ Check Indian payment preferences (UPI, COD)

💡 Research Tips

• Spend 2-3 weeks researching before committing
• Talk to 10+ potential customers before building
• Look for "pain点" people are already paying to solve
• Start narrow — you can always expand later
• Test with a minimum viable product first
