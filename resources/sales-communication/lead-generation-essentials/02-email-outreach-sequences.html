Email Outreach Sequences

Cold email templates that actually get replies — because nobody likes spam

📧 Sequence 1: The Value-First Approach

Email 1: Day 1 — Helpful Observation

Subject: Quick observation about [Company Name] Hi [Name], I was looking at [specific thing - website, ad, social post] and noticed something that could help you [achieve specific result]. I helped [Similar Company] with the same thing and they saw [specific result] within [timeframe]. Would you be open to a 15-minute call? I'll share exactly what we did. [Your Name]

Email 2: Day 3 — Case Study

Subject: How [Client] solved [their problem] Hi [Name], Remember my last email about [their challenge]? Here's a quick case study: [Client] was struggling with [problem]. We implemented [solution] and they saw [specific result] in [timeframe]. Full story: [Link] Worth a quick read? [Your Name]

Email 3: Day 7 — Free Resource

Subject: Free resource for [Company Name] Hi [Name], I put together a free [resource type] specifically for [industry] companies like yours. It covers: • [Benefit 1] • [Benefit 2] • [Benefit 3] Download here: [Link] No strings attached — just thought it would help. [Your Name]

💡 Cold Email Best Practices

• Keep emails under 150 words — busy people skim
• Personalize everything — mention their company, role, or recent activity
• One clear CTA per email — don't confuse with multiple asks
• Send Tuesday-Thursday, 8-10 AM their time
• Follow up 3-5 times — most replies come after follow-up #3
