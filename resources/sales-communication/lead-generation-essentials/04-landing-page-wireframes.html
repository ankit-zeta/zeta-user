📄 Wireframe 2: Webinar/Workshop Registration

Event Registration Page

Urgency
"Only [X] seats left"

💡 Landing Page Conversion Tips

• One page, one goal — don't distract with navigation
• Headline should match the ad/email that brought them there
• Mobile-first design — 70%+ traffic is mobile in India
• Fast loading — every second of delay costs 7% conversion
• A/B test headlines and CTAs — small changes, big results
