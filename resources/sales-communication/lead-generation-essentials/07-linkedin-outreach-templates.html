LinkedIn Outreach Templates

LinkedIn DM templates that start real conversations — not spam

💼 Connection Request Messages

Template 1: Common Ground

Hi [Name], I noticed we're both in the [industry] space. I've been following your work on [topic] and found your recent post about [specific post] really insightful. Would love to connect and exchange ideas. No pitch — just genuinely interested in connecting with fellow professionals. [Your Name]

Template 2: Value-Add

Hi [Name], I came across your profile and saw you're working on [their project/challenge]. I recently helped [Client] with something similar and achieved [result]. Would love to connect and share what worked for us — no strings attached. [Your Name]

📩 Follow-Up Messages

Follow-Up 1: Value First

Hi [Name], Thanks for connecting! I wanted to share something I thought might help with [their challenge]: [Link to valuable content / quick tip] No agenda — just thought it would be useful given what you're working on. If you ever want to chat about [topic], I'm happy to share more insights. [Your Name]

💡 LinkedIn Outreach Rules

• Personalize every message — never use generic templates
• Engage with their content before reaching out
• Keep messages under 100 words
• Don't pitch in the first message — build relationship first
• Follow LinkedIn's daily connection limits (20-30/day)
• Provide value before asking for anything
