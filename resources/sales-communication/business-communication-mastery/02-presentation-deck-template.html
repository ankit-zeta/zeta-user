Presentation Deck Template

10-slide pitch deck template that wins clients and closes deals

📊 The 10-Slide Framework

Slide 1: Title

• Company Name + Logo
• One-line tagline
• Presenter name + date
• Contact information

Slide 2: Problem

• What problem are you solving?
• Who has this problem?
• How big is the problem?
• Current solutions and why they fail

Slide 3: Solution

• Your product/service in one sentence
• Key features (3-4 max)
• How it's different/better
• Demo or screenshot

Slide 4: Market Opportunity

• TAM, SAM, SOM
• Target audience size
• Growth trends
• Your initial target segment

Slide 5: Business Model

• How you make money
• Pricing structure
• Unit economics
• Customer lifetime value

Slide 6: Traction

• Key metrics (users, revenue, growth)
• Notable clients/partners
• Milestones achieved
• Growth trajectory

Slide 7: Competition

• Competitive landscape
• Your unique advantages
• Why customers choose you
• Barriers to entry for others

Slide 8: Go-to-Market

• Sales channels
• Marketing strategy
• Distribution plan
• Customer acquisition cost

Slide 9: Team

• Founders/Key team
• Relevant experience
• Advisory board
• Why this team wins

Slide 10: Ask

• What you need (investment, partnership, etc.)
• Amount and use of funds
• Expected outcomes
• Next steps + CTA

💡 Presentation Tips

• One idea per slide — don't overwhelm
• Use visuals over text — 60% images, 40% text
• Practice the 10-minute rule — if you can't explain it in 10 minutes, simplify
• End with a clear CTA — what do you want them to do next?
