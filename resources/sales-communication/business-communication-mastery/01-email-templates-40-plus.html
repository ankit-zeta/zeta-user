Email Templates (40+)

Professional email templates for every business situation

💼 Client Communication

Template 1: Project Kickoff Email

Subject: Let's Get Started — [Project Name] Hi [Client Name], Excited to kick off [Project Name]! Here's what to expect: 📅 Timeline: [Start Date] - [End Date] 🎯 Goal: [Project objective] 📋 Next Steps: [Immediate action items] I'll send you weekly updates every [Day]. If you need anything before then, just reply to this email. Looking forward to working together! [Your Name]

Template 2: Progress Update

Subject: [Project Name] Update — Week [X] Hi [Client Name], Here's your weekly update: ✅ Completed This Week: • [Milestone 1] • [Milestone 2] 🔄 In Progress: • [Current task] 📅 Next Week: • [Upcoming task] Any questions? Let's hop on a quick call. [Your Name]

Template 3: Delay Notification

Subject: Update on [Project Name] Timeline Hi [Client Name], I wanted to give you a heads up about [Project Name]. Due to [reason], we'll need [X] additional days to complete [specific task]. New completion date: [Date] I apologize for the inconvenience. To make up for it, I'll [compensation - e.g., add extra feature, expedite another part]. Let me know if this works for you. [Your Name]

🤝 Networking Emails

Template 4: Introduction Request

Subject: Quick favor, [Name] Hi [Name], I noticed you're connected with [Person] at [Company]. I'd love to get introduced to discuss [specific topic]. Would you be willing to make an introduction? I've prepared a brief note you can forward: "Hi [Person], I'd like to introduce [Your Name] who specializes in [topic]. They had a question about [specific area]. Would you be open to a quick chat?" Thanks in advance! [Your Name]

💡 Email Best Practices

• Subject line should be clear and specific — no clickbait
• Keep emails under 200 words for busy recipients
• One clear CTA per email
• Use their name — emails with names get 26% more opens
• Proofread before sending — errors kill credibility
