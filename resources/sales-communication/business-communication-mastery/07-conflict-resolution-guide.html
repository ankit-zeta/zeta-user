Conflict Resolution Guide

Turn conflicts into opportunities — because how you handle problems defines your reputation

🔄 The HEARD Framework

H - Hear

Listen fully without interrupting. Let them express their frustration completely.

E - Empathize

Validate their feelings. "I understand why you feel that way."

A - Apologize

Take responsibility where appropriate. "I'm sorry this happened."

R - Resolve

Offer a clear solution. "Here's what I'll do to fix this."

D - Document

Follow up in writing. Confirm what was agreed. Prevent recurrence.

💬 Difficult Conversation Scripts

When You Made a Mistake

"I need to apologize. I made a mistake with [specific issue]. I take full responsibility. Here's what happened: [brief explanation] Here's how I'll fix it: [specific solution] Here's how I'll prevent it from happening again: [prevention measure] I value our relationship and I'm committed to making this right."

When They're Upset About Delays

"I completely understand your frustration. The delay is not acceptable and I apologize. Here's the current status: [honest update] Here's the new timeline: [realistic commitment] I'll personally ensure this stays on track. You'll receive updates every [frequency] until this is complete."

💡 Conflict Resolution Rules

• Respond within 24 hours — delays make problems worse
• Never get defensive — listen first, respond second
• Focus on solutions, not blame
• Document everything in writing
• Follow up to ensure satisfaction — turn critics into advocates
