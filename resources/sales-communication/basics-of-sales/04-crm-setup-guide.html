CRM Setup Guide

Set up your first CRM in 30 minutes — organize leads, track deals, never miss a follow-up

📋 Step 1: Choose Your CRM

CRM Best For Price
HubSpot Beginners, small teams Free (paid plans from $45/mo)
Zoho CRM Budget-conscious, India-based Free (paid plans from ₹800/mo)
Pipedrive Sales-focused, visual pipeline From $14/user/mo
Freshsales Indian businesses, phone integration Free (paid plans from ₹999/mo)

🔧 Step 2: Initial Setup Checklist

  • Create your account
    Sign up at your chosen CRM. Use your work email for credibility.
  • Set up your profile
    Add your name, photo, company info, and contact details.
  • Connect your email
    Sync Gmail/Outlook for automatic email tracking.
  • Connect your phone
    Enable call logging and SMS tracking if available.
  • Install mobile app
    Access your CRM on the go for quick updates.

📊 Step 3: Create Your Pipeline

Stage Description Action Required
New Lead Just entered your system Respond within 5 minutes
Contacted You've reached out Schedule follow-up
Qualified Has budget, authority, need, timeline Schedule discovery call
Proposal Sent Sent pricing/proposal Follow up in 3 days
Negotiating Discussing terms Address objections
Closed Won Deal completed Onboard client
Closed Lost Deal didn't close Log reason, schedule follow-up

👥 Step 4: Add Your Contacts

  • Import existing contacts
    Export from Gmail/Excel and import to CRM (CSV format).
  • Add custom fields
    Company, Industry, Source, Budget, Decision Maker?
  • Tag your contacts
    Hot Lead, Cold Lead, Referral, Website, etc.
  • Add notes
    Any context from previous conversations.

⏰ Step 5: Set Up Reminders & Automation

  • Daily reminder: Review pipeline
    Set 9 AM daily reminder to check your pipeline.
  • Follow-up reminders
    Auto-remind 3 days after sending proposal.
  • Email templates
    Save your best emails as templates for quick use.
  • Lead capture forms
    Connect website forms to auto-add leads to CRM.

💡 Quick Win: First 7 Days

Day 1: Set up CRM + import contacts
Day 2: Add 10 leads manually
Day 3: Make 10 calls, log all conversations
Day 4: Send 5 follow-up emails
Day 5: Review pipeline, update stages
Day 6: Set up automation rules
Day 7: Review metrics, adjust approach
