Sales Script Templates

Copy-paste ready scripts for cold calls, follow-ups, and closing conversations

📞 Cold Call Opening Scripts

Script 1: Problem-Aware Lead

Hi [Name], this is [Your Name] from [Company]. I noticed you've been [specific action - e.g., "looking at our pricing page" / "downloaded our guide on X"]. Quick question — are you currently struggling with [specific problem they likely have]? Great, I can help with that. Do you have 2 minutes right now, or should I call back at a better time?

Script 2: Referral Introduction

Hi [Name], this is [Your Name] from [Company]. [Mutual Connection] suggested I reach out to you. They mentioned you're working on [specific project/challenge]. We've helped [Company Type] like [Similar Client] achieve [specific result]. I'd love to explore if we can do the same for you. When would be a good time for a 15-minute call this week?

Script 3: Value-First Approach

Hi [Name], I'm [Your Name] from [Company]. I'm calling because we just published a report on [industry trend] and your company came up as a potential beneficiary. The key finding: [specific insight relevant to them]. Would you like me to send you the full report? I can also walk you through how [specific companies] are using this insight to [specific result].

✉️ Follow-Up Email Templates

Email 1: Post-Call Follow-Up (Within 24 Hours)

Subject: Great connecting, [Name] — next steps Hi [Name], Thanks for taking the time to chat today. I enjoyed learning about [specific thing they mentioned]. As promised, here's [resource/link you promised]: [Insert link] Based on our conversation, I believe we can help you: • [Benefit 1 specific to their situation] • [Benefit 2 specific to their situation] • [Benefit 3 specific to their situation] Would [specific day] at [specific time] work for a deeper dive? Best, [Your Name]

Email 2: Value-Add Follow-Up (Day 3)

Subject: Thought you'd find this useful Hi [Name], I came across this [article/case study/tool] and immediately thought of our conversation about [their challenge]. [Link to valuable resource] Key takeaway: [1-2 sentences summarizing why it's relevant to them] This is exactly what [similar company] used to [specific result they achieved]. Worth a quick read — let me know what you think! [Your Name]

Email 3: Soft Close (Day 7)

Subject: Quick question, [Name] Hi [Name], I know you're busy, so I'll keep this brief. After our conversation, I put together a quick analysis of how we could help [their company] achieve [specific goal]. Here's the summary: • Current situation: [What they told you] • Our approach: [How you'd solve it] • Expected outcome: [Specific result with timeline] Would it be helpful if I walked you through this in a 15-minute call? If the timing isn't right, no worries at all — just let me know and I'll check back in a month. [Your Name]

🎯 Closing Scripts

Assumptive Close

"Based on everything we've discussed, it sounds like [Product/Service] is a great fit for you. Let's get you started — would you prefer the [Option A] or [Option B] plan? I can have everything set up for you by [specific date/time]."

Alternative Close

"I can see you're ready to move forward. We have two options: Option 1: [Describe Option A with price] Option 2: [Describe Option B with price] Which one works better for your budget and timeline?"

Urgency Close

"I want to be transparent — we're [offering X benefit / have Y availability] until [specific date]. After that, [explain what changes - price increases, spots fill up, etc.]. If we move forward today, I can lock in [specific benefit] for you. Should I proceed?"

Summary Close

"Let me make sure I understand correctly: • You need [Requirement 1] • You're looking to achieve [Goal 1] • Your budget is [Budget Range] • You'd like to start by [Timeline] Does that all sound accurate? If so, I have the perfect solution for you..."

💡 Pro Tips

• Always personalize scripts with specific details from your research
• Record yourself practicing to identify areas for improvement
• Keep a "wins" folder of successful conversations to reference
• Adapt scripts based on the prospect's communication style
