📊 Common Pricing Models

Model Best For Pricing Formula
Hourly Ongoing work, unclear scope Hours × Rate
Project Defined scope, clear deliverables Est. Hours × Rate + Overhead
Value-Based High-impact projects % of client's value gain
Retainer Ongoing support, predictable income Monthly fee for X hours
Package Standardized services Good/Better/Best tiers

💡 Pricing Tips for Indian Market

• Start with 20% less than your target, increase as you build portfolio
• Never work for free — offer discounted "beta" pricing instead
• Always get 50% upfront for projects over ₹20,000
• Review and increase rates every 6 months
• Price based on value, not hours worked
