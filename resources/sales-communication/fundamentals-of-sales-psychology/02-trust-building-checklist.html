Trust-Building Checklist

Build trust faster — because people buy from people they trust

🤝 Before the Meeting

  • Research their company
    Know their recent news, products, and challenges before you reach out.
  • Research the person
    Check LinkedIn, recent posts, and shared connections.
  • Prepare relevant case studies
    Have 2-3 examples similar to their industry/situation ready.
  • Test your tech
    Camera, mic, internet — nothing kills trust faster than tech issues.

🗣️ During the Meeting

  • Listen more than you talk
    Aim for 70/30 — they talk 70%, you talk 30%.
  • Ask thoughtful questions
    Show genuine curiosity about their business and challenges.
  • Mirror their communication style
    If they're formal, be formal. If casual, match that energy.
  • Be honest about limitations
    If you can't solve something, say so. Honesty builds trust.
  • Take notes
    Show you value what they're saying by writing it down.

📧 After the Meeting

  • Send follow-up within 24 hours
    Reference specific points from the conversation.
  • Deliver on promises
    If you said you'd send something, send it. Reliability = trust.
  • Share valuable content
    Send articles, tools, or insights relevant to their challenges.
  • Connect on LinkedIn
    Personalized connection request with a note about your meeting.
  • Follow up consistently
    Don't disappear — stay on their radar with value-add touchpoints.

💡 Trust Accelerators

• Use their name in conversation (people love hearing their name)
• Reference mutual connections or shared experiences
• Share client success stories with specific numbers
• Offer a small free win before asking for anything
• Be consistent — follow through every single time
