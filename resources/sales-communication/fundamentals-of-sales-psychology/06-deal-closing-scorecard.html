Deal Closing Scorecard

Rate each deal before closing — know when to push and when to walk away

📊 The MEDDIC Scorecard

Criteria Question to Answer Score (1-5)
M - Metrics Do they have clear success metrics? What ROI do they expect? _____
E - Economic Buyer Can they approve the budget? Who signs the check? _____
D - Decision Criteria What criteria are they using to evaluate solutions? _____
D - Decision Process What's their buying process? Who's involved? _____
I - Identify Pain How urgent is their problem? What's the cost of inaction? _____
C - Champion Do you have an internal advocate pushing for you? _____
C - Competition Who else are they evaluating? What's your differentiator? _____

🎯 Scoring Guide

Total Score Rating Action
28-35 Hot Deal 🔥 Close aggressively. Push for signature this week.
20-27 Warm Deal 🌡️ Identify gaps. Focus on weak areas before pushing to close.
10-19 Cold Deal ❄️ Nurture with value. Don't push to close — build relationship first.
Below 10 No Deal 🚫 Walk away or restart qualification. Wrong fit or wrong timing.

💡 Scoring Tips

• Be honest — inflated scores waste your time
• Review scorecard before every closing call
• If any single criteria scores 1-2, address it first
• Track win rates by score to calibrate your judgment
