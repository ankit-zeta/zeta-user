Negotiation Cheat Sheet

Win-win negotiations — create value, don't just split it

🎯 The 4 Variables to Negotiate

1. Price

What you charge. Never negotiate on price alone — always trade for value.

2. Scope

What's included. More scope = higher price. Less scope = lower price.

3. Timeline

When it's delivered. Faster delivery = premium. Longer timeline = discount.

4. Terms

Payment terms, contracts, support. Annual contracts = discount vs monthly.

💡 Negotiation Tactics

Anchoring

Start higher than your target. Give yourself room to negotiate down while still hitting your goal.

The Flinch

React with mild surprise to their offer. "That's lower than we typically work with." Makes them reconsider.

Broken Record

Repeat your key point calmly. "I understand, but our standard is..." Consistency builds credibility.

Silence

After making an offer, stop talking. Let them fill the silence. Often they'll concede.

🔄 The Trade Matrix

If They Want... Ask For... Example
Lower price Less scope or longer timeline "I can reduce the price if we remove X feature"
Faster delivery Higher price or priority fee "Expedited delivery adds 20% to the project cost"
More features Higher price or extended contract "Adding that feature would be an additional ₹X"
Flexible payment Slightly higher total price "We can do installments, but the total would be ₹X"

💡 Golden Rule

Never negotiate against yourself. Make your offer, then wait. If they push back, ask "What would make this work for you?" Let them solve their own objection.
