AI Product Building Glossary

Key terms for building, deploying, and monetizing AI-powered products

AI Product Concepts

AI-Native Product
Product where AI is core, not bolt-on. AI handles primary value delivery. Example: Jasper, Midjourney, Notion AI.
AI-Enhanced Product
Existing product with AI features added. AI improves, doesn't replace. Example: Gmail Smart Compose, Figma AI.
RAG (Retrieval Augmented Generation)
Retrieves relevant docs before generating. Reduces hallucinations. Vector DB + embeddings + LLM. Core of knowledge-based AI products.
Fine-tuning
Training base model on domain-specific data. Custom behavior, tone, knowledge. Expensive but powerful. OpenAI charges per token.
Prompt Caching
Store and reuse prompt contexts. Reduces cost and latency. OpenAI and Anthropic offer this. Essential for high-traffic products.
Embedding
Convert text to numerical vectors. Used for semantic search, clustering, recommendations. OpenAI ada-002, Cohere embed.
Vector Database
Store and query embeddings. Pinecone, Weaviate, Qdrant, pgvector. Powers semantic search and RAG systems.
AI Agent
Autonomous AI that plans, uses tools, and executes multi-step tasks. LangChain, CrewAI, AutoGen. Future of AI products.

Monetization Terms

Usage-Based Pricing
Pay per API call, token, or generation. Aligns cost with value. OpenAI charges per token. Best for variable usage.
Tiered Pricing
Free, Starter, Pro, Enterprise. Feature gates + usage limits. Most common SaaS model. Reduces friction for new users.
Credit System
Users buy credits, spend per action. Predictable for users, flexible for you. Common in AI content tools.
GPT Store Revenue
OpenAI pays creators based on usage. Build popular Custom GPTs, earn revenue share. Still early but growing.
API Reselling
Call OpenAI/Anthropic API, mark up, resell to users. Build value layer on top. Margin: 30-70% depending on volume.
White-Label AI
License your AI product to other businesses. B2B2C model. Higher ACV, fewer customers. Enterprise sales skills needed.

💡 Pro Tip

AI products fail when they're "cool tech" without clear value. Start with the problem, not the technology. "AI-powered" isn't a value proposition — "Save 10 hours/week on X" is.
