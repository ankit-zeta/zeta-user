Product Launch Checklist

Pre-launch validation, audience building, launch execution, post-launch iteration, and scaling checklists

Pre-Launch: Validate & Build Audience

  • Complete 20+ customer interviews
    Ask about their problems, current solutions, willingness to pay. Record and transcribe. Look for patterns.
  • Survey results: 40%+ say "yes, I'd pay"
    If less than 40%, rethink your idea or target audience. Surveys with 50+ responses minimum.
  • Landing page live with email capture
    One clear value proposition, one CTA, no navigation. Test headline variations. Target: 100+ signups.
  • Competitor analysis complete
    3-5 competitors analyzed: pricing, features, reviews, weaknesses. Your differentiation clear.
  • MVP defined (core features only)
    List 3-5 core features. Everything else is V2. Scope creep kills launches. Ship fast, iterate later.
  • Waitlist growing (500+ emails)
    Content marketing, social media, communities, partnerships. Every email is a potential launch day sale.
  • Beta testers recruited (10-20)
    From waitlist. Free access in exchange for feedback and testimonials. Test onboarding, fix bugs.

Pre-Launch: Setup & Assets

  • Payment processing configured
    Stripe/PayPal/Gumroad connected. Test purchase flow end-to-end. Refund policy documented.
  • Email sequences loaded
    Welcome sequence (5-7 emails), launch sequence (6 emails over 2 weeks), post-purchase onboarding.
  • Product page / sales page complete
    Benefits > Features. Social proof. FAQ. Guarantee. Clear CTA. Mobile-optimized. Load time < 3s.
  • Social media assets created
    Launch announcement graphics, demo screenshots/video, testimonial graphics, countdown posts.
  • PR / blogger outreach list ready
    30-50 contacts in your niche. Personalized pitches drafted. Press kit with screenshots, logo, bio.
  • Support system ready
    Help desk (or shared inbox), FAQ document, onboarding guide, common issues documented.

Launch: Execute & Promote

  • Launch day email sent at optimal time
    Tuesday-Thursday, 9-11 AM in your audience's timezone. Subject line A/B tested. Segmented lists.
  • Social media blitz (all platforms)
    Twitter thread, LinkedIn post, Instagram stories, Facebook groups. Pin launch post. Update bios.
  • Community sharing (tasteful, value-first)
    Reddit, Indie Hackers, Hacker News, relevant Slack/Discord groups. Don't spam — share your story.
  • Monitor sales and fix issues in real-time
    Have a team member on support. Check payment processing. Respond to every comment within 1 hour.
  • Share social proof throughout the day
    "100 sales in 4 hours!" "Join 500+ customers." Real numbers build momentum and FOMO.
  • Early bird deadline communicated
    Countdown in emails and social. "Price goes up in 48 hours." Create genuine urgency.

Post-Launch: Iterate & Scale

  • Collect feedback from first 50 customers
    Survey (Typeform), personal emails, support tickets. What do they love? What's confusing?
  • Request testimonials and reviews
    Ask after they've had success (not immediately). Offer incentive if needed. Social proof compounds.
  • Fix top 3 issues identified
    Bugs, confusion points, onboarding gaps. Ship fixes within 1 week. Communicate updates to users.
  • Set up evergreen funnel
    Webinar, email nurture, or content funnel for ongoing sales. Don't rely only on launch-day pushes.
  • Launch referral program
    Give $X, Get $X. Or: Give 1 month free, get 1 month free. Referred customers have 16% higher LTV.
  • Plan next marketing push
    Content marketing, SEO, paid ads, partnerships, affiliate program. Don't stop at launch day.

Pro Tip

The launch is not the finish line — it's the starting line. The real work begins post-launch. Your first 50 customers are your most valuable. Treat them like co-creators. Their feedback will shape your product into something people actually want to pay for.
