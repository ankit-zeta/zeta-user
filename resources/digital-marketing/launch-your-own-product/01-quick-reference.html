Pricing Strategies

Value-Based Pricing

Price based on customer perceived value
Research: "What would you pay for X?"
Best for: SaaS, courses, consulting
Formula: Value delivered × 10-20% = Price

Competitive Pricing

Price relative to competitors
Match, undercut (-10-20%), or premium (+20-50%)
Best for: Markets with clear alternatives
Requires: Differentiation to justify price

Cost-Plus Pricing

Cost to produce + desired margin
Formula: Cost × (1 + margin%) = Price
Best for: Physical products, commodities
Limit: Doesn't account for market value

Psychological Pricing

$47 instead of $50, $97 instead of $100
Bundle pricing: 3 for $99 (vs $47 each)
Anchor pricing: Show $200, sell for $97
Best for: Digital products, courses

Launch Day Checklist

Before Launch

✅ Landing page live and tested
✅ Payment processing working
✅ Email sequences loaded
✅ Social media posts scheduled
✅ Support inbox monitored

During Launch

✅ Send launch email at optimal time
✅ Post on all social channels
✅ Engage with every comment
✅ Share in communities (tastefully)
✅ Monitor sales and fix issues

After Launch (Day 1-7)

✅ Follow up with buyers (thank you)
✅ Request early reviews/testimonials
✅ Address any issues immediately
✅ Share social proof (sales numbers)
✅ Announce early bird deadline

Post-Launch (Week 2-4)

✅ Collect user feedback surveys
✅ Iterate on product based on feedback
✅ Optimize conversion funnel
✅ Plan next marketing push
✅ Set up referral program

Pro Tip

The #1 product launch mistake is building in isolation. Start with validation — talk to 20+ potential customers before building anything. A validated idea with a small audience beats a brilliant idea with no audience. Pre-sell before you build.
