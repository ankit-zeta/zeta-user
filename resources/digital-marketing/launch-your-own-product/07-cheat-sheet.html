Pricing Psychology

Charm Pricing

$47 instead of $50 (6% more conversions)
$97 instead of $100 (3-4% lift)
$197 instead of $200 (perceived savings)
Works best for: Under $100 price points
Use for: First-time buyers, impulse purchases

Prestige Pricing

$199 instead of $197 (signals quality)
$500 instead of $499 (premium positioning)
Works best for: Luxury, consulting, courses
Use for: Established brands, high-value offers
Rule: Round numbers = perceived quality

Anchor Pricing

Show $299 crossed out, sell for $97
"Normally $299 — launch price $97"
"Save $202 with early bird pricing"
Works for: Every product launch
Rule: Anchor must be believable

Bundle Pricing

"3 for $97" (vs $47 each = save $44)
"Get the bundle, save 30%"
Works for: Digital products, templates
Increases: Average order value 20-40%
Rule: Show individual prices for contrast

Urgency Tactics (Ethical)

Time-Limited Pricing

"Early bird ends Friday midnight"
"Price goes up in 48 hours"
"Launch discount: 48 hours only"
Rule: Must be REAL — fake urgency kills trust
Best: 48-72 hour early bird window

Quantity-Limited

"Only 50 spots at this price"
"First 100 customers get [bonus]"
"Limited to 200 beta users"
Rule: Limit must be genuine
Works for: Courses, coaching, SaaS

Bonus Stacking

"Launch buyers get: [Bonus 1] + [Bonus 2]"
"Order today and get [Bonus] free (worth $97)"
"Bonus expires when early bird ends"
Rule: Bonuses must be genuinely valuable
Stack 2-3 bonuses, not 10

Social Proof Urgency

"127 people already joined"
"Selling fast — only X left at this price"
"Join [number] happy customers"
Works for: Any product
Rule: Use real, verified numbers

Common Launch Mistakes

Building in Isolation

❌ Spending 6 months building before validating
✅ Talk to 20+ customers first
❌ "I'll launch when it's perfect"
✅ Ship MVP, iterate with feedback
❌ No audience before launch
✅ Build waitlist for 4-8 weeks minimum

Weak Launch Day Execution

❌ "I'll just post once on social"
✅ Multi-channel blitz: email + social + communities
❌ No launch email sequence
✅ 6-email sequence over 2 weeks
❌ Ignore comments and DMs
✅ Reply to EVERY interaction within 1 hour

Pricing Mistakes

❌ Pricing on cost (what it cost you to build)
✅ Price on value (what it's worth to customer)
❌ No early bird discount
✅ 30-50% off for first 48-72 hours
❌ Only one price tier
✅ 2-3 tiers: Basic, Pro, Premium

Post-Launch Failures

❌ "Launch day is the finish line"
✅ Launch is the starting line
❌ No follow-up with buyers
✅ Personal thank you + onboarding
❌ Ignoring feedback
✅ Top 3 fixes shipped within 1 week

Quick Formulas

Revenue Target

Revenue Goal = Price × Units Sold
Example: $47 × 200 = $9,400
Units Needed = Revenue Goal ÷ Price
Example: $10,000 ÷ $47 = 213 units

Waitlist Conversion

Expected Sales = Waitlist Size × Conversion Rate
Typical rate: 5-15%
Example: 500 × 10% = 50 sales
Target: 10%+ for validated products

Email Launch Results

Sales = List Size × Open Rate × CTR × Conversion Rate
Example: 1,000 × 40% × 10% × 15% = 6 sales
Optimize: Subject line (open) → Body (CTR) → Offer (conv)

Break-Even

Break-Even Units = Fixed Costs ÷ (Price - Variable Cost)
Example: $2,000 ÷ ($47 - $0) = 43 units
Digital products: Variable cost = $0 → Every sale is profit

Pro Tip

The best launch cheat code is simple: validate before building, build an audience before launching, and execute launch day like your business depends on it (because it does). Most people skip these three steps and wonder why their launch flopped.
