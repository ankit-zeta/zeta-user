Product Launch Glossary

Essential terminology for launching digital products, SaaS, courses, and physical products: MVP, validation, pre-sell, launch stack, and more

Validation & Pre-Launch Terms

MVP (Minimum Viable Product)
The simplest version of your product that solves the core problem. Built to validate demand before investing in full development. Should take 2-4 weeks, not 6 months.
Validation
The process of confirming people will pay for your product before building it. Methods: surveys, interviews, pre-sales, landing page tests, crowdfunding. Validation = reducing risk.
Pre-Sell
Selling your product before it's fully built. Accepts payment upfront to validate demand and fund development. Strongest form of validation — people vote with their wallet.
Waitlist
List of people who sign up to be notified when your product launches. Indicates interest but not commitment. Conversion rate from waitlist to sale is typically 5-15%.
Landing Page
Single-page website designed to capture leads or sales. One offer, one CTA, no navigation distractions. Essential for launch — test messaging before building product.
Customer Interview
One-on-one conversation with potential customers to understand their problems, workflows, and willingness to pay. Do 20+ interviews before building. Ask open-ended questions.

Launch Strategy Terms

Launch Stack
The collection of tools used for your product launch: email platform, payment processor, landing page builder, analytics, social media. Keep it simple — 4-5 tools max.
Early Bird
Discounted price offered to first buyers during launch. Creates urgency and rewards early adopters. Typical: 30-50% off regular price for first 48-72 hours or first 100 buyers.
Social Proof
Evidence that others are buying and enjoying your product. Includes: testimonials, reviews, sales numbers, media mentions, user counts. Critical for overcoming purchase hesitation.
Scarcity
Limited availability (real or perceived) that creates urgency. Examples: "Only 50 spots," "Price goes up Friday," "Limited edition." Must be genuine — fake scarcity destroys trust.
Product Hunt Launch
Submitting your product to Product Hunt for exposure to 100K+ tech/startup audience. Requires preparation: hunter, assets, launch day engagement. Can drive 1,000+ signups in 24 hours.
Launch Day
The single day you officially release your product. All marketing converges: emails, social, PR, communities. Plan every hour. Have a team member monitoring support and issues.

Post-Launch Terms

Iterate
Improving your product based on real user feedback. Release small updates frequently (weekly/biweekly). Don't wait for perfection — ship, learn, improve. The cycle never stops.
Retention Rate
Percentage of customers who continue using/paying for your product over time. Formula: (Customers at end - New) ÷ Customers at start × 100. Target: 80%+ monthly for SaaS.
NPS (Net Promoter Score)
Customer loyalty metric: "How likely are you to recommend us?" Scale 0-10. Promoters (9-10), Passives (7-8), Detractors (0-6). NPS = %Promoters - %Detractors. Target: 50+.
Churn Rate
Percentage of customers who stop using/paying over a period. Formula: Lost customers ÷ Starting customers × 100. Monthly SaaS benchmark: 3-7%. High churn = product or onboarding problem.
Referral Program
System that incentivizes existing customers to bring new ones. Typical: Give $20, Get $20. Best growth channel — referred customers have 16% higher lifetime value and lower churn.
Upsell / Cross-Sell
Upsell: Upgrade to higher tier/more features. Cross-sell: Buy related product. Best time: After customer achieves success with base product. Don't upsell too early — build trust first.

Pro Tip

Don't get stuck learning every term before launching. The essential trio is: MVP (build it simple), Validation (confirm people want it), and Launch Stack (4-5 tools to sell it). Everything else you learn as you go.
