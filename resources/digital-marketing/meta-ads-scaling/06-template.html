Campaign Structure Template

Standard Scaling Structure (E-commerce)

CAMPAIGN: CONV_[Geo]_[Product]_[Date] — CBO: $[Budget]/day
├── AD SET: LAL_1%_Purchasers_180d
│   ├── AD: VID_Hook1_SocialProof_V1
│   ├── AD: VID_Hook2_Benefit_V1
│   ├── AD: CAR_Steps_V1
│   └── AD: IMG_Offer_V1
├── AD SET: LAL_2-3%_Purchasers_180d
│   ├── AD: VID_Hook1_SocialProof_V1
│   ├── AD: VID_Hook3_UGC_V1
│   └── AD: CAR_BeforeAfter_V1
├── AD SET: Broad_NoInterests
│   ├── AD: VID_Hook2_Benefit_V1
│   ├── AD: VID_Hook3_UGC_V1
│   └── AD: IMG_Brand_V1
├── AD SET: Retarget_Web_Visitors_30d
│   ├── AD: VID_Testimonial_V1
│   ├── AD: CAR_Product_Features_V1
│   └── AD: IMG_Discount_V1
└── AD SET: Retarget_Cart_Abandoners_7d
    ├── AD: VID_Urgency_V1
    ├── AD: IMG_FastAction_V1
    └── AD: CAR_SocialProof_V1

Naming Convention: [Type]_[Audience]_[Timeframe] — clear, sortable, searchable.

Rules: 3-7 ad sets per campaign. 3-5 ads per ad set. CBO for budget distribution. Separate campaigns for each major audience type when scaling aggressively.

Ad Copy Templates

PAS Framework (Problem → Agitate → Solution)

Primary Text (≤125 chars visible):
[Problem]: Still struggling with [pain point]?
[Agitate]: It's costing you [time/money/frustration].
[Solution]: [Product] fixes this in [timeframe].
[Social Proof]: Join [X] customers who [result].

Headline (≤40 chars):
[Benefit] Without [Pain Point]

Description (≤30 chars):
[Secondary benefit] | [CTA]

Hook Templates (First 3 Seconds)

Problem Hook:
"Stop wasting [time/money] on [thing that doesn't work]"

Curiosity Hook:
"POV: You finally [desired outcome]"

Social Proof Hook:
"How [X] people [achieved result] in [timeframe]"

Myth-Busting Hook:
"[Common belief] is wrong. Here's why..."

Contrast Hook:
"Before: [bad state]. After: [good state]."

Urgency Hook:
"[X] hours left: [irresistible offer]"

Challenge Hook:
"Can you [simple action] in [timeframe]? Watch this..."

CTA Button Options by Intent

Low Intent (Awareness):    Learn More, See More, Discover
Medium Intent (Consideration): Sign Up, Get Offer, Download
High Intent (Conversion):  Shop Now, Buy Now, Book Now, Get Started
Retargeting:               Claim Offer, Complete Order, Finish Setup

Test Priority Matrix

HIGH IMPACT + EASY:
1. New hook on winning creative
2. CTA button change
3. Headline swap

HIGH IMPACT + HARD:
4. New creative concept (full production)
5. New landing page
6. New offer/bundle

LOW IMPACT + EASY:
7. Description text change
8. Color overlay on image
9. Music change on video

LOW IMPACT + HARD:
10. New video format (long-form)
11. New ad format (Collection)
12. New campaign structure

💡 Pro Tip

Save these templates as Notion pages or Google Docs. Duplicate for each campaign. Consistency in naming, tracking, and reporting is what separates chaotic advertisers from systematic scalers.
