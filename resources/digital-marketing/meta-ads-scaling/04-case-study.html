Case Study 1: E-Commerce Scaling

SkinCare Pro — From $100/Day to $5,000/Day in 90 Days

Industry: DTC Skincare | Product: $49 serum bundle | AOV: $67 | Margin: 65%

Starting Point: $100/day budget, 2.1 ROAS, $32 CPA, struggling to scale past $3K/month total spend.

Phase 1: Foundation (Days 1-14)

• Installed CAPI alongside Pixel (was Pixel-only)
• Event Match Quality improved: 6.2 → 9.1
• Created 5 Custom Audiences: Purchasers 30/60/90/180d, Cart Abandoners 7d
• Built Lookalike 1% from each Custom Audience
• Verified all standard events in Events Manager

Phase 2: Creative Overhaul (Days 15-30)

• Replaced 12 underperforming creatives with 8 new concepts
• Tested 5 hooks per concept using Dynamic Creative
• Winner: UGC "POV: Your skin after 2 weeks" (42% hook rate)
• Added carousel format showing before/after progression
• Budget: $100 → $150/day (20% increases every 4 days)

Phase 3: Audience Expansion (Days 31-60)

• Launched LAL 1% Purchasers campaign (separate from original)
• Added LAL 2-3% for broader reach
• Tested broad targeting (no interests) — ROAS 3.8x
• Expanded to Instagram Reels placement (+22% reach)
• Budget: $150 → $500/day via 20% increases

Phase 4: Aggressive Scaling (Days 61-90)

• Launched Advantage+ Shopping Campaign (ASC)
• ASC auto-optimized across all audiences
• Added 3 new UGC creator partnerships (whitelisted)
• Expanded geo: US → UK + Canada
• Budget: $500 → $5,000/day (ASC + original campaigns)

Key Strategies

  • CAPI First: Server-side tracking improved attribution accuracy by 40%, giving Meta better conversion data to optimize with.
  • UGC > Brand: UGC creatives outperformed polished brand content by 2.3x on ROAS and 1.8x on CTR.
  • ASC Breakthrough: Advantage+ Shopping Campaigns scaled 3x faster than manual campaigns with 15% better ROAS.
  • Geo Expansion: UK market had 30% lower CPM than US, providing efficient scale.

Case Study 2: SaaS Lead Generation

CloudMetrics — Scaling MQLs from 50/Week to 400/Week

Industry: B2B SaaS | Product: $99/mo analytics tool | ACV: $1,188 | Sales Cycle: 21 days

Starting Point: $2,000/month budget, $85 CPL, 50 MQLs/week, 12% MQL→SQL rate, inconsistent lead quality.

Monthly Spend
$38

400

Phase 1: Lead Quality Filter (Weeks 1-3)

• Added qualifying questions to lead forms (company size, role)
• Created "High Intent" Custom Event for leads answering qualifying questions
• Switched optimization from Lead → High Intent Lead
• CPL increased $85 → $95 but MQL→SQL rate: 12% → 28%
• Net effective CPL dropped from $708/MQL → $339/MQL

Phase 2: Creative Matrix (Weeks 4-6)

• Tested 12 ad concepts across 4 angles: Pain Point, Social Proof, Demo, Comparison
• Winner: "How [Competitor] users save 10hrs/week" (3.2% CTR)
• Added LinkedIn-style carousel (problem → solution → proof → CTA)
• Created video testimonials from 3 customers
• CPL dropped: $95 → $52

Phase 3: Audience Expansion (Weeks 7-10)

• Built Lookalike 1% from SQL Opps (not just leads)
• Added Lookalike from website visitors who viewed pricing page
• Tested: Job Title targeting vs Interest targeting
• Job Title (Marketing Directors, VPs) won with 40% lower CPL
• Launched retargeting campaign for free trial users

Phase 4: Full Scale (Weeks 11-16)

• 3 campaigns running: Prospecting (LAL 1%), Retargeting (Trial users), Re-engagement (Dormant trials)
• Budget split: 60% Prospecting / 25% Retargeting / 15% Re-engagement
• Automated lead scoring integration (HubSpot → Meta offline events)
• Budget: $2K → $12K/month, MQLs: 50 → 400/week

Key Strategies

  • Optimize for SQL, Not Lead: Higher-quality optimization event reduced wasted spend by 52% despite higher CPL.
  • LinkedIn-Style Creative: Professional carousels with data-driven headlines outperformed consumer-style ads by 2.8x.
  • Pricing Page Retargeting: Retargeting pricing page visitors had 8x higher conversion rate than cold traffic.
  • Offline Conversion Import: Importing CRM data (SQL, Opp, Won) back to Meta improved optimization significantly.

Case Study 3: Local Business

Premium Dental — 3 Locations, $50/Day to $400/Day

Industry: Dental Practice | Services: $150-2,000 | Locations: 3 cities, 50mi radius each | LTV: $3,500

Starting Point: $50/day (split across 3 locations), $65 per appointment booking, 40% show rate, no tracking.

Phase 1: Tracking Setup (Weeks 1-2)

• Installed Pixel on booking page + thank you page
• Connected CAPI via Google Tag Manager server-side
• Created Custom Conversions: "Booking Confirmed"
• Set up offline conversion import from practice management software
• Verified booking events flowing correctly

Phase 2: Campaign Restructure (Weeks 3-4)

• Split into 3 separate campaigns (one per location)
• Each campaign: 3 ad sets (Prospecting, Retargeting, Emergency)
• Location-specific landing pages with Google Maps embed
• Added call tracking (CallRail integration)
• Budget: $50/day → $100/day (2 locations performing well)

Phase 3: Creative & Offer (Weeks 5-8)

• Tested 6 ad concepts: Before/After, Patient Testimonial, Doctor Intro, Emergency, Special Offer, Office Tour
• Winner: Patient testimonial video (52% hook rate)
• Added "Book Free Consultation" lead magnet
• Emergency ad set: "Tooth pain? Same-day appointments" (highest CTR: 3.8%)
• Budget: $100 → $250/day

Phase 4: Scale & Automate (Weeks 9-16)

• Launched Advantage+ Campaign (3 locations combined)
• Auto-allocated budget to best-performing location
• Added Messenger chatbot for instant booking
• WhatsApp click-to-message for emergency patients
• Budget: $250 → $400/day, bookings: 60 → 180/month

Key Strategies

  • Location Segmentation: Separate campaigns per location prevented budget cannibalization and enabled geo-specific optimization.
  • Emergency Angle: Urgent pain-point creative had 3x higher CTR than general branding ads.
  • Messenger Booking: In-app booking reduced friction — 40% of bookings came through Messenger vs website.
  • Offline Import: Tracking show-up rate and treatment value enabled true ROAS calculation (8.2x on $3,500 LTV).

Case Study 4: Info Product Launch

GrowthAcademy — Course Launch from $0 to $120K in 14 Days

Industry: Online Education | Product: $497 course + $997 VIP upsell | Audience: Aspiring marketers, 25-45

Starting Point: 12K email list, no prior ad spend, 7-day launch window, $5K ad budget allocated.

Pre-Launch: List Building (Weeks 1-4)

• Created free "5-Day Marketing Challenge" lead magnet
• Ran lead generation ads: $2,500 budget
• CPL: $4.20 (12,000 leads → 5,800 challenge completers)
• Built Custom Audience: Challenge Completers
• Built Lookalike 1% from Challenge Completers
• Total list grew: 12K → 24K

Launch Phase 1: Open Cart (Days 1-3)

• Retargeting: Challenge Completers (Custom Audience)
• Ad: "The challenge is over. Here's what's next."
• Offer: $497 course + fast-action bonus (first 50 only)
• Budget: $800/day
• Result: 89 sales ($44K revenue, 5.5x ROAS)

Launch Phase 2: Social Proof (Days 4-7)

• Added testimonial ads from Day 1-3 buyers
• "47 people already enrolled — here's what they're saying"
• Retargeting: Website visitors who didn't purchase
• Retargeting: Video viewers (sales page, 50%+ watched)
• Budget: $1,200/day
• Result: 98 sales ($48.7K revenue, 7.1x ROAS)

Launch Phase 3: Urgency Close (Days 8-14)

• "Cart closes in 48 hours" countdown creative
• VIP upsell targeting: Previous course buyers + high engagers
• Lookalike 1% from Purchasers (scaled cold audience)
• Budget: $1,500/day
• Result: 54 course sales + 38 VIP upgrades ($27.3K revenue, 10.8x ROAS)

Key Strategies

  • List Building First: $2,500 in lead gen ads created a warm audience worth $120K in launch revenue — 48x return on list-building spend.
  • Three-Phase Launch: Open Cart → Social Proof → Urgency Close maximized revenue from each audience temperature.
  • Retargeting Heavy: 70% of revenue came from retargeting (warm audiences), only 30% from cold Lookalikes.
  • Fast-Action Bonus: Created urgency without discounting — protected price positioning while driving early sales.

Key Insight Across All Case Studies

Every successful scaling story shares three elements: (1) Robust tracking (Pixel + CAPI) as foundation, (2) Creative diversity as the growth engine, and (3) Horizontal expansion (new audiences, geos, campaigns) before aggressive vertical budget increases.
