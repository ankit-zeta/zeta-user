Prompt Building Framework

CRISPE Framework Practice

Task Type:
Content writing / Analysis / Code / Research
Capacity (Role):
e.g., "Senior copywriter with 10 years experience"
Insight (Context):
e.g., "B2B SaaS startup, Series A, targeting CTOs"
Statement (Goal):
e.g., "Write 5 email subject lines for product launch"
Personality (Tone):
e.g., "Professional but approachable, data-driven"
Experiment (Variations):
e.g., "Give me 3 versions: curious, urgent, value-focused"
