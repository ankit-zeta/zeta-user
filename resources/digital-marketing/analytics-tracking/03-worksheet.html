Governance Rules

  • All lowercase, underscores only (no spaces, caps, special chars)
  • Document in shared "UTM Master Sheet" (Google Sheets)
  • Use URL Builder tool for every link (ga-dev-tools.web.app/campaign-url-builder)
  • Auto-tagging ON for Google Ads (gclid)
  • Quarterly audit: filter GA4 by utm_source → check consistency
