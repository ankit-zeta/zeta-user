Social Media Marketing Glossary

Essential terminology for organic and paid social media across all major platforms

Core Metrics & Analytics

Reach
Number of unique accounts that saw your content at least once
Impressions
Total number of times content was displayed (includes multiple views by same user)
Engagement Rate
(Total engagements ÷ Reach) × 100; engagements = likes, comments, shares, saves, clicks
Click-Through Rate (CTR)
(Link clicks ÷ Impressions) × 100; measures how compelling your CTA is
CPM
Cost Per Mille (thousand impressions); standard pricing model for brand awareness ads
CPC
Cost Per Click; pricing model where you pay only when someone clicks your ad
ROAS
Return on Ad Spend; revenue generated ÷ ad spend; target typically 3:1 or higher
Video Retention
Average watch time as percentage of total video length; key algorithm signal

Content Formats

Carousel
Multi-slide post (swipeable); 2-10 images/videos; high engagement format on IG/LI
Reel
Short-form vertical video (9:16) up to 90s on Instagram; algorithm favorite for reach
Story
Ephemeral 24-hour content; full-screen vertical; interactive stickers (polls, Q&A, links)
Thread
Connected series of posts (Twitter/X, LinkedIn); tells longer story in chunks
UGC
User-Generated Content; content created by customers/fans featuring your brand
Lead Magnet
Free resource (ebook, template, checklist) offered in exchange for contact info
Content Pillar
Core theme/topic category that structures your content strategy (e.g., Educate, Entertain)
Evergreen Content
Content that remains relevant long-term; not tied to trends or news cycles

Platform-Specific Terms

Algorithm
Platform's ranking system deciding which content to show; based on engagement, relevance, recency
Shadowban
Content silently hidden from non-followers; usually from spammy behavior or guideline violations
Explore Page
Instagram's discovery feed showing content from accounts you don't follow based on interests
For You Page (FYP)
TikTok's main discovery feed; highly personalized algorithmic content stream
LinkedIn Pulse
LinkedIn's long-form publishing platform for articles; boosts professional authority
Twitter Spaces
Live audio conversations on Twitter/X; hosts + speakers + listeners
Duet/Stitch
TikTok features: Duet = side-by-side video; Stitch = clip + your reaction
Highlight
Saved Story collections on Instagram profile; permanent showcase of best content

Paid & Growth Terms

Boost Post
Simplified ad creation from existing organic post; limited targeting vs full Ads Manager
Lookalike Audience
New audience similar to your existing customers/visitors; created from source audience
Retargeting
Showing ads to people who previously interacted (visited site, watched video, engaged)
A/B Testing
Comparing two variants (creative, copy, audience) to determine better performer
Frequency
Average times each person sees your ad; >3 risks ad fatigue and wasted spend
Creative Fatigue
Performance decline from audience seeing same creative too often; refresh every 2-4 weeks
Influencer Marketing
Partnering with creators to promote to their audience; nano/micro/macro/mega tiers
Social Listening
Monitoring brand mentions, keywords, competitors across platforms for insights

Strategy & Planning

Content Calendar
Scheduled plan of what to post, when, on which platforms; includes copy, assets, links
Batch Creation
Producing multiple content pieces in one session; improves consistency and efficiency
Content Repurposing
Adapting one piece of content for multiple platforms/formats (video → clips → carousel → thread)
Social SEO
Optimizing profiles and content for platform search (keywords in bio, captions, hashtags)
Community Management
Active engagement with audience: replies, DMs, comment moderation, conversation starters
Brand Voice
Consistent personality/tone across all communications; defined by attributes (witty, authoritative, warm)
Crisis Management
Plan for handling negative viral situations; response templates, escalation paths, approval chain
Employee Advocacy
Encouraging employees to share brand content; amplifies reach through trusted networks
