Comment Response Templates

Positive Feedback

"Thank you! 🙌 So glad [specific point] resonated."

"Appreciate you! What was your biggest takeaway?"

"Love hearing that! Tag someone who needs this."

Questions (Know Answer)

"Great question! [Concise answer]. More details in my [guide/post/video] — link in bio."

"Short answer: [answer]. Long answer: I made a whole post about this — check my profile!"

Questions (Don't Know)

"Honestly, I'd need to research that. But [related insight I do know]. Let me look into it and follow up!"

"Good catch — I don't have data on that specific case. My experience suggests [educated guess], but verify independently."

Disagreement/Criticism

"Fair point! I see it differently because [reason], but I respect that perspective. What's your experience been?"

"Thanks for sharing that view. Can you elaborate on [specific aspect]? I'm genuinely curious."

Spam/Trolls

Delete + block. Don't engage. Report if abusive.

If borderline: "This doesn't add to the conversation. Let's keep it constructive."

Feature Requests

"Love this idea! Added to our roadmap. Follow for updates — I'll tag you when it ships 🚀"

"Great suggestion! We're actually exploring [related feature]. What would make it most useful for you?"

DM Sales Templates

Cold Outreach (Value-First)

Hi [Name], Loved your recent post about [specific topic] — especially your point about [detail]. I help [target audience] achieve [result] through [method]. Thought you might find this [resource/case study] useful: [link] No pitch — just genuinely thought it could help. Keep crushing it! — [Your Name]

Warm Follow-Up (Post-Engagement)

Hey [Name]! Thanks for engaging with my [post about X]. Curious — what's your current approach to [related challenge]? I've been helping [similar people] solve this with [method], and [specific result] is typical. If you're open to it, I'd love to share a quick loom/video showing how it works. No pressure either way. — [Your Name]

Discovery Call Booking

[Name], Based on what you've shared about [challenge], I think I can help. I have a 15-min discovery call where I: 1. Map your current situation 2. Identify the #1 bottleneck 3. Outline a 90-day roadmap No pitch — just clarity. Worth 15 mins? If yes, pick a time: [Calendly link] — [Your Name]

Post-Call Follow-Up

Hi [Name], Great conversation earlier! Quick recap: Your situation: [1-sentence summary] Main bottleneck: [identified] Recommended path: [your solution] Investment: [price/terms] Next step: [specific action + deadline] Attached: [proposal/case study/relevant resource] Let me know questions. I'll follow up [day] if I don't hear back. — [Your Name]

Comment Strategies for Growth

Strategic Commenting

• Comment on 10 target accounts daily (prospects, peers, influencers)

• Add genuine value — not "Great post!"

• Share insight, ask follow-up, or respectfully disagree

• Your profile gets clicks from their audience

Comment Seeding

• On your own posts: End with specific question

• "What's your #1 challenge with [topic]?"

• "Which would you choose: A or B?"

• Reply to EVERY comment within 1 hour

Controversial Takes (Carefully)

• "Unpopular opinion: [take with evidence]"

• Invites debate → comments → reach

• Only on topics you truly believe + can defend

• Stay professional; attack ideas, not people

Community Building

• Feature followers: "Shoutout to @user for [win]"

• Run weekly thread: "Win Wednesday — share your win"

• Ask for input: "Building [thing] — what should I include?"

• Create inside jokes/recurring formats

Influencer / UGC Partnership

We'd love to send you [product] to try. No obligation to post. If you genuinely like it, we'd be thrilled if you shared your honest thoughts. We also have an affiliate program ([commission]% + exclusive code for your audience). Interested? Reply and I'll get it shipped. — [Your Name]

Podcast / Interview Request

Hi [Name], [Specific compliment about their work]. I host [Podcast Name] — [description, audience size, past guests]. Your expertise in [topic] would be incredible for our episode on [theme]. Format: 30-45 min, conversational. We handle editing/promotion. You get raw files + clips for your channels. Recent episodes: [2-3 relevant links] Interested? Pick a time: [Calendly link] — [Your Name]

💡 Pro Tip

Personalization > volume. 5 personalized DMs beat 50 templates. Reference specific content they created. Show you actually know them.
