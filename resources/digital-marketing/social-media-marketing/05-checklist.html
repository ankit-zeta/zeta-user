Social Media Audit Checklist

Complete profile optimization, content quality review, engagement analysis, and competitor benchmarking

Profile Optimization

  • Username/Handle consistency
    Same or recognizable handle across all platforms
  • Profile photo/logo
    High-res, on-brand, recognizable at small sizes (400×400px min)
  • Cover/banner image
    On-brand, includes value prop or CTA, correct dimensions per platform
  • Bio/Description optimized
    Clear value prop, keywords, CTA, link, branded hashtag, location
  • Website link in bio
    Trackable (UTM), mobile-optimized landing page, link-in-bio tool if multiple links
  • Contact info complete
    Email, phone, address (if local), business hours
  • Business category correct
    Accurate classification for algorithm and discovery
  • Verified/Badge status
    Applied for verification if eligible; Meta Verified, Twitter Blue, LinkedIn verification
  • Highlights/Featured sections
    Instagram Highlights, LinkedIn Featured, Twitter Pinned Tweet, YouTube Playlists

Content Quality Review

  • Visual consistency
    Cohesive color palette, fonts, filters, templates across last 20 posts
  • Content pillar balance
    Last 30 posts reflect intended pillar distribution (Educate/Entertain/Inspire/Promote/Connect)
  • Format diversity
    Using all relevant formats (carousel, reel, story, image, video, live)
  • Caption quality
    Hook in first line, value-driven, clear CTA, proper formatting, no typos
  • Hashtag strategy
    Relevant, mixed volume, branded, rotating sets, not spammy
  • Alt text on images
    Descriptive, keyword-rich for accessibility and SEO
  • Video captions/subtitles
    Burned-in or platform-native; 85% watch without sound
  • Brand voice consistency
    Tone, vocabulary, personality consistent across all posts and replies
  • CTA variety
    Not every post sells; mix of engage, learn, share, buy, sign up

Engagement Patterns

  • Reply rate & speed
    Responding to >90% comments within 2 hours; DMs within 4 hours
  • Community engagement
    Liking/commenting on follower posts, UGC reshares, conversation starters
  • Question usage
    Regular polls, Q&A, "what do you think" prompts in captions/stories
  • UGC encouragement
    Branded hashtag, contests, features, easy submission process
  • Negative feedback handling
    Professional, timely, public acknowledgment + private resolution
  • Engagement rate trends
    30-day rolling average stable or growing; investigate drops

Competitor Analysis

  • Top 5 competitors identified
    Direct (same offer, same audience) + indirect (same audience, different offer)
  • Content gap analysis
    Topics they cover that you don't; formats they use that you don't
  • Frequency benchmarking
    Their posting frequency vs yours; quality vs quantity tradeoffs
  • Engagement benchmarking
    Their avg engagement rate vs yours; what drives their spikes
  • Growth rate comparison
    Monthly follower growth % comparison over 6 months
  • Paid ad library review
    Meta Ad Library, LinkedIn Ads, TikTok Creative Center — active creatives, offers, hooks
  • Influencer partnerships
    Who they work with, frequency, content style, estimated costs

Platform-Specific Checks

  • Instagram: Highlights organized, Reels cover images, Shop enabled, Guides created, Collabs used
  • LinkedIn: Company page complete, Showcase pages, Employee notifications on, Newsletter active
  • Twitter/X: Lists created, Pinned tweet current, Spaces hosted, Communities joined
  • TikTok: Bio link active, Playlists organized, Commercial Music Library used, Creator Marketplace
  • YouTube: Channel trailer, Playlists optimized, Shorts shelf, Community tab, End screens/cards
  • Facebook: Group connected, Reviews enabled, Shop/Appointments, Events created, Messenger greeting

Analytics & Measurement

  • Native analytics connected
    Instagram Insights, LinkedIn Analytics, Twitter Analytics, TikTok Analytics, YouTube Studio
  • UTM tracking on all links
    Consistent naming convention; tracked in GA4
  • Conversion tracking
    Pixel/CAPI installed; events firing (lead, purchase, signup)
  • Monthly reporting template
    KPIs, top posts, audience insights, competitor comparison, recommendations
  • Quarterly deep-dive
    Audience demographics, content audit, strategy adjustment, budget reallocation

💡 Pro Tip

Run this audit quarterly. Score each section 1-5. Focus improvement efforts on lowest-scoring areas first. Track score progression over time.
