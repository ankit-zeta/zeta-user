Content Pillar Framework

Educate (30%)

How-to guides, tips, tutorials, industry insights, myth-busting, FAQs. Positions you as authority.

Entertain (20%)

Behind-the-scenes, humor, trends, challenges, day-in-the-life, bloopers. Humanizes brand.

Inspire (20%)

Success stories, transformations, quotes, user spotlights, milestones, vision/mission content.

Promote (15%)

Product launches, offers, testimonials, case studies, webinars, lead magnets. Direct conversion focus.

Connect (15%)

Polls, questions, AMAs, community highlights, UGC features, comments engagement. Builds relationships.

Hashtag Strategy

Types of Hashtags

Branded: #YourBrand, #YourCampaign
Niche: #DigitalMarketing, #SEOTips (10K-500K posts)
Community: #MarketingTwitter, #BuildInPublic
Trending: #MondayMotivation, #SmallBusiness
Location: #NYCBusiness, #LondonMarketing

Optimal Counts

Instagram: 8-15 (mix of sizes)
LinkedIn: 3-5 (niche + branded)
Twitter/X: 1-2 (highly relevant)
TikTok: 3-5 (niche + trending)
YouTube: 3-5 (in description)
Facebook: 1-2 (minimal impact)

Research Process

1. List 20 niche hashtags
2. Check post volume (avoid >1M)
3. Analyze top posts for content ideas
4. Create 3 hashtag sets (rotate)
5. Track performance monthly

Pro Tips

• Hide hashtags in first comment (IG)
• Use line breaks before hashtags
• Create branded hashtag for campaigns
• Monitor competitor hashtags
• Avoid banned/spammy tags
