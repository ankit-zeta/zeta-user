Google Ads Glossary

Essential terminology for scaling Google Ads campaigns across Search, Shopping, Display, YouTube, and Performance Max

Core Cost Metrics

CPC (Cost Per Click)
Amount paid when someone clicks your ad. Formula: Total Spend ÷ Total Clicks. Lower CPC with same conversion rate = more traffic per dollar. Key lever for scaling efficiency.
CPM (Cost Per Mille)
Cost per 1,000 impressions. Formula: (Spend ÷ Impressions) × 1000. Used for Display, YouTube, and awareness campaigns. Rising CPM signals audience saturation.
CPA (Cost Per Acquisition)
Cost to acquire one conversion. Formula: Total Spend ÷ Conversions. Must be below customer LTV × margin for profitability. The primary scaling metric.
CPA vs Target CPA
Actual CPA is what you pay; Target CPA is what you bid. When Target CPA is set, Google optimizes bids to average around that target. Lower Target CPA = stricter filtering.
tCPA (Target CPA)
Automated bidding strategy where Google sets bids to achieve conversions at your target cost. Requires 30+ conversions/month. Best for scaling with predictable costs.
tROAS (Target ROAS)
Automated bidding strategy targeting a specific return on ad spend percentage. Example: 400% means $4 revenue per $1 spent. Requires value tracking and 50+ conversions/month.

Performance Metrics

ROAS (Return on Ad Spend)
Revenue per dollar spent. Formula: Revenue ÷ Ad Spend × 100 (as percentage). Example: $400 revenue ÷ $100 spend = 400% ROAS. Break-even ROAS = 1 ÷ profit margin.
CTR (Click-Through Rate)
Percentage of people who clicked your ad. Formula: Clicks ÷ Impressions × 100. Search benchmark: 3-5%. Display benchmark: 0.5%. Higher CTR improves Quality Score.
Conversion Rate (CVR)
Percentage of clicks that result in a conversion. Formula: Conversions ÷ Clicks × 100. Search benchmark: 3-5%. Higher CVR = lower CPA = more scale headroom.
Impression Share (IS)
Percentage of available impressions your ads received. Formula: Your Impressions ÷ Total Eligible Impressions. < 50% = room to grow. 80%+ = near saturation.
Quality Score (1-10)
Google's rating of ad quality based on expected CTR, ad relevance, and landing page experience. Higher Quality Score = lower CPC + better ad position. 7+ is target for scaling.
Ad Rank
Formula: Bid × Quality Score × Expected CTR × Ad Relevance × Landing Page Experience. Determines ad position and whether ad shows. Higher Ad Rank = better position at lower cost.

Keyword Terms

Search Terms Report
Shows actual queries triggered your ads. Review weekly. Add high-performers as keywords, add irrelevant terms as negatives. Critical for scaling efficiency.
Negative Keywords
Keywords that prevent your ads from showing. Add irrelevant or low-intent queries. Reduces wasted spend, improves CTR and CPA. Essential for scaling without waste.
Keyword Match Types
Controls how closely a query must match your keyword: Exact [kw], Phrase "kw", Broad kw. Exact = most control, Broad = most volume. Scale with Broad + Smart Bidding.
Search Query vs Keyword
Keyword = what you bid on. Search Query = what the user actually typed. Gap between them determines efficiency. Use match types and negatives to close the gap.
Keyword Planner
Google's free tool for keyword research. Shows search volume, competition, CPC estimates, and forecasts. Essential for identifying scaling opportunities.
Search Volume
Average monthly searches for a keyword. Scale by targeting keywords with higher volume. Beware: high volume often means high competition and CPC.

Audience & Targeting Terms

Similar Audiences (Lookalikes)
Google's expansion of your first-party data to find new users with similar behaviors. Build from converters, high-value customers, or website visitors. Core scaling tool.
In-Market Audience
Users actively researching or comparing products in a category. Google tracks browsing behavior to identify purchase intent. High-converting for scaling.
Custom Intent Audience
Audience built from specific keywords, URLs, and apps your ideal customer uses. Target competitor brand names, review sites, and industry content URLs.
RLSA (Remarketing Lists for Search Ads)
Target past website visitors when they search on Google. Adjust bids for returning users. Higher intent = higher conversion rate = lower CPA at scale.
Customer Match
Upload your customer email list to Google. Find these users across Search, YouTube, Gmail, and Display. Use for remarketing, exclusion, and Similar audience creation.
Audience Segments
Groups of users defined by demographics, interests, behaviors, or first-party data. Layer with keywords for precision targeting at scale.

Campaign Structure Terms

Ad Group
Container within a campaign holding keywords, ads, and targeting. Structure: Campaign → Ad Group → Keywords/Ads. Group tightly themed keywords for ad relevance.
Responsive Search Ads (RSA)
Ads with up to 15 headlines and 4 descriptions. Google tests combinations and learns winners. Provide diverse assets for AI optimization at scale.
Performance Max (PMax)
Full-funnel campaign type using all Google channels (Search, Display, YouTube, Gmail, Discover, Maps) with AI optimization. Highest scaling potential for e-commerce.
Smart Shopping → PMax
Google migrated Smart Shopping to Performance Max. PMax adds YouTube, Discover, and Gmail placements. Requires product feed optimization for scaling.
Campaign Budget
Daily spending limit per campaign. Controls pacing. Increase ≤ 20% every 3-4 days to avoid learning reset. Budget allocation determines scaling trajectory.
Ad Rotation
How Google shows multiple ads in an ad group. "Optimize" (default) favors best performers. "Do not optimize" rotates evenly for testing. Use "Optimize" for scaling.

Tracking & Measurement Terms

Conversion Tracking
Google Ads tag on your website that tracks when clicks become leads or sales. Essential for Smart Bidding. Without it, automated strategies cannot optimize.
Google Tag Manager (GTM)
Tag management system for deploying conversion tracking, analytics, and remarketing tags without code changes. Simplifies scaling infrastructure setup.
Attribution Model
How credit is assigned to touchpoints in the conversion path. Default: Data-Driven attribution. Last Click ignores upper funnel. Choose based on scaling goals.
Data-Driven Attribution
Google's ML-based attribution that distributes credit based on actual impact. More accurate than rule-based models. Use for scaling decisions.
Cross-Device Tracking
Tracks users across devices (mobile → desktop). Google connects signed-in users. Improves conversion data accuracy for scaling across device types.
Conversion Value Rules
Assign different values to conversions based on conditions (location, device, audience). Helps Google optimize for highest-value conversions when scaling.

Pro Tip

Bookmark Google's official Ads Help Center and Skillshop. They're the definitive sources when platform changes affect your scaling strategy. Google updates terminology and features frequently — stay current.
