Agency Glossary

Retainer, project-based, value-based, SOP, KPI, client onboarding, scope creep, and account management terms

Pricing & Revenue Terms

Retainer
Monthly recurring fee for ongoing services. Client pays $X/month for defined scope. Best for: stable revenue, long-term relationships. Typical: $1K-10K/month.
Project-Based Pricing
Fixed price for a defined project with clear deliverables and timeline. Protects you from scope creep if scope is well-defined. Typical: $2K-50K per project.
Value-Based Pricing
Price based on the value/results you deliver to the client, not your time. Example: Charge $10K for a campaign that generates $100K revenue. Highest revenue model.
Productized Service
Fixed scope, fixed price, repeatable service. "SEO audit for $997" or "Facebook ads management for $2,500/mo." Scalable because it's standardized.
Revenue Per Employee
Total revenue ÷ number of employees. Agency benchmark: $150K-250K per employee. Below $100K = unprofitable. Above $300K = highly efficient.
Utilization Rate
Percentage of time spent on billable vs non-billable work. Formula: Billable Hours ÷ Total Hours x 100. Target: 70-80%. Below 60% = profitability problem.

Operations Terms

SOP (Standard Operating Procedure)
Documented step-by-step process for recurring tasks. Enables delegation, consistency, and scaling. Without SOPs, you can't hire effectively. Create one for every repeated task.
KPI (Key Performance Indicator)
Measurable metric that tracks success. Examples: Revenue, profit margin, client retention, employee utilization. Without KPIs, you're guessing. Track weekly.
Scope Creep
Gradual expansion of project scope without corresponding price increase. "Can you also do X?" adds up fast. Prevent with: clear SOW, change order process, boundary-setting.
SOW (Statement of Work)
Document defining project scope, deliverables, timeline, and price. Protects both parties. Without SOW, you're exposed to scope creep and disputes. Always have one.
Client Onboarding
Process of bringing a new client into your agency. Includes: contract, kickoff call, access setup, expectations alignment. First 30 days set the tone for the entire relationship.
Account Management
Ongoing relationship management with clients. Includes: regular check-ins, reporting, expectation management, upselling. Good account management = high retention rate.

Growth Terms

Client Retention Rate
Percentage of clients who stay over a period. Formula: (Clients at end - New) / Clients at start x 100. Agency benchmark: 80-90% annually. Below 70% = delivery or relationship problem.
Average Client Lifetime
How long clients stay on average. Formula: 1 / Monthly Churn Rate. Typical: 12-24 months. Longer lifetime = higher LTV = more investment in acquisition justified.
Net Promoter Score (NPS)
Client loyalty metric: "How likely are you to refer us?" Score 0-10. Target: 50+. High NPS = referral engine. Low NPS = churn risk. Survey quarterly.
Capacity Planning
Forecasting when you'll need to hire based on current workload and pipeline. Formula: Available hours / Hours per client = Max clients. Plan 2-3 months ahead.

Pro Tip

The most important agency term is "SOP." Without standard operating procedures, you can't delegate, you can't scale, and you're stuck doing everything yourself. Document every process from day one — your future self (and future team) will thank you.
