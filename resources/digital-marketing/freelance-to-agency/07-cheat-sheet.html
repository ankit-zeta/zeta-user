Agency Systems Cheat Sheet

Pricing formulas, scope creep prevention, hiring, and financial management

Pricing Formulas

Minimum Hourly Rate
(Monthly Income Goal + Monthly Costs + Profit Margin) ÷ Billable Hours = Minimum Rate
Target Retainer Price
Monthly Hours Needed × Minimum Hourly Rate × 1.2 (buffer) = Retainer Price
Value-Based Price
Client's Revenue Increase × Your % (10-25%) = Value-Based Price
Project Price
Estimated Hours × Hourly Rate × 1.3 (scope buffer) = Project Price
Break-Even Utilization
Fixed Costs ÷ (Revenue Per Hour × Available Hours) = Minimum Utilization %

Scope Creep Prevention

Trigger PhraseResponse
"Can you also do...?""Happy to! Let me put together a change order with pricing for that."
"This should be included""The SOW covers [X]. What you're asking is outside scope. Here's the pricing."
"Quick favor...""I can do that! It'll be [X hours] at [rate]. Want me to proceed?"
"We need it faster""I can expedite. Rush fee is 50% premium. Want me to prioritize?"
"Just a small change""Small changes add up. Let me track and we can address in next month's scope."
"Other agencies include...""Our pricing is based on defined scope. Happy to add that to the next phase."

Hiring Checklist

Before You Hire

Revenue supports salary for 6 months
You're at capacity (turning away work)
SOPs exist for the role
You can define the role clearly
You have a trial project ready

Hiring Process

Job post on 3 platforms
Skills assessment (paid trial)
Interview (2 rounds max)
Reference check (1 client)
2-week paid trial before full offer

First 30 Days

Day 1-7: Shadow you on all work
Day 8-14: Do work, you review
Day 15-21: Handle 1 client (test)
Day 22-30: Full workload + review
End of month: Performance review

Red Flags

Can't follow SOPs exactly
Needs constant supervision
Misses deadlines in trial
Doesn't ask questions
Poor communication

Financial Management

Profit Margin Target
(Revenue - All Costs) ÷ Revenue ≥ 40% for healthy agency

Revenue Benchmarks

Solo: $80-150K/yr
2-person: $200-300K/yr
Micro (3-5): $500K-1M/yr
Small (6-10): $1-2M/yr
Per employee: $150-250K

Cost Structure

Labor: 40-50% of revenue
Tools/software: 5-8%
Marketing: 5-10%
Overhead: 5-10%
Profit: 25-40%

Cash Flow Rules

Collect 50% upfront always
Net 15 or Net 30 payment terms
Invoice immediately on delivery
Follow up at Day 1, 7, 14
Charge late fees (1.5%/mo)

Emergency Fund

Target: 3-6 months expenses
Calculate: Monthly costs × 6
Keep in separate account
Never touch for growth investments
Rebuild after any drawdown

Pro Tip

The #1 agency killer is scope creep. Every "quick favor" erodes your margin. Track scope creep monthly — if it's happening repeatedly, your SOW is too vague or your pricing is too low. Both are fixable, but only if you measure it.
