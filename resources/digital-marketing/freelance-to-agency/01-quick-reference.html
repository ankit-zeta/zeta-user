Client Acquisition Channels

Cold Outreach

Email, LinkedIn DMs, Twitter
Target: Companies with clear need
Personalize every message
Follow up 3-5 times
Conversion: 1-3% response, 0.5-1% close

Referrals

Ask every happy client
Offer referral incentive (10-20%)
Make it easy (email template)
Best clients refer best clients
Conversion: 30-50% close rate

Content Marketing

Case studies, tutorials, thought leadership
Show expertise, not pitch
SEO for long-term traffic
Social media for visibility
Builds trust before the pitch

Freelance Platforms

Upwork, Fiverr, Toptal
Good for starting, not for scaling
Build portfolio and reviews
Transition to direct clients
Conversion: 5-15% proposals

Pro Tip

The biggest jump is from solo freelancer to freelancer + contractor. That's where you stop trading time for money. Delegate delivery first, then sales, then operations. Never hire before you have enough revenue to support the salary for 6 months.
