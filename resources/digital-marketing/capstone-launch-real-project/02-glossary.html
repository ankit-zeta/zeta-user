Capstone Project Glossary

MVP, product-market fit, launch funnel, conversion rate, CAC, LTV, iteration, pivot, and scaling terms

Product & Validation Terms

MVP (Minimum Viable Product)
The simplest version of your product that solves one core problem. NOT a prototype — it's real enough to sell. Goal: validate demand before building everything. Example: 3-lesson course before full curriculum.
Product-Market Fit
When your product satisfies strong market demand. Signals: customers come back, word-of-mouth grows, sales happen without heavy marketing. Marc Andreessen: "being in a good market with a product that can satisfy that market."
Validation
Testing whether your idea has paying customers BEFORE building the full product. Methods: landing page tests, pre-sales, interviews, surveys. Target: 10+ pre-sales or 5%+ landing page conversion.
Pivot
Changing direction based on market feedback. Not failure — it's smart business. Example: Started as SEO course, audience wants Facebook ads course instead. Pivot fast, don't cling to original idea.
Iteration
Improving your product based on real user feedback. Small, continuous changes. NOT waiting months for a perfect version. Launch → learn → improve → repeat. Speed of iteration wins.
Value Proposition
Clear statement of what your product does, who it's for, and why it's better. Formula: "I help [audience] achieve [outcome] through [method] without [pain point]."

Marketing & Funnel Terms

Launch Funnel
The path customers take from discovery to purchase. Typical: Traffic → Landing Page → Email Opt-in → Nurture Sequence → Sales Page → Checkout → Thank You Page. Optimize each step.
Conversion Rate
Percentage of visitors who take desired action. Formula: Conversions ÷ Visitors × 100. Landing page benchmark: 2-5%. Sales page benchmark: 1-3%. Below 1% = fix your offer or targeting.
CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost)
How much you spend to acquire one customer. Formula: Total Marketing Spend ÷ Customers Acquired. Must be less than LTV. If CAC > LTV, you're losing money on every sale.
LTV (Lifetime Value)
Total revenue a customer generates over their relationship with you. Formula: Average Order Value × Purchase Frequency × Customer Lifespan. Higher LTV = more you can spend on acquisition.
Email List
Your most valuable asset. People who opted in to hear from you. Unlike social followers, you OWN this list. Email converts 3-5x better than social media. Build it from day one.
Pre-Launch
Phase before your product goes live. Build anticipation: waitlist, early access, founding member pricing. Collect emails, gather testimonials, create buzz. 2-4 weeks minimum.

Growth & Scaling Terms

Organic Growth
Growth without paid advertising. SEO, content marketing, social media, referrals. Slower but sustainable and cheaper. Foundation for paid growth. Build organic first, amplify with ads.
Paid Growth
Growth through advertising. Google Ads, Facebook Ads, sponsored content. Faster but costs money. Only works if CAC < LTV. Requires budget and testing. Best after product-market fit.
Viral Coefficient
How many new users each existing user brings. Formula: Invites Sent × Conversion Rate. Above 1.0 = viral growth (each user brings more than 1 new user). Rare but powerful.
Churn Rate
Percentage of customers who stop buying. Formula: Customers Lost ÷ Total Customers × 100. For subscriptions: monthly churn. Target: under 5% monthly. High churn = product problem.
Social Proof
Evidence that others use and value your product. Testimonials, reviews, case studies, user counts, media mentions. Reduces purchase anxiety. Add to every page of your funnel.
Evergreen
Content or product that stays relevant over time. Evergreen courses sell for years. Evergreen content ranks in SEO long-term. Build evergreen assets, not just trendy content.

Pro Tip

The capstone glossary terms are all connected: MVP leads to validation, validation leads to product-market fit, product-market fit enables scaling, scaling requires managing CAC and LTV. Master the connections, not just the definitions.
