2. Email Launch Sequence

5-DAY LAUNCH SEQUENCE

DAY 1 — STORY + VALUE ───────────────────────── Subject: The mistake that cost me $10,000 Body: Share a personal story about the problem your product solves. Tease the solution. No hard sell. Build connection. CTA: "Reply with your biggest challenge" DAY 2 — PROBLEM + AGITATION ───────────────────────── Subject: Why [audience] are struggling Body: Deep dive into the problem. Use data, examples, case studies. Agitate the pain. Make them feel the cost of inaction. CTA: "Tomorrow I'll share the solution" DAY 3 — SOLUTION + LAUNCH ───────────────────────── Subject: It's here 🎉 Body: Introduce your product. Full details, features, benefits. Include social proof. Launch special pricing (24-48 hours). CTA: "Get [Product] at [Price]" DAY 4 — SOCIAL PROOF ───────────────────────── Subject: What [X] people are saying Body: Share testimonials, results, screenshots. Address common objections. FAQ section. CTA: "Join them — [link]" DAY 5 — URGENCY + CLOSE ───────────────────────────────── Subject: Last chance (price goes up tonight) Body: Remind of deadline. Recap benefits. Final social proof. Scarcity: "Only [X] spots at this price" CTA: "Get access before midnight"

3. Validation Survey

10-QUESTION VALIDATION SURVEY

Send to 10 people in your target audience. 1. What's your biggest challenge with [topic]? (Open-ended) 2. How are you currently solving this? (Open-ended) 3. What have you tried that didn't work? (Open-ended) 4. How much time/money have you spent on this? (Multiple choice: $0-100, $100-500, $500-1000, $1000+) 5. If there was a solution that [your value prop], would you be interested? (Scale 1-5: Not at all to Very interested) 6. What would make you buy this today? (Open-ended) 7. What's your biggest concern about a product like this? (Open-ended) 8. How would you prefer to access this? (Course/ebook/tool/service) (Multiple choice) 9. What's your budget for solving this problem? (Multiple choice: $0-50, $50-200, $200-500, $500+) 10. Would you be willing to be a beta tester? (Yes/No + email if yes) SCORING: ───────── • 7+ people say "Very interested" = VALIDATED • 5-6 people = Need to refine idea • Under 5 = Pivot or abandon

Pro Tip

Use these templates as starting points, not rigid scripts. Customize the language for your audience, your voice, and your product. The structure is proven — the words should be yours. Start with the template, then make it feel natural.
