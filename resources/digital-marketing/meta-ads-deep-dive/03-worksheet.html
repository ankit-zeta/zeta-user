Step 7: Launch Day Checklist

Final verification before hitting publish

  • All ad sets have 3-5 ad variations
  • Creatives meet specs (no text overlay issues, correct dimensions)
  • Copy proofread (no typos, compliant claims, clear CTA)
  • Landing page loads fast (<3s), mobile-optimized, congruent with ad
  • Pixel/CAPI firing on landing page and thank you page
  • UTM parameters appended to all destination URLs
  • Budget and schedule correct
  • Exclusion audiences applied (customers, employees)
  • Automated rules set for cost guards
  • Slack/email notifications for spend/CPA alerts

💡 Pro Tip

Fill this worksheet BEFORE opening Ads Manager. It prevents "blank canvas paralysis" and ensures strategic alignment. Duplicate for each new campaign.
