A/B Testing & Experimentation Tools

  • Meta Experiments (Lift Tests)
    Native A/B testing: Holdout, Geo, Creative, Audience. Statistical significance built-in. Free for eligible accounts.
    Meta Experiments →
    OfficialStatisticalFree
  • Varos
    Benchmarking platform. Compare your CPA, CPM, CTR, ROAS against anonymized peers in your vertical. Free tier available.
    varos.io →
    BenchmarksPeer Data
  • Northbeam / Triple Whale
    Multi-touch attribution for e-commerce. Better than Meta's reporting for true ROAS. From $500/mo.
    northbeam.io →
    AttributionE-commerce
  • Google Optimize / VWO
    Landing page A/B testing. Test headlines, layouts, offers. Improves CVR → better Meta ROAS. Free tiers available.
    optimize.google.com →
