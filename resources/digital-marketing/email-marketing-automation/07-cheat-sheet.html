Email Marketing Cheat Sheet

Subject line formulas, send time tips, deliverability checklist, and key email marketing metrics

Subject Line Formulas

Curiosity Gap

"The one thing most [audience] get wrong"
"I was wrong about [topic]"
"What nobody tells you about [topic]"
"This changed everything for me"
Rule: Must deliver on the curiosity

Benefit-Driven

"How to [achieve result] in [timeframe]"
"[Result] without [pain point]"
"The [adjective] way to [achieve goal]"
"[Number] ways to [improve metric]"
Rule: Be specific, not vague

Urgency & Scarcity

"Last chance: [offer] expires tonight"
"Only [X] spots left"
"[Discount]% off — 24 hours only"
"Your [item] expires in [time]"
Rule: Urgency must be real

Personalization

"{{first_name}}, this is for you"
"Your [topic] plan is ready"
"A recommendation for you, {{first_name}}"
"Based on your interest in [topic]"
Rule: Use merge tags, not fake personalization

Social Proof

"[Number] people already joined"
"Why [notable person] uses [product]"
"The [topic] strategy that drove [result]"
"[X] stars from [number] reviews"
Rule: Use real, verifiable numbers

Question Format

"Are you making this [topic] mistake?"
"What would you do with [result]?"
"Ready to [achieve goal]?"
"Still struggling with [pain point]?"
Rule: Ask questions you can answer

Send Time Optimization

B2B Best Times

Tuesday-Thursday: 9-11 AM
Wednesday 10 AM: Highest open rates
Avoid: Monday morning, Friday afternoon
Segment by: Timezone
Test: Your audience may differ

B2C Best Times

Saturday morning: 9-10 AM
Weekday evenings: 7-9 PM
Lunch time: 12-1 PM
Segment by: Timezone + behavior
Test: Mobile vs desktop open times

Newsletter Timing

Weekly: Same day, same time (consistency)
Daily: Morning (6-8 AM) for news
Biweekly: Mid-week performs best
Key: Consistency builds habit
Audience learns when to expect you

Launch Timing

Early bird open: Tuesday 9 AM
Last chance: Thursday 8 PM
Cart close: Day of deadline, morning
Follow-up: Next morning 9 AM
Avoid: Weekends for high-ticket

Deliverability Checklist

Authentication (Do Once)

SPF record added to DNS
DKIM keys generated and added
DMARC policy set (start with "none")
Custom sending domain configured
Verify: mail-tester.com (score 9/10+)

Ongoing Hygiene

Remove hard bounces immediately
Clean list every 30 days
Re-engage or suppress inactive (90 days)
Never buy email lists
Double opt-in for new subscribers

Content Best Practices

Subject: 30-50 characters
Preheader: 40-100 characters
Text-to-image ratio: 80:20
One clear CTA per email
Mobile-first design

Volume Management

Warm new IPs: start with engaged
Consistent volume (don't spike)
Segment sends by engagement
Monitor sender score (above 90)
Check inbox placement monthly

Key Metrics Quick Reference

Open Rate Benchmarks

B2B: 20-25%
B2C: 15-25%
Newsletter: 25-40%
Welcome series: 50%+
Below 15%: Subject line problem

CTR Benchmarks

Overall: 2-5%
Newsletter: 3-7%
Promotional: 1-3%
Automation: 5-15%
Below 1%: Content or CTA problem

Revenue Formula

Email Revenue = List Size x Open Rate x CTR x Conversion Rate x AOV
Example: 10,000 x 25% x 5% x 3% x $50 = $187.50
Goal: $0.05-0.50 revenue per email sent

List Health Metrics

Bounce rate: < 2%
Unsubscribe rate: < 0.5%
Spam complaint: < 0.1%
Open rate trend: Stable or growing
Growth rate: 3-5% monthly

Pro Tip

Email marketing ROI is $36 for every $1 spent. Focus on: (1) Deliverability first, (2) Subject lines for opens, (3) Content for clicks, (4) Offers for revenue. Most people skip deliverability and wonder why nobody opens their emails.
