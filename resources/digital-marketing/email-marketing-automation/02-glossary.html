Email Marketing Glossary

Open rate, CTR, bounce, unsubscribe, segmentation, automation, drip, welcome series, and deliverability terms

Core Email Metrics

Open Rate
Percentage of delivered emails that were opened. Formula: Unique Opens ÷ Delivered × 100. Benchmark: 20-30%. Measures subject line effectiveness.
Click-Through Rate (CTR)
Percentage of recipients who clicked a link. Formula: Unique Clicks ÷ Delivered × 100. Benchmark: 2-5%. Measures content and CTA effectiveness.
Click-to-Open Rate (CTOR)
Percentage of openers who clicked. Formula: Unique Clicks ÷ Unique Opens × 100. Benchmark: 10-20%. Better measure of email content quality than CTR.
Bounce Rate
Percentage of emails that couldn't be delivered. Hard bounce: invalid address. Soft bounce: temporary issue. Target: < 2%. Clean bounces immediately.
Unsubscribe Rate
Percentage who opted out. Formula: Unsubscribes ÷ Delivered × 100. Benchmark: < 0.5%. Above 1% = content or frequency problem.
Spam Complaint Rate
Percentage who marked as spam. Target: < 0.1%. Above 0.1% = serious deliverability risk. Monitor in ESP dashboard.

Automation Terms

Drip Campaign
Pre-written email sequence sent on a schedule. Triggered by user action (signup, purchase) or time delay. Automated, consistent, scalable.
Welcome Series
Automated sequence for new subscribers. Sets expectations, delivers value, introduces brand. 5-7 emails over 10-14 days. Highest open rates of any sequence.
Automation Trigger
The event that starts an automated sequence. Examples: form submission, purchase, cart abandonment, link click, tag added, date-based.
Segmentation
Dividing your list into targeted groups based on behavior, demographics, or engagement. Segmented emails get 14% higher open rates and 100% more clicks than non-segmented.
Tag
Label applied to subscribers based on behavior or attributes. Used for segmentation and triggering automations. Example: "purchased-course," "visited-pricing."
Merge Tag
Dynamic placeholder that inserts subscriber data. Example: {{first_name}} becomes "John." Used for personalization. Increases open rates 10-20%.

Deliverability Terms

Deliverability
Percentage of emails that reach the inbox (not spam). Formula: Inbox Deliveries ÷ Total Sent × 100. Target: 95%+. Affected by sender reputation, content, authentication.
Sender Reputation
Score assigned by ISPs based on sending behavior. Factors: bounce rate, complaint rate, engagement, volume consistency. Good reputation = inbox placement.
SPF (Sender Policy Framework)
DNS record listing authorized sending servers. Prevents spoofing. Required for deliverability. Add to DNS: v=spf1 include:_spf.google.com ~all
DKIM (DomainKeys Identified Mail)
Cryptographic signature verifying email authenticity. Proves email wasn't modified in transit. Required by Gmail, Yahoo. Set up in ESP settings.
DMARC
Policy for handling failed SPF/DKIM checks. Options: none (monitor), quarantine (spam), reject (block). Start with "none" then move to "quarantine."
Warm-up
Gradually increasing email volume to build sender reputation. Start with engaged subscribers, increase daily. Takes 2-4 weeks. Required for new domains/IPs.

Pro Tip

Focus on these three terms first: Open Rate (subject lines), CTR (content quality), and Deliverability (inbox placement). Everything else builds on these three. A 30% open rate and 5% CTR with good deliverability is a profitable email program.
