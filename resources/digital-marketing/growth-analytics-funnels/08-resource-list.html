Pro Tip

Start with GA4 (free) + Hotjar or Clarity (free) + Looker Studio (free). This covers 80% of growth analytics needs. Add Mixpanel or Amplitude when you need product-level event tracking. Don't pay for tools until you've maxed out the free tiers.
