Growth Analytics Cheat Sheet

Key formulas, tool comparison, metric benchmarks, and common analytics pitfalls

Key Formulas

CAC

Total Marketing Spend ÷ New Customers
Example: $10,000 ÷ 200 = $50 CAC
Target: < 33% of LTV

LTV

Avg Monthly Revenue × Avg Lifespan (months)
Example: $50 × 24 months = $1,200 LTV
Or: ARPU ÷ Churn Rate

LTV:CAC Ratio

LTV ÷ CAC
Example: $1,200 ÷ $50 = 24x
Target: 3:1 or higher

Churn Rate

Lost Customers ÷ Starting Customers × 100
Example: 50 ÷ 500 = 10% monthly churn
SaaS benchmark: 3-7%

Conversion Rate

Conversions ÷ Total Visitors × 100
Example: 50 ÷ 2,000 = 2.5%
Landing page benchmark: 2-5%

Payback Period

CAC ÷ Monthly Revenue per Customer
Example: $50 ÷ $50 = 1 month
Target: < 12 months

Viral Coefficient (K)

Avg Invitations × Conversion Rate
Example: 3 invites × 20% = 0.6
K > 1.0 = viral growth

NPS

% Promoters (9-10) - % Detractors (0-6)
Example: 60% - 15% = 45 NPS
Target: 50+

Tool Comparison

GA4 (Google Analytics)

Best for: Web analytics, traffic, events
Price: Free
Strength: Integrations, Google ecosystem
Weakness: Steep learning curve, sampling
Start here: Yes

Mixpanel

Best for: Product analytics, funnels, cohorts
Price: Free up to 20M events/mo
Strength: Event-based, powerful funnels
Weakness: Complex setup, event limits
Start here: For SaaS/products

Amplitude

Best for: Product analytics, behavioral analysis
Price: Free up to 10M events/mo
Strength: Cohorts, behavioral targeting
Weakness: Enterprise features paid
Start here: For growth-stage products

PostHog

Best for: All-in-one (analytics, flags, recordings)
Price: Free up to 1M events/mo
Strength: Open source, feature flags, sessions
Weakness: Self-hosting requires devops
Start here: For developer-led teams

Hotjar

Best for: Heatmaps, session recordings, surveys
Price: Free up to 35 sessions/day
Strength: Visual insights, easy setup
Weakness: Not for quantitative analysis
Start here: For UX/conversion optimization

Microsoft Clarity

Best for: Heatmaps, session recordings (free)
Price: Free (unlimited)
Strength: Completely free, no limits
Weakness: Basic analytics, no funnels
Start here: Free Hotjar alternative

Common Analytics Pitfalls

Vanity Metrics Trap

Page views, followers, email subscribers feel good but don't pay bills. Focus on: Revenue, CAC, LTV, conversion rate. Vanity metrics are leading indicators only when tied to revenue.

Analysis Paralysis

Tracking 50 metrics = tracking none. Pick 3-5 that directly impact revenue. Review weekly. Everything else is noise. Action beats perfection.

Ignoring Segmentation

Aggregate data hides problems. Segment by: channel, device, geography, cohort. Your overall CVR might be 3% but organic might be 6% and paid 1%. Averages lie.

Not Enough Data

Making decisions on 100 visitors is guessing. Minimum for reliable tests: 1,000 per variation. For trends: 1,000+ events. For cohorts: 100+ per cohort. Be patient.

Pro Tip

The cheat sheet formula that matters most: LTV:CAC ratio. If it's below 3:1, you're spending too much to acquire customers. Fix your funnel before scaling ads. A great product with a broken funnel will always lose to a good product with a great funnel.
