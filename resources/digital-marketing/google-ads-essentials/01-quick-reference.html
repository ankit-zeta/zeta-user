Keyword Match Types (Search)

Broad MatchkeywordRelated searches, synonyms, variationsLow (Max reach)
Phrase Match"keyword"Searches including meaning of keywordMedium
Exact Match[keyword]Same meaning/intent (incl. close variants)High (Max control)
Negative Match-keywordExcludes searches containing termEssential for all

Broad Match Best Practices

• Pair with Smart Bidding (Target CPA/ROAS)
• Use broad match negatives aggressively
• Monitor search terms weekly
• Best for: Discovery, new accounts, Performance Max signals

Phrase Match Best Practices

• Default for most Search campaigns
• Balance of reach + control
• "red running shoes" matches "buy red running shoes"
• Add negatives for irrelevant modifiers

Exact Match Reality

• Now includes close variants (plurals, reordering, same intent)
• Not truly "exact" anymore
• Use for: Brand terms, high-value exact queries
• Still tightest control available

Negative Keyword Strategy

• Campaign-level: Universal negatives (free, cheap, jobs, diy, tutorial)
• Ad Group-level: Specific exclusions
• Lists: Shared across campaigns
• Review Search Terms Report weekly

Quality Score Factors

Expected CTR (Most Weight)

Likelihood of click based on keyword-ad relevance. Improve: tighter ad groups, compelling copy, relevant keywords.

Ad Relevance

How well ad matches keyword intent. Improve: keyword in headline, specific ad groups, match ad to search intent.

Landing Page Experience

Relevance, transparency, navigation, load speed. Improve: keyword on page, clear offer, fast mobile, no popups.

Quality Score Scale

1-10 (10 best). 7+ = good. Each point ≈ 16% CPC discount/penalty. Check at keyword level.

Campaign Structure Best Practices

Account Structure

Campaign → Ad Group → Keywords/Ads
Campaign = Budget, Location, Language, Network
Ad Group = Theme (15-20 keywords max)
SKAGs (Single Keyword Ad Groups) = outdated

Naming Convention

Campaign: [Type]_[Geo]_[Product]_[MatchType]_[Date]
Ad Group: [Theme]_[MatchType]
Example: SRCH_US_Shoes_Broad_Jan24 / Running_Phrase

Ad Group Themes

By product category: "Running Shoes"
By intent: "Buy Running Shoes", "Best Running Shoes"
By match type: "Broad", "Phrase", "Exact" (legacy)
Modern: Mixed match types in one AG

RSA Best Practices

Responsive Search Ads (default)
15 headlines (min 3), 4 descriptions (min 2)
Pin headline 1 for brand/offers
Use ad strength indicator (aim Excellent)

Audience Targeting (Search & Display)

In-Market

Actively researching/buying: "Motor Vehicles", "Real Estate", "Software", "Business Services"

Affinity

Long-term interests: "Health & Fitness Buffs", "Tech Enthusiasts", "Luxury Shoppers"

Custom Segments

Build from: Keywords, URLs, Apps, Places. "People who searched for X" or "Visited competitor sites"

Your Data Segments

Remarketing: Website visitors, YouTube viewers, App users, Customer Match (email upload), Similar segments

Demographics

Age, Gender, Parental Status, Household Income (US/JP/CA/AU), Homeownership

Combined Segments

AND/OR/NOT logic: (In-Market: Cars) AND (Affinity: Luxury) NOT (Customer Match: Buyers)
