Winning Ad Elements

  • Headline 2: "Licensed & Insured • 60-Min Response"
  • Description: "Burst pipes? No hot water? AC out? We're there in 60 mins. Upfront pricing. Call now!"
  • Extensions: Call (tracking #), Location (GBP), Sitelinks (Emergency, Water Heater, AC, Reviews), Callout (Licensed, Insured, Upfront Pricing, 24/7)

Key Optimizations

  • Week 1: Added call tracking → discovered 70% leads from calls, not forms
  • Week 2: Switched to Target CPA $50 → CPL dropped from $68 to $45
  • Week 4: Added "Emergency" ad customizer by hour → 22% higher CTR 6pm-6am
  • Month 2: PMax + Local Campaign captured 30% of leads Search missed

Key Insight

For local services: Call extensions + Call Tracking + Location Extensions = 3x more leads than form-only. Emergency intent = high urgency = call now behavior. Bid aggressively on "near me" + emergency terms.

Search - Competitor

Keywords: Competitor brand + "alternative", "pricing", "vs"

Search - Problem-Based

Keywords: "how to manage remote team", "track team tasks", "project timeline tool"
Match: Broad (Smart Bidding)

Display/Video - Retargeting

Audiences: Trial signups (no upgrade), Pricing page visitors, Blog readers
Budget: 20% ($3K)

YouTube - Demand Gen

Demo video + founder story
Custom segment: SaaS review sites visitors
Budget: 10% ($1.5K)

Winning Elements

  • RSA Headlines: "Asana Alternative — 50% Less", "Built for Remote Teams", "Free 14-Day Trial — No CC"
  • Landing Page: Interactive demo (no gate) → "Start Trial" above fold → Social proof (logos, testimonials)
  • Offline Conversion Import: CRM → Google Ads (GCLID) → Optimize for "Paid Customer" not "Trial"

Key Optimizations

  • Week 2: Added offline conversion import → Algorithm optimized for paid customers, not trials
  • Month 1: Competitor campaign CPA 40% lower than generic → Shifted 20% budget there
  • Month 2: Video retargeting "Still deciding?" → 15% trial-to-paid lift

Key Insight

B2B SaaS: Offline conversion import (CRM → Google Ads) is the unlock. Optimize for PAID CUSTOMER, not trial signup. Competitor conquesting + problem-based keywords = highest intent. Demo video retargeting closes the loop.

💡 Pro Tip

All three cases share: Proper tracking (Enhanced Conversions + CAPI + Offline Import), Smart Bidding (Target CPA/ROAS), Multi-campaign structure (Search + PMax + Retargeting), and weekly optimization cadence.
