Google Ads Glossary

Essential terminology for Search, Display, Shopping, Video, and Performance Max campaigns

Core Metrics

CPC
Cost Per Click. Amount paid for each click. Average CPC = Total Cost ÷ Clicks.
CPM
Cost Per Mille (thousand impressions). Used for Display/Video awareness campaigns.
CTR
Click-Through Rate. Clicks ÷ Impressions × 100. Search benchmark: 2-5%; Display: 0.5-1%.
CPA / CPL
Cost Per Acquisition / Cost Per Lead. Cost ÷ Conversions. Primary efficiency metric.
ROAS
Return on Ad Spend. Conversion Value ÷ Cost. Expressed as ratio (4.0 = 4:1) or %. E-commerce primary KPI.
Conversion Rate
Conversions ÷ Clicks × 100. Post-click performance. Search avg: 3-5%; Display: 0.5-1%.
Impression Share
Impressions received ÷ Impressions eligible for. Lost IS (Budget) vs Lost IS (Rank).
Quality Score
1-10 rating per keyword: Expected CTR, Ad Relevance, Landing Page Experience. Higher = lower CPC.

Campaign & Structure Terms

Campaign
Top level: budget, location, language, networks, bidding strategy. Contains ad groups.
Ad Group
Container for keywords and ads sharing theme. 15-20 keywords max. Shared bids (legacy) or ad-level.
Keyword
Word/phrase triggering ads. Match types: Broad, Phrase, Exact, Negative.
Search Query / Search Term
Actual user query that triggered ad. Review in Search Terms Report. Add as keyword or negative.
RSA (Responsive Search Ad)
Default ad format. 15 headlines, 4 descriptions. Google mixes/matches. Replaces Expanded Text Ads.
Ad Strength
RSA quality indicator: Poor → Average → Good → Excellent. Based on uniqueness, relevance, quantity.
Asset
Individual component: headline, description, image, video, sitelink. Used in RSA, PMax, Demand Gen.

Bidding & Budget Terms

Smart Bidding
ML-powered automated bidding: Target CPA, Target ROAS, Maximize Conversions, Maximize Value.
Portfolio Bid Strategy
Shared bid strategy across multiple campaigns. Centralized management, shared learning.
Learning Period
~7 days after launch/major change. Performance unstable. Avoid edits. "Learning" label in UI.
Daily Budget
Average daily spend. Google may spend 2x daily (averages over month). Not a hard cap.
Shared Budget
Single budget across multiple campaigns. Google allocates to best performers.
Bid Adjustment
% increase/decrease for device, location, ad schedule, audience. -90% to +900%.
Enhanced CPC (eCPC)
Manual CPC + automatic bid adjustments for likely conversions. Semi-automated.

Tracking & Measurement

Conversion
Valuable action: purchase, lead, sign-up, call, store visit. Defined in Conversions settings.
Conversion Action
Specific conversion type with settings: category, value, count (one/every), window, attribution.
Conversion Window
Time after click to count conversion: 1-90 days (Search), 1-30 days (Display/Video). Default 30d.
Attribution Model
How credit assigned: Data-Driven (default), Last Click, First Click, Linear, Time Decay, Position-Based.
Google Tag (gtag.js)
Unified tag for Google Ads, GA4, Floodlight. Replaces separate conversion tags.
Enhanced Conversions
Hashes first-party data (email, phone, name) for better matching. Required for accurate measurement.
Offline Conversion Import
Upload offline conversions (CRM, POS) via GCLID. Critical for B2B, long sales cycles.

Campaign Types & Features

Performance Max (PMax)
Goal-based campaign across ALL Google inventory. Asset groups + audience signals. Automated everything.
Demand Gen
Visual-first campaign: YouTube Shorts/In-stream, Discover, Gmail. Social-style creative. Replaces Discovery.
Shopping / Merchant Center
Product feed (XML/CSV/API) → Shopping ads. Requires GTIN/MPN, images, prices, availability.
Local Campaigns
Drive store visits. Links to Google Business Profile. Optimizes for "Get Directions" / calls.
App Campaigns
Automated across Search, Play, Display, YouTube. Optimize for installs or in-app actions.
Video Action Campaigns
YouTube drives conversions (not just views). CTA button, headline, sitelinks. Replaced by Demand Gen.
RLSA (Remarketing Lists for Search Ads)
Bid adjustments for past visitors on Search. "Bid +50% for cart abandoners searching brand terms."
Dynamic Search Ads (DSA)
Auto-generates headlines/landing pages from website content. Good for large inventories, gap filling.
