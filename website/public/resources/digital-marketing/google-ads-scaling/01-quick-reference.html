Keyword Match Types

Exact Match [keyword]

Triggers on the exact phrase and close variations
Most control, lowest volume, highest intent
Use for: proven converters, tight budget control
Example: [buy running shoes online]

Phrase Match "keyword"

Triggers when the phrase is included (in order)
Broader than exact, tighter than broad
Use for: scaling with moderate control
Example: "running shoes" → buy running shoes online

Broad Match keyword

Triggers on related terms, synonyms, and intent
Highest volume, lowest control, most learning
Use for: aggressive scaling with Smart Bidding
Example: running shoes → best sneakers for jogging

Match Type Strategy

Start: Exact + Phrase for control
Scale: Add Broad + Smart Bidding (Target CPA/ROAS)
Monitor: Search Terms report weekly
Clean: Add negatives for irrelevant queries

Scaling Frameworks

Vertical Scaling

Increase budget on winning campaigns
Rule: +20% budget every 3-4 days
Monitor CPA for 48h after each increase
Stop if CPA rises > 15% above target

Horizontal Scaling

Expand to new keywords, audiences, campaigns
Lower risk than vertical scaling
Builds diversified campaign portfolio
Test 2-3 new variables per week

Geographic Scaling

Expand to new regions, cities, countries
Start: Top cities → Regional → National
Localize ad copy and landing pages
Separate campaigns per geo for budget control

Channel Expansion

Add new campaign types (Search → Shopping → PMax)
Each channel captures different intent levels
Performance Max as full-funnel amplifier
YouTube for awareness feeding Search demand

Scaling Decision Matrix

Scale Aggressively

✅ ROAS > target for 14+ days
✅ CPA < target for 14+ days
✅ Quality Score 7+ on key terms
✅ 100+ conversions per month
✅ Conversion tracking fully verified

Scale Cautiously

⚠️ ROAS near target (within 10%)
⚠️ CPA near target (within 15%)
⚠️ Quality Score 5-7
⚠️ 30-100 conversions per month
⚠️ Limited creative/keyword pipeline

Optimize First

❌ ROAS below target for 7+ days
❌ CPA above target for 7+ days
❌ Quality Score < 5
❌ < 30 conversions per month
❌ Tracking issues or data gaps

Pause & Rebuild

🛑 ROAS < breakeven for 14+ days
🛑 CPA > 2x target for 14+ days
🛑 Account suspension or policy issues
🛑 Landing page broken or slow
🛑 Attribution completely broken

Pro Tip

Google Ads rewards quality. Before scaling budget, improve Quality Score by refining ad copy, landing page relevance, and expected CTR. A 1-point Quality Score improvement can reduce CPC by 10-15%, stretching your scaling budget further.
