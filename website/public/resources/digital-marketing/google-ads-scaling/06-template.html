RSA Ad Copy Templates for Scaling

Scaling-Optimized RSA Structure

HEADLINES (15 — for maximum AI optimization): 1. [Core Benefit] + [Product] (PIN Position 1) 2. Free [Shipping/Trial/Consultation] 3. [Number]+ [5-Star Reviews/Customers] 4. [Key Feature] — [Advantage] 5. [Price] Starting at $[Amount] 6. {KeyWord:Default Headline} (DKI) 7. [Social Proof] — [Authority Signal] 8. New: [Feature/Product] Just Launched 9. [Guarantee] — [Risk Reversal] 10. [CTA] — [Benefit] 11. [Competitor] Alternative — Better [Feature] 12. Trusted by [Notable Customers] 13. [Award/Certification] Winner [Year] 14. [Limited Time] — [Offer] 15. [Brand] — [Tagline] DESCRIPTIONS (4): 1. [Benefit] + [Offer] + [CTA]. [Social Proof]. [Guarantee]. 2. [Feature] delivers [Result]. [Number]+ customers. [CTA]. 3. [Competitor] alternative with [Advantage]. Free [Trial/Demo]. [CTA]. 4. [Social Proof]. [Award]. [Guarantee]. [Urgency]. [CTA]. Best Practices for Scaling RSAs: - Provide 15 diverse headlines (not 15 variations of same message) - Include at least 3 headline types: Benefit, Social Proof, CTA - Use DKI in 2-3 headlines for keyword coverage - Pin H1 only if brand/offer consistency is critical - Test pinning strategy: Pin H1 vs. No pins (2 weeks each)

Keyword Expansion List Template

Scaling Keyword Categories

HIGH INTENT (Bottom Funnel — Scale First): ├── [Product] + buy/purchase/order/price ├── [Product] + near me / online / delivery ├── [Product] + discount/coupon/deal ├── Best [Product] for [Use Case] ├── [Product] vs [Competitor] └── [Brand] + pricing/plans MID INTENT (Middle Funnel — Scale Second): ├── Best [Product Category] 2026 ├── Top [Product] for [Need] ├── [Product] reviews/comparison ├── How to choose [Product] ├── [Product] features/benefits └── [Category] buying guide LOW INTENT (Top Funnel — Scale Carefully): ├── How to [solve problem] ├── What is [solution] ├── [Problem] causes/solutions ├── [Industry] trends ├── [Topic] guide/tutorial └── [Question] explained COMPETITOR (Conquest — Scale with Exact Match): ├── [Competitor] alternative ├── [Competitor] vs [Brand] ├── [Competitor] pricing ├── [Competitor] reviews ├── Switch from [Competitor] └── [Competitor] competitor
