Google Ads Scaling Cheat Sheet

Bidding strategies, match types, ad specs, Quality Score factors, scaling rules, and quick-reference formulas

Bidding Strategy Quick Reference

Target CPA

Best for: Lead gen, predictable costs
Requires: 30+ conv/month
Scaling: Raise budget 20% every 3-4 days
Risk: May limit volume if target too tight
Adjustment: Raise target 10% to increase volume

Target ROAS

Best for: E-commerce with revenue tracking
Requires: 50+ conv/month, value tracking
Scaling: Lower ROAS target 10% to increase volume
Risk: May overspend on low-value conversions
Adjustment: Use Conversion Value Rules for margin tiers

Maximize Conversions

Best for: Testing, under 30 conv/month
Requires: Conversion tracking active
Scaling: Just increase budget
Risk: No cost control, CPA may fluctuate
Switch to: Target CPA once 30+ conv achieved

Maximize Conversion Value

Best for: E-commerce without ROAS history
Requires: Value tracking on conversions
Scaling: Increase budget, optimize product feed
Risk: May chase high-AOV, low-margin products
Switch to: Target ROAS once 50+ conv achieved

Manual CPC

Best for: Full control, small budgets
Requires: None
Scaling: Adjust bids per keyword manually
Risk: Time-intensive, no AI optimization
Transition to: Enhanced CPC → Smart Bidding

Maximize Clicks

Best for: Top-of-funnel, traffic testing
Requires: None
Scaling: Set CPC cap, increase budget
Risk: No conversion optimization
Switch to: Target CPA after collecting conversion data

Match Type Scaling Rules

Exact Match [keyword]

Control: Highest
Volume: Lowest
Intent: Highest
Use for: Proven converters, tight CPA control
Scale by: Adding close variations, expanding to similar terms

Phrase Match "keyword"

Control: Medium
Volume: Medium
Intent: Medium-High
Use for: Balanced scaling with moderate control
Scale by: Adding related phrases, monitoring Search Terms weekly

Broad Match keyword

Control: Lowest
Volume: Highest
Intent: Variable
Use for: Aggressive scaling WITH Smart Bidding only
Rule: Never use Broad without Target CPA/ROAS
Scale by: Adding negatives aggressively weekly

Match Type Progression

Start: Exact + Phrase (control)
Scale: Add Broad + Smart Bidding
Monitor: Search Terms report weekly
Clean: Add negatives for irrelevant queries
Goal: 70% Exact, 20% Phrase, 10% Broad

Ad Specs & Limits

RSA (Responsive Search Ad)

Headlines: 15 max, 30 chars each
Descriptions: 4 max, 90 chars each
Pinned positions: 1, 2, or 3
Display URL paths: 2 (15 chars each)
Ad Strength: Aim for "Good" or "Excellent"

Shopping Ads

Title: 150 chars (optimize for 70)
Description: 5,000 chars (use 150)
Image: 1:1 ratio, white background
Price: Must be accurate, match LP
Brand: Required field in feed

Performance Max

Headlines: 5 (30 chars each)
Descriptions: 5 (90 chars each)
Images: 3-20 (1:1, 1.91:1, 4:5)
Videos: 0-5 (5s, 15s, 30s, 60s)
Logos: 1-5 (1:1, 4:1)

YouTube Ads

Skip after: 5 seconds (TrueView)
Recommended: 15-60 seconds
CTA: 10 chars overlay
Headline: 15 chars overlay
Landing page: Must match ad content

Quality Score Quick Factors

Expected CTR (35% of QS)

Target: Above average for all keywords
Boost: Ad extensions, specific ad copy
Hurt: Generic ads, no extensions
Fix: Tighten ad groups, add DKI
Check: Keywords → Quality Score tab

Ad Relevance (35% of QS)

Target: Above average
Boost: Keyword in H1, intent match
Hurt: Generic ads across ad groups
Fix: Single-theme ad groups (STAGs)
Check: Ad Relevance column in KW view

Landing Page Experience (30% of QS)

Target: Above average
Boost: Speed (< 3s), relevance, mobile-first
Hurt: Slow load, no keyword, poor UX
Fix: LP optimization, message match
Check: Landing Page Experienc column

Quality Score Impact

QS 10: Pay ~50% less per click
QS 7: Pay ~20% less per click
QS 5: Pay baseline CPC
QS 3: Pay ~67% more per click
QS 1: Pay ~150% more (or won't show)

Scaling Rules of Thumb

Budget Increases

Rule: +15-20% every 3-4 days
Max: Never increase > 25% in one change
Monitor: 48 hours after each increase
Stop: If CPA rises > 15% above target
Reset: Revert to previous budget, wait 7 days

New Keyword Additions

Rule: 2-3 new ad groups per week
Start: Phrase + Broad (Smart Bidding only)
Monitor: 7-14 days for data
Promote: Winners to Exact + own ad group
Cull: No conversions after 100 clicks

Audience Expansion

Rule: 1 new audience per week
Start: Observation mode always
Switch: To Targeting after 7 days of data
Bid Adjust: +10-50% based on performance
Exclude: Past converters from prospecting

Geographic Scaling

Rule: Top cities first, then regions
Separate: Campaigns per geo for budget control
Localize: Ad copy and landing pages
Monitor: CPA per region (may vary 20-40%)
Pause: Regions with CPA > 2x target

Key Formulas

ROAS Formula

ROAS = Revenue ÷ Ad Spend × 100
Break-even ROAS = 1 ÷ Profit Margin
Example: 60% margin → Break-even = 167%
Target: 300-500% for profitable scaling

CPA Formula

CPA = Total Spend ÷ Total Conversions
Max CPA = Customer LTV × Profit Margin
Example: $200 LTV × 60% = $120 max CPA
Target: 50% of max CPA for healthy scaling

Impression Share

IS = Your Impressions ÷ Total Eligible
Lost IS (Budget) = Can't afford more
Lost IS (Rank) = QS or bid too low
Target: 70%+ for scaling campaigns

Conversion Rate

CVR = Conversions ÷ Clicks × 100
Search benchmark: 3-5%
Display benchmark: 0.5-1.5%
E-commerce benchmark: 2-3%
Below benchmark = fix LP before scaling

Pro Tip

Print this cheat sheet and keep it next to your screen. The scaling rules (20% budget increases, 3-4 day intervals, 48-hour monitoring) are the difference between profitable scaling and wasted budget. When in doubt, scale slower.
