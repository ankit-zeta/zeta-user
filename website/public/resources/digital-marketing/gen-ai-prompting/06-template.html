Email Marketing

Welcome Email Sequence
Act as an email marketing specialist. Write a 5-email welcome sequence for [product/service]. Context: New subscriber signed up for [lead magnet] on [date]. Audience: [description of subscriber]. Goal: Educate, build trust, convert to paying customer. Brand voice: [professional/friendly/technical]. For each email provide: 1. Subject line (under 50 chars) 2. Preview text (under 90 chars) 3. Email body (150-250 words) 4. CTA button text 5. Send timing (day + time) Emails should follow: Day 0 (Welcome) → Day 1 (Value) → Day 3 (Social proof) → Day 5 (Objection handling) → Day 7 (Offer).

Social Media Content

LinkedIn Thought Leadership Post
Act as a LinkedIn content strategist. Write a thought leadership post about [topic]. Context: I'm a [role] at [company] with [X years] experience. Audience: [who reads my LinkedIn]. Goal: [engagement / leads / brand awareness]. Format: - Hook (first line that stops the scroll) - Story or insight (2-3 paragraphs) - Key takeaway (bolded) - Question to drive comments - 3-5 relevant hashtags Tone: Professional but conversational. Share real experience, not theory. Length: 150-200 words (ideal for LinkedIn). Avoid: Emojis in body, clickbait, generic advice.

Ad Copywriting

Meta Ads Copy
Act as a performance marketer specializing in Meta Ads. Write ad copy for [product]. Product: [what you're selling] Price: [price or "free trial"] Target audience: [demographics + interests + pain points] Campaign objective: [conversions / leads / traffic] Unique value proposition: [what makes it different] Provide: 1. Primary text (3 variants: curiosity, urgency, value) 2. Headline (under 40 chars, 3 variants) 3. Description (under 30 chars, 2 variants) 4. CTA button recommendation Rules: - No claims without proof - Include social proof where possible - Match landing page message - Under 125 chars for primary text in mobile view

Blog Content

SEO Blog Post Outline
Act as an SEO content strategist. Create a blog post outline for the keyword "[target keyword]". Search intent: [informational / commercial / transactional] Target audience: [who] Word count target: [1500-2500] Competitor analysis: [top 3 ranking URLs] Provide: 1. Title (with keyword, under 60 chars) 2. Meta description (under 155 chars) 3. H2 headings (5-8 sections) 4. H3 subheadings under each H2 5. Key points to cover under each section 6. Internal linking opportunities 7. FAQ section (5 questions) 8. Featured snippet opportunity Include: stats to cite, expert quotes to reference, visual content suggestions.

Research & Analysis

Competitor Analysis
Act as a market research analyst. Analyze the competitor: [competitor name/URL]. Research and provide: 1. Value proposition (one sentence) 2. Target audience (who they serve) 3. Pricing model (free/freemium/subscription/one-time) 4. Key features (top 5) 5. Content strategy (blog, social, email, ads) 6. Strengths (what they do well) 7. Weaknesses (gaps we can exploit) 8. Customer reviews summary (from G2, Trustpilot, etc.) Present as a comparison table if possible. Flag any information you're unsure about.
