Agency Systems Checklist

Setup, operations, and scale phase checklists for building your agency

Phase 1: Setup (Month 1-2)

Foundation — Before You Hire

Legal & Finance: Business entity (LLC), business bank account, separate accounting, basic contract template
Pricing Model: Choose your model (retainer/project/value-based), set minimum rates, create rate card
Services Defined: Write clear service descriptions, define scope boundaries, create deliverable templates
Contract & SOW: Master service agreement, statement of work template, payment terms (Net 15 or Net 30)
Tools Stack: Project management (Asana), communication (Slack), reporting (Looker Studio), time tracking (Harvest)
Portfolio: Case studies with results, client testimonials, before/after examples
Website: Professional site with services, case studies, contact form, social proof

Phase 2: Operations (Month 3-6)

Client Delivery — Build the Machine

Onboarding SOP: Welcome email, questionnaire, kickoff call template, access checklist, 30-day plan
Service SOPs: Document every recurring task, step-by-step instructions, quality checkpoints
Reporting: Weekly status update template, monthly performance report, automated data collection
Communication: Response time standards, escalation process, regular check-in cadence
Project Management: Task templates, deadline tracking, workload management, client portal
First Hire: Define role, write job description, hire contractor (not full-time), transfer 1 client as test

Phase 3: Scale (Month 6-12)

Growth — From Freelancer to Agency

Hiring System: Standardized interview process, skills assessment, trial project structure
Training: Onboarding program for new hires, shadow period, mentorship structure
KPI Dashboard: Revenue, profit margin, utilization rate, client retention, NPS — tracked weekly
Sales System: Lead generation, proposal template, follow-up sequence, referral program
Client Retention: Quarterly business reviews, upsell process, churn prevention, exit interviews
Capacity Planning: Forecast when you'll need to hire, pipeline tracking, revenue forecasting
Financial: Monthly P&L review, cash flow management, emergency fund (3-6 months expenses)
Delegation: Remove yourself from delivery, focus on sales + strategy, document your role too

Pro Tip

The checklist order matters: Setup first (you can't hire without systems), Operations second (you can't scale without clients), Scale third (you can't grow without revenue). Don't skip phases — each builds on the previous.
