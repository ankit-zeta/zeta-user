Agency Resource List

Bonsai, HoneyBook, Asana, Slack, Loom, and essential agency tools

Project Management

Asana

Project management for agencies. Task templates, timelines, workload management. Great for client work.

Free for 10 users / $10.99/user/mo Pro

Monday.com

Visual project management. Good for client-facing dashboards. More visual than Asana.

$8/seat/mo Basic / $16/seat/mo Standard

ClickUp

All-in-one workspace. Docs, tasks, goals, whiteboards. Replaces multiple tools.

Free / $7/user/mo Unlimited

Trello

Simple Kanban boards. Good for small teams or simple workflows. Easy to learn.

Free / $5/user/mo Standard

Client Management (CRM)

HoneyBook

CRM for service-based businesses. Contracts, invoices, scheduling, client portal. All-in-one.

$19/mo Starter / $79/mo Essentials

Dubsado

CRM with strong workflow automation. Custom forms, contracts, proposals. Great for agencies.

$20/mo Starter / $40/mo Premier

Pipedrive

Sales-focused CRM. Pipeline management, deal tracking, email integration. Good for B2B.

$14/user/mo Essential / $29/user/mo Advanced

HubSpot

Free CRM with marketing tools. Good for growing agencies. Scales to enterprise.

Free CRM / $45/mo Starter

Contracts & Invoicing

Bonsai

Freelancer/agency platform. Contracts, proposals, invoicing, time tracking, tax prep. All-in-one.

$17/mo Starter / $32/mo Professional

FreshBooks

Invoicing + accounting. Professional invoices, expense tracking, time tracking. Client-friendly.

$8.50/mo Lite / $15/mo Plus

Wave

Free invoicing + accounting. Good for starting out. Paid payroll and payment processing.

Free / $16/mo for payroll

QuickBooks

Industry-standard accounting. Full bookkeeping, tax prep, payroll. Best for larger agencies.

$15/mo Simple Start / $30/mo Plus

Communication & Documentation

Slack

Team communication. Channels for projects, clients, internal. Integrates with everything.

Free / $7.25/user/mo Pro

Loom

Video messaging. Record walkthroughs, tutorials, reports. Saves hours of meetings.

Free (25 vids) / $12.50/user/mo Business

Notion

Knowledge base + wiki. Document SOPs, playbooks, processes. Great for agencies.

Free / $8/user/mo Plus

Google Workspace

Email, docs, sheets, slides, drive. Professional email. Collaboration tools.

$6/user/mo Business Starter

Pro Tip

Start with free tools, upgrade as revenue justifies it. Don't pay for tools you're not using. The best tool is the one your team actually uses — a $100/mo tool nobody uses is worse than a free tool everyone uses. Reevaluate tool stack every 6 months.
