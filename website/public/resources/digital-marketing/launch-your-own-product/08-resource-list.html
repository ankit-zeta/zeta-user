Pro Tip

Minimum viable launch stack: Gumroad/Lemon Squeezy (payments) + ConvertKit (email) + Carrd (landing page) + Plausible (analytics). Total cost: under $30/month. Don't over-tool — spend that time building your product and audience instead.
