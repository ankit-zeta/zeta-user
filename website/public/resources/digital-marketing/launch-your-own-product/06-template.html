Landing Page Copy Template

High-Converting Landing Page Structure

HEADLINE (Problem + Promise): "[Result] Without [Pain Point]" Example: "Ship Your Side Project in 2 Weeks Without Writing Code" SUBHEADLINE (Who + What): "For [target audience] who want to [desired outcome]" Example: "For indie hackers who want to launch profitable products faster" HERO CTA: [Button: Start Building → Free] Below button: "Join 2,000+ builders" or "No credit card required" PROBLEM SECTION: "Sound familiar?" • [Pain point 1 — be specific] • [Pain point 2 — emotional trigger] • [Pain point 3 — cost of inaction] SOLUTION SECTION: "Introducing [Product Name]" [1-2 sentences describing what it does] [3 key benefits as bullet points] HOW IT WORKS (3 Steps): 1. [Action] → [Result] 2. [Action] → [Result] 3. [Action] → [Result] SOCIAL PROOF: "[Testimonial quote]" — [Name], [Role] • [Number] customers/users • [Rating] stars on [Platform] • [Logo bar of 3-5 notable customers] FEATURES (3-4 max): Feature 1: [What] → [Benefit] Feature 2: [What] → [Benefit] Feature 3: [What] → [Benefit] GUARANTEE: "[X]-day money-back guarantee. No questions asked." PRICING: Basic: $[Price]/mo — [What's included] Pro: $[Price]/mo — Everything in Basic + [Premium features] [CTA button on each tier] FAQ (3-5 questions): Q: [Most common objection] A: [Answer that addresses concern] FINAL CTA: "[Headline repeated]" [CTA button] "Join [number] customers"

Launch Email Sequence Template

6-Email Launch Sequence

EMAIL 1 (Monday — Story + Problem): Subject: "I spent 6 months failing at [topic]" • Personal story about the problem • "I tried everything..." • "Then I discovered..." • Tease the solution coming Wednesday • No selling yet — build anticipation EMAIL 2 (Wednesday — Solution Reveal): Subject: "I built something to fix [problem]" • Introduce your product • What it does (1-2 sentences) • 3 key benefits • "Want early access?" • Link to landing page (not buy page yet) EMAIL 3 (Friday — Early Bird Opens): Subject: "Early bird is live (48 hours only)" • Announce launch • Early bird price: $[X] (regular $[Y]) • What's included • Social proof (first buyers, testimonials) • Clear CTA: Get Early Bird Price → • Urgency: "Price goes up Sunday midnight" EMAIL 4 (Saturday — FAQ + Objection Handling): Subject: "Your questions answered" • Top 5 objections as FAQ • Each answer addresses concern directly • Additional testimonials • Reminder: early bird ends tomorrow • CTA: Lock In Your Price → EMAIL 5 (Sunday — Last Chance): Subject: "⏰ Final hours: Early bird ends tonight" • Urgency-focused • Recap benefits • Final testimonial • "After midnight, price goes to $[regular]" • Bold CTA: Get Early Bird Price → EMAIL 6 (Tuesday — Post-Launch): Subject: "Thank you + what's next" • Thank buyers publicly • Share launch results (social proof) • What's coming next (roadmap teaser) • Ask for feedback • Upsell: Premium tier or add-on POST-PURCHASE SEQUENCE (7 days): Day 0: Welcome + login instructions Day 1: Quick start guide Day 3: Advanced feature spotlight Day 5: Case study / success story Day 7: Feedback request + testimonial ask

Social Media Launch Posts

Twitter/X Launch Thread

THREAD (10 tweets): 1/ "I just launched [Product] after [X months] of building. Here's what I learned and why it took [so long / so fast]: 🧵" 2/ "The problem: [One sentence describing the pain point] I talked to [X] people who all said the same thing..." 3/ "[Quote from customer interview or tweet about the problem]" 4/ "So I built [Product] — [One sentence describing solution]" 5/ "Here's how it works: → Step 1: [Action] → Step 2: [Action] → Step 3: [Result]" 6/ "What makes it different: • [Differentiator 1] • [Differentiator 2] • [Differentiator 3]" 7/ "Early results from beta: • [Metric 1] • [Metric 2] • [Testimonial quote]" 8/ "Launch special: [X]% off for the first [X] customers Regular price: $[Y] Launch price: $[Z] Link in reply 👇" 9/ "I'm terrified to hit publish on this. If you've ever wanted to [desired outcome], this is for you. [Link]" 10/ "If this thread resonated: 🔁 RT the first tweet 💬 Drop a reply with your biggest [topic] challenge 📩 DM me for questions Thank you for reading. 🙏"

LinkedIn Launch Post

"I spent [X months] building something I wish existed. The problem: [Pain point in 1 sentence] After talking to [X] [target audience], I realized I wasn't alone. So I built [Product Name]. Here's what it does: → [Benefit 1] → [Benefit 2] → [Benefit 3] It's live today. Early bird pricing: $[X] (regular $[Y]) Link in comments 👇 #BuildingInPublic #LaunchDay #[Industry]"

Launch Day Hour-by-Hour

Launch Day Timeline

8:00 AM — Final product test, checkout test, email preview 8:30 AM — Team briefing: roles, responsibilities, emergency contacts 9:00 AM — Send launch email to waitlist 9:15 AM — Post Twitter thread (pin to profile) 9:30 AM — Post LinkedIn update 9:45 AM — Post Instagram stories + feed post 10:00 AM — Share in communities (Reddit, Indie Hackers, Slack groups) 10:30 AM — Respond to first wave of comments/DMs 11:00 AM — Monitor sales dashboard, fix any issues 12:00 PM — Midday social proof tweet: "X sales in 3 hours!" 1:00 PM — Personal outreach to 10 VIP contacts 2:00 PM — Follow up with bloggers/influencers who haven't responded 3:00 PM — Thank early buyers via personal email 4:00 PM — Share customer testimonial on social 5:00 PM — End-of-day metrics: sales, revenue, email stats 6:00 PM — Evening social post with launch recap 8:00 PM — Final check: support inbox, any urgent issues 9:00 PM — Celebrate 🎉 (you earned it)

Pro Tip

These templates are starting points — customize them with your voice, story, and brand. The best launch copy is specific, emotional, and honest. Don't try to sound like a corporation. People buy from people they trust.
