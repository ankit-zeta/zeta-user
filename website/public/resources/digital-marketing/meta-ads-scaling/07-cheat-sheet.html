Budget Rules & Formulas

The 20% Scaling Rule

Daily budget increase ≤ 20% every 3-4 days
Example: $100 → $120 → $144 → $173 → $207
Monitor CPA 48h after each increase
If CPA > 15% above target: roll back

Funnel Budget Formula

TOF: Total Budget × 50-60%
MOF: Total Budget × 20-25%
BOF: Total Budget × 15-25%
Adjust: Higher LTV = more TOF budget

Minimum Viable Budget

Per ad set: $20-50/day minimum
Per campaign (CBO): $50-100/day minimum
Per account: $100+/day for meaningful data
Below = too slow to learn, unreliable data

Scale Ceiling Indicators

CPA > 1.5x target = stop scaling
Frequency > 3.5 = stop scaling
ROAS < 1.2x = stop scaling
Learning Limited = stop scaling

Scaling Formulas

ROAS Formula

ROAS = Revenue ÷ Ad Spend
Break-even ROAS = 1 ÷ Profit Margin
Example: 65% margin → 1/0.65 = 1.54x break-even
Target ROAS = Break-even × 2-3x

CPA Formula

CPA = Total Spend ÷ Conversions
Max CPA = Customer LTV × Margin %
Example: $500 LTV × 65% = $325 max CPA
Target: Max CPA × 0.5 = $162.50

LTV:CAC Ratio

LTV:CAC = Customer LTV ÷ CAC (CPA)
Target: > 3:1 for healthy scaling
Below 3:1 = unsustainable growth
Below 1:1 = losing money on every customer

Frequency Formula

Frequency = Impressions ÷ Reach
Target: < 3 (prospecting), < 5 (retargeting)
Frequency > 3 = creative fatigue risk
Frequency > 5 = audience saturated

Common Scaling Mistakes

❌ Budget Doubling

Mistake: Increasing budget 50-100% overnight
Result: Resets learning phase, CPA spikes
Fix: 20% increases every 3-4 days
Why: Meta needs time to re-optimize delivery

❌ Editing During Learning

Mistake: Changing budget/audience/bid too frequently
Result: Each edit resets learning (72h clock)
Fix: Wait 72h minimum between edits
Why: Learning phase needs 50 events to stabilize

❌ Ignoring Creative Fatigue

Mistake: Running same ads for 3+ months
Result: Rising CPM, falling CTR, declining ROAS
Fix: Refresh creative every 2-4 weeks
Why: Audience sees same ad = diminishing returns

❌ Scaling Without Tracking

Mistake: Scaling before Pixel + CAPI verified
Result: Bad data → bad optimization → wasted spend
Fix: Verify tracking 100% before scaling
Why: Meta optimizes based on conversion data quality

❌ No Exclusion Audiences

Mistake: Showing ads to existing customers
Result: Wasted spend, annoyed customers
Fix: Exclude purchasers, leads, employees
Why: Prospecting budget should reach NEW people only

❌ Too Many Campaigns

Mistake: 15+ campaigns running simultaneously
Result: Budget spread thin, no campaign exits learning
Fix: 3-7 campaigns max, consolidate
Why: Meta needs budget concentration to optimize
