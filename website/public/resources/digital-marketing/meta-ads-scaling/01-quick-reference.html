Budget Allocation Rules

The 20% Rule

Increase daily budget ≤ 20% every 3-4 days
Example: $100 → $120 → $144 → $173
Monitor CPA for 48h after each increase
If CPA rises > 15%: roll back to previous level

Funnel Budget Split

TOF (Prospecting): 50-60% of total budget
MOF (Warm): 20-25% of total budget
BOF (Retargeting): 15-25% of total budget
Adjust based on LTV and funnel velocity

CBO vs ABO for Scaling

CBO: Let Meta distribute to winners (default)
ABO: Manual control when testing specific audiences
Use CBO for scaling, ABO for testing
Min 3-5 ad sets per CBO campaign

Budget Ceiling Indicators

CPA > 1.5x target for 3+ days
Frequency > 3.5 (audience saturated)
ROAS < 1.2x (below breakeven)
Learning Limited status (too small)

Audience Expansion Tactics

Lookalike Progression

Start: 1% Lookalike (highest quality)
Step 2: 2-3% Lookalike (balanced)
Step 3: 5-10% Lookalike (reach)
Step 4: Broad targeting (AI finds converters)

Custom Audience Expansion

Website visitors: 30d → 60d → 180d
Engagement: Video viewers → Post engagers
Customer list: Purchasers → All customers
Lookalike from each Custom Audience type

Interest Stacking

Layer 3-5 related interests per ad set
Test: Fitness + Yoga + Health Food
Test: Entrepreneur + SaaS + Startups
Use Advantage+ for auto-expansion

Geographic Expansion

Start: Top 3 cities → Country-wide
Step 2: Adjacent countries (similar culture)
Step 3: International (localize creative)
Always test: US → UK → CA → AU → EU

Scaling Decision Matrix

Scale Aggressively (Vertical)

✅ ROAS > target for 14+ days
✅ CPA < target for 14+ days
✅ Frequency < 2.5
✅ 100+ conversions per week
✅ Creative pipeline ready (3+ concepts)

Scale Cautiously (Horizontal)

⚠️ ROAS near target (within 10%)
⚠️ CPA near target (within 15%)
⚠️ Frequency 2.5-3.5
⚠️ 50-100 conversions per week
⚠️ Limited creative pipeline

Don't Scale (Optimize First)

❌ ROAS below target for 7+ days
❌ CPA above target for 7+ days
❌ Frequency > 3.5
❌ < 50 conversions per week
❌ Creative fatigue signals

Pause & Rebuild

🛑 ROAS < breakeven for 14+ days
🛑 CPA > 2x target for 14+ days
🛑 Ad account issues/rejections
🛑 Attribution broken (iOS, CAPI)
🛑 Landing page/conversion issue

💡 Pro Tip

Always scale horizontally FIRST (new audiences, creatives, campaigns). Vertical scaling hits diminishing returns fast. Horizontal builds a diversified portfolio that's resilient to creative fatigue and audience saturation.
