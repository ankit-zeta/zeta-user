Freelancing Tools

Invoicing, project management, contracts, time tracking, proposals, and business operations tools

Invoicing & Payments

  • Stripe
    Online payments: Invoicing, subscriptions, payment links, international. 2.9% + 30¢. Dashboard + API. Essential for freelancers.
    stripe.com →
    PaymentsInvoicingSubscriptions
  • Wise (TransferWise)
    International transfers at mid-market rate. Multi-currency account. Local bank details in US, UK, EU, AU. Cheaper than PayPal.
    wise.com →
    InternationalLow FeesMulti-currency
  • HoneyBook / Dubsado
    All-in-one: Proposals, contracts, invoices, payments, scheduling, client portal. ~$19-39/mo. Great for service businesses.
    honeybook.com →
    All-in-oneClient PortalContracts
  • FreshBooks / Wave
    Accounting + invoicing. FreshBooks: ~$15/mo, great UX. Wave: Free, basic features. Both: expense tracking, reports, tax prep.
    freshbooks.com →
    AccountingInvoicingFree Option
  • PayPal / Payoneer
    Widely accepted. PayPal: 3.49% + 49¢, instant access. Payoneer: Better for marketplace payouts, mass pay. Higher fees.
    paypal.com →
    UniversalMarketplace

Legal & Business Setup

  • Stripe Atlas / Clerky / Firstbase
    Incorporate US company (LLC/C-Corp) from anywhere. Bank account, EIN, legal docs. $500-1000. For international founders.
    stripe.com/atlas →
    IncorporationUS EntityBank Account
  • IRS EIN Application
    Free Employer ID Number. Required for: Business bank account, hiring, S-Corp election, vendor forms. Instant online.
    IRS EIN →
    FreeOfficialRequired
  • Business Bank Accounts (Mercury, Novo, Relay, Chase)
    Mercury: Startup-friendly, API, virtual cards. Novo: No fees, integrations. Relay: Sub-accounts for profit first. Chase: Branches, credit.
    mercury.com →
    StartupAPINo Fees
  • QuickBooks Online / Xero
    Full accounting: Bank sync, reconciliation, payroll, tax, reports. QBO: ~$30/mo. Xero: ~$15/mo. Accountant access free.
    quickbooks.intuit.com →
    AccountingTaxPayroll
  • Collective / Heard / Bench
    Bookkeeping + tax for freelancers/S-corps. Collective: S-Corp setup + payroll + tax. ~$200-300/mo. Saves 10+ hrs/mo.
    collective.com →
    S-CorpTaxBookkeeping
