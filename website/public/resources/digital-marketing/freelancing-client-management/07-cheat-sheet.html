Difficult Client Cheat Sheet

Scope creep responses, late payment follow-ups, revision limit enforcement, expectations management, termination protocols

Scope Creep Responses

"Can you just add...?"

"That's a great idea! It's outside our current scope, but I'd be happy to add it as a Phase 2 item. Want me to send a quick change order with time/cost?"

"While you're at it..."

"I want to make sure we deliver quality on what we've committed to. That addition would require [X hours / $Y] and extend the timeline by [Z days]. Shall I scope it separately?"

"It's a small change"

"Small changes add up! Our agreement includes [2] revision rounds. This would be round [3]. Per our contract, additional rounds are $[X] each. Want to proceed?"

"My colleague/boss wants..."

"Totally get it. To keep things on track, could you consolidate all feedback through [SPOC Name]? That way we avoid conflicting directions and stay within scope."

"We didn't discuss this but..."

"No problem! Let me document this as a change request with impact on timeline/budget. You'll have it for approval by EOD."

Late Payment Follow-Ups

Day 1 (Due Date)

Subject: Invoice #[Number] — Due Today Hi [Name], friendly reminder that Invoice #[Number] for $[Amount] is due today. Link: [Stripe/Bank details]. Let me know if you need anything!

Day 3

Subject: Following up — Invoice #[Number] Hi [Name], checking in on Invoice #[Number] ($[Amount]). Happy to hop on a quick call if there are any questions. Payment link: [Link]

Day 7 (Late)

Subject: Past Due — Invoice #[Number] — Late Fee Applies Hi [Name], Invoice #[Number] is now 7 days past due. Per our agreement, a 1.5% monthly late fee applies starting today. Please process by [Date] to avoid further charges. [Link]

Day 15 (Escalation)

Subject: URGENT — Invoice #[Number] — Work Pause Notice Hi [Name], Invoice #[Number] is 15 days overdue. As outlined in our contract, we'll pause all project work effective [Date] until payment is received. Please confirm payment timeline.

Day 30 (Final)

Subject: Final Notice — Invoice #[Number] — Collections Hi [Name], despite multiple reminders, Invoice #[Number] ($[Amount]) remains unpaid after 30 days. We'll be engaging collections/legal counsel on [Date]. This can be resolved today with payment: [Link]

Revision Limit Enforcement

Round 1-2 (Included)

"Great feedback! This falls within our included revision rounds. I'll implement and send updated version by [Date]."

Round 3+ (Billable)

"This would be revision round [3], which exceeds our included [2] rounds. Additional rounds are $[X] each. Shall I proceed and add to next invoice?"

Vague Feedback

"To keep revisions efficient, could you provide specific, actionable feedback? e.g., 'Move button 20px left' vs 'Make it pop more.' Screenshots/Loom videos help!"

Contradictory Feedback

"I'm seeing some conflicting direction between [Stakeholder A] and [Stakeholder B]. Could you align internally and send one consolidated feedback doc? This prevents rework."

Endless Tweaks

"We're at revision [4] on this deliverable. At this point, we're polishing beyond the original scope. Let's call this 'done' and move to the next milestone. Additional polish: $[X]/hr."

Expectations Management

Unrealistic Timeline

"I'd love to hit that date! Based on our current scope and capacity, the realistic timeline is [X weeks]. To meet [their date], we'd need to: reduce scope to [Y], add [Z] resources ($), or start immediately with deposit."

Budget Mismatch

"I understand the budget is $[X]. For the scope we discussed, the investment is $[Y]. Options: 1) Reduce scope to fit $[X], 2) Phase the project (Phase 1 now, Phase 2 later), 3) Explore retainer for ongoing work."

"Make it like [Competitor]"

"Great reference! That site likely cost $[X] and took [Y] months. We can apply those principles: [specific elements]. For a custom build at your budget, I'd recommend [approach]."

Micromanagement

"I appreciate your involvement! To keep momentum, our process works best with: consolidated feedback at milestones, SPOC for decisions, bi-weekly syncs. Happy to add a weekly 15-min check-in if that helps."

Ghosting / Unresponsive

"Hi [Name], haven't heard back on [deliverable/feedback]. We're ready to proceed but need your sign-off by [Date] to stay on timeline. If [Date] passes, timeline shifts accordingly. Let me know!"

Red Flags — When to Walk Away (Before Signing)

🚩 "We'll pay when funded"

No budget certainty. Walk away or require deposit.

🚩 "Equity instead of cash"

High risk, zero liquidity. Only if you'd invest cash.

🚩 "Unlimited revisions"

Scope creep guarantee. Cap at 2-3 rounds.

🚩 No decision-maker access

Feedback loops through intermediaries = delays + dilution.

🚩 "Quick/Simple" for complex work

Undervalues expertise. They don't understand the work.

🚩 Bad-mouths previous freelancers

Pattern: They're the common denominator. You're next.

🚩 Rushes contract review

Wants you to skip legal. Protects them, not you.

🚩 Demands work before deposit

No deposit = no commitment. Never start without it.

💡 Pro Tip

Document every difficult conversation in writing (email/Slack thread). "Per our call just now, you requested X which is outside scope. I'll send a change order by EOD." Paper trail = protection.
