Template 1: Proposal Template

Customize per client — save as master, duplicate for each proposal

[YOUR LOGO / LETTERHEAD] PROPOSAL: [Project Title] Prepared for: [Client Name / Company] Date: [Date] | Valid Until: [Date + 30 days] Prepared by: [Your Name / Company] ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────── 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY [Client] is seeking [solution] to [achieve outcome]. We propose [approach] delivering [key deliverables] within [timeframe] for an investment of [amount]. This will enable [Client] to [specific business outcome: revenue, efficiency, growth]. 2. PROBLEM STATEMENT [Describe the current situation using client's own words from discovery.] Current challenges: • [Challenge 1: e.g., "Website converts at 0.8% vs industry 2.5%"] • [Challenge 2: e.g., "Manual processes cost 20 hrs/week"] • [Challenge 3: e.g., "No analytics visibility into user behavior"] Business impact: [Quantify: lost revenue, wasted time, competitive disadvantage] 3. PROPOSED SOLUTION Our approach: [High-level methodology/philosophy] Phase 1: Discovery & Strategy (Week 1-2) • [Deliverable 1: e.g., "Comprehensive audit + strategy document"] • [Deliverable 2: e.g., "Sitemap, user flows, wireframes"] • Acceptance: Strategy document approved by [Client Contact] Phase 2: Design & Development (Week 3-6) • [Deliverable 1: e.g., "High-fidelity designs for 8 pages"] • [Deliverable 2: e.g., "Webflow build: CMS, interactions, responsive"] • [Deliverable 3: e.g., "Staging environment for review"] • Acceptance: Staging demo approved Phase 3: Launch & Handoff (Week 7) • [Deliverable 1: e.g., "Production deployment + DNS"] • [Deliverable 2: e.g., "CMS training session (recorded)"] • [Deliverable 3: e.g., "Documentation + 30-day support"] • Acceptance: Live site + training complete 4. Template 2: Contract Template (Key Clauses)

Attach Scope of Work (Template 1, Sections 3-6) as Exhibit A

MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT This Agreement is entered into [Date] between: [Your Company], ["Provider"] and [Client Company], ["Client"] 1. SERVICES Provider shall perform Services described in Exhibit A (Scope of Work). Provider has authority to determine method/manner of performance. 2. COMPENSATION Fees per Exhibit A. Payment terms: [Net-15 / per milestone]. Late payments: 1.5%/month. Expenses pre-approved in writing. 3. TIMELINE Start: [Date] | Estimated Completion: [Date] Milestones per Exhibit A. Delays from Client dependencies extend timeline equally. 4. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY Work Product: Client owns all deliverables upon full payment. Provider Retains: Pre-existing code, tools, methodologies, frameworks, generic components. License: Provider grants Client perpetual, worldwide, exclusive license to Work Product. 5. CONFIDENTIALITY Both parties protect Confidential Information (non-public business info). Exceptions: Public knowledge, independently developed, legal requirement. Survives: 3 years post-termination. 6. TERM & TERMINATION Term: Until Services complete or terminated. Either party: 15 days written notice. For Cause: Immediate (breach, insolvency). On Termination: Pay for work completed + Kill Fee (25% of remaining). Transition: 5 business days handoff assistance. 7. WARRANTIES & DISCLAIMERS Provider: Professional standard of care. No other warranties. Client: Owns/provides all content/assets legally. Complies with laws. 8. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY Neither party liable for indirect/consequential damages. Provider's liability capped at total fees paid (or 2x fees). Excludes: IP infringement, Confidentiality breach, Gross negligence. 9. INDEMNIFICATION Client indemnifies Provider for: Client content legality, Third-party claims from Client materials. Provider indemnifies Client for: IP infringement in Work Product. 10. GENERAL Governing Law: [Your State/Country]. Disputes: Mediation → Arbitration (AAA). Entire Agreement: Supersedes all prior. Amendments in writing. Severability: Invalid clause removed, rest stands. Assignment: Neither party without consent (except acquisition). SIGNATURES: Provider: _________________________ Date: ________ Client: ___________________________ Date: ________
