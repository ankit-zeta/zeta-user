Proposal Structure (Winning Format)

1. Executive Summary

One paragraph: Client's problem → Your solution → Expected outcome. Write last, place first.

2. Problem Statement

Show you understand: Current pain, business impact, cost of inaction. Use their words from discovery.

3. Proposed Solution

What you'll deliver: Phases, milestones, deliverables, timeline. Visual: Gantt or simple table.

4. Investment & ROI

Price + payment terms. ROI calculation: "This $15K project saves $50K/yr = 3.3x return in year 1."

5. Social Proof

Relevant case study (same industry/problem), testimonial, logo bar. "We helped [Similar Co] achieve [Result]."

6. Next Steps

Clear CTA: "Sign below → Kickoff scheduled → Deposit → We start Monday." Remove friction.

7. Terms & Assumptions

What's included/excluded, revision rounds, timeline dependencies, cancellation policy, IP ownership.

8. About You/Team

Brief bio, relevant experience, why you. Photo builds trust. Keep it client-focused, not ego.

Contract Essentials

Scope of Work (SOW)

Detailed deliverables, formats, quantities, revisions included, what's NOT included. Attach as Exhibit A.

Payment Terms

Deposit (25-50%), Milestones, Net-15/30, Late fees (1.5%/mo), Kill fee (25-50% if cancelled), Expenses reimbursed.

Timeline & Milestones

Start/end dates, milestone dates, dependencies (client provides X by Y), buffer time, change order process.

Intellectual Property

Work-for-hire: Client owns final deliverables. You retain: Pre-existing code, methodologies, tools, generic components.

Confidentiality / NDA

Mutual or one-way. Define confidential info. Exceptions: Public knowledge, independently developed, legal requirement.

Termination

Either party: 14-30 days notice. For cause: Immediate. Payment for work completed + kill fee. Transition assistance.

Liability & Indemnity

Cap liability at contract value (or 2x). No consequential damages. Client indemnifies for their content/data legality.

Dispute Resolution

Mediation → Arbitration (AAA/JAMS) → Court. Venue: Your jurisdiction. Prevailing party gets attorney fees.

Scope Management Rules

Define "Done" Upfront

Acceptance criteria per deliverable: "Landing page: 5 sections, mobile responsive, 3 browser tested, CMS editable."

Revision Limits

Include 2-3 rounds in price. Additional: $X/round or hourly. Document feedback in writing (not Slack/verbal).

Change Order Process

New request → Impact assessment (time/cost) → Written change order → Client signs → Then work starts. No exceptions.

Client Dependencies

"We need your brand assets by Friday to meet Monday milestone." Document in proposal. Missed = timeline shifts.

Payment Terms Cheat Sheet

Standard Structures

50/50 (deposit/final) — Simple, fair
30/40/30 (deposit/milestone/final) — Safer
25/25/25/25 (4 milestones) — Large projects
Monthly retainer: 1st of month, auto-charge

Red Flags

Net-60/90, "Pay when funded", "Equity only", No deposit, Vague milestones, Unlimited revisions, No kill fee.

Collection Process

Day 1: Friendly reminder
Day 7: Firm email + late fee notice
Day 15: Pause work (contract allows)
Day 30: Formal demand → Collections/legal

Tools

Stripe (cards, ACH), Wise (international), HoneyBook/Dubsado (proposals+payments), QuickBooks/Xero (invoicing).

Client Communication Cadence

Weekly Status Email

Friday 4pm: This week's wins, Next week's focus, Blockers/Needs, Decisions needed. Template saves 30 min.

Bi-Weekly Sync Call

30 min: Progress, Demo, Feedback, Adjustments. Record + notes in CRM/Notion. Cancel if nothing to discuss.

Slack/Async Boundaries

Response SLA: 4 hrs business hours. Urgent = Call/Text. No weekends unless emergency retainer. Use threads.

Quarterly Business Review

Retainer clients: Results vs goals, Upcoming priorities, Scope adjustments, Relationship health. Strategic, not tactical.

💡 Pro Tip

Never send a proposal without a discovery call first. You can't price what you don't understand. The call is where you uncover the real budget, timeline, and decision-makers.
