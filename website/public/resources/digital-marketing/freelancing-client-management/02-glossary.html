Freelancing Glossary

Essential terminology for contracts, pricing, client management, and business operations

Contract & Legal Terms

Scope of Work (SOW)
Detailed description of deliverables, tasks, timelines, and acceptance criteria. The "what" of the agreement.
Master Services Agreement (MSA)
Framework contract governing ongoing relationship. SOWs attach as exhibits. Reduces renegotiation per project.
Statement of Work
Project-specific document under MSA: Scope, timeline, pricing, acceptance criteria, assumptions.
Work for Hire
Legal doctrine: Client owns copyright of work created. Standard in freelance contracts. You retain pre-existing IP.
Kill Fee / Termination Fee
Payment owed if client cancels mid-project. Typically 25-50% of remaining contract value. Protects your time investment.
Indemnification
One party agrees to cover losses/legal fees if their actions cause harm. Mutual = both ways. Limit scope carefully.
Limitation of Liability
Cap on damages. Standard: Contract value or 2x fees. Excludes: Gross negligence, IP infringement, confidentiality breach.
Force Majeure
"Act of God" clause: Neither party liable for delays from events beyond control (pandemic, natural disaster, war).

Pricing & Financial Terms

Retainer
Recurring fee for reserved capacity/access. Not "pre-paid hours" — it's for availability. Unused time typically doesn't roll over.
Value-Based Pricing
Price based on client's perceived value/ROI, not your time. "This project generates $100K → 20% = $20K fee."
Scope Creep
Uncontrolled expansion of project scope without corresponding price/timeline adjustment. #1 profit killer.
Change Order
Formal document modifying scope, price, or timeline. Signed by both parties before additional work begins.
Net-30 / Net-15
Payment due 30/15 days after invoice date. Shorter = better cash flow. Negotiate Net-15 or due on receipt.
Deposit / Down Payment
Upfront payment (25-50%) before work starts. Secures commitment, covers initial costs, filters non-serious clients.
Milestone Payment
Payment tied to specific deliverable completion. Reduces risk vs single final payment. 3-4 milestones typical.
Late Fee
Penalty for overdue payment. Standard: 1.5%/month (18% APR) or flat $50-100. Must be in contract to enforce.

Client Management Terms

Discovery Call
Initial 15-30 min call to understand problem, budget, timeline, decision process. Qualifies lead before proposal.
Kickoff Meeting
Project start: Align on goals, timeline, communication, tools, contacts, first milestones. Sets tone for engagement.
Status Report
Regular update: Completed, In Progress, Upcoming, Blockers, Decisions Needed. Weekly/bi-weekly. Template-driven.
RACI Matrix
Responsible, Accountable, Consulted, Informed. Clarifies who does what. Essential for multi-stakeholder projects.
Single Point of Contact (SPOC)
One client representative for approvals/feedback. Prevents conflicting directions. Designate in kickoff.
Scope Boundary
Explicit list of what's NOT included. "Out of scope: Copywriting, Photography, Video, 3rd-party integrations, SEO."
Acceptance Criteria
Measurable standards for deliverable approval. "Page loads <3s, passes WCAG AA, matches Figma design ±2px."
Sign-off
Formal approval: Email "Approved" or signed document. Triggers next milestone payment. No work continues without it.

Business Operations Terms

Sole Proprietorship
Default freelance structure. No legal separation. Personal liability. Simple taxes (Schedule C).
LLC (Limited Liability Company)
Legal entity separating personal/business assets. Pass-through taxes. Credibility. $50-800 setup varies by state.
S-Corp Election
LLC taxed as S-Corp. Save self-employment tax on distributions. Requires: Reasonable salary, payroll, separate accounting.
EIN (Employer ID Number)
Business tax ID from IRS. Free. Required for: Business bank account, hiring, S-Corp, some clients' vendor forms.
W-9 / W-8BEN
W-9: US contractor tax form (client needs for 1099). W-8BEN: Foreign contractor (no US tax withholding).
1099-NEC
Client files if paid $600+ in year. You receive copy. Report on Schedule C. Track all client payments.
Quarterly Estimated Taxes
Pay-as-you-go: April 15, June 15, Sept 15, Jan 15. Avoid penalty: Pay 100% prior year tax or 90% current year.
Business Bank Account
Separate from personal. Required for LLC/S-Corp. Clean bookkeeping. Professional for clients. Mercury, Novo, Chase, Local CU.

Sales & Pipeline Terms

Lead / Prospect / Opportunity
Lead: Initial inquiry. Prospect: Qualified (budget, authority, need, timeline). Opportunity: Active proposal stage.
Sales Pipeline
Visual stages: Lead → Discovery → Proposal → Negotiation → Closed Won/Lost. Track in CRM (HubSpot, Pipedrive, Notion).
Conversion Rate
Proposals sent → Contracts signed. Freelance avg: 25-40%. Improve: Better qualification, stronger proposals.
Average Contract Value (ACV)
Total contract revenue / Number of contracts. Track monthly. Goal: Increase ACV over time via pricing/upsells.
Sales Cycle Length
Days from first contact to signed contract. Freelance: 2-8 weeks. Shorten: Clear process, decision-maker access, urgency.
Referral Pipeline
Systematic: Ask at project close, Incentivize (discount/credit), Track source, Nurture referrers. Best lead source.
Upsell / Cross-sell
Upsell: Higher tier/expand scope. Cross-sell: Adjacent service. "Since we built the site, need ongoing maintenance?"
Churn (Retainer)
Clients leaving per period. Monthly churn = Lost clients / Total clients. Target < 5% monthly. Exit interview = gold.

💡 Pro Tip

Create a "Client Dictionary" in your CRM: Each client's terminology, decision-makers, preferences, communication style. Reduces onboarding time for new team members and prevents miscommunication.
