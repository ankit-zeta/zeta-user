Case Study 1: SaaS Startup — "DevTools Pro"

From 0 to 15K LinkedIn followers in 12 months

B2B developer tool company targeting engineering teams and CTOs.

Strategy Breakdown

Content Pillars

Technical tutorials (40%), Engineering culture (25%), Product updates (20%), Industry commentary (15%)

Format Mix

Text posts with code snippets (50%), Carousel explainers (30%), Native video demos (15%), Polls (5%)

Posting Cadence

3x/week (Tue, Thu, Sat 9am EST); Founder posts 1x/week; Team members rotate 2x/week

Growth Tactics

Employee advocacy program, Guest posts on dev blogs, HN/Reddit cross-posting, Conference speaking clips

Top Performing Content

  • "How we reduced build times 80%" — Carousel, 12K impressions, 890 engagements, 45 demo requests
  • "Why we chose Rust over Go" — Text post, 8.4K impressions, 620 engagements, sparked 200+ comment technical debate
  • "A day in the life of our founding engineer" — Video, 5.2K views, 340 engagements, humanized brand

Key Insight

Technical audiences value depth over breadth. One detailed technical post outperforms 10 generic "thought leadership" posts. Show your work, share code, explain tradeoffs.

Clean beauty brand targeting women 25-40, $40-80 AOV, subscription model.

Strategy Breakdown

Content Pillars

Education (ingredients, routines) 35%, UGC/testimonials 25%, Behind-the-scenes 20%, Promotions 15%, Community 5%

Format Mix

Reels (40%), Carousels (30%), Single images (20%), Stories daily (10%), Lives monthly

Posting Cadence

Feed: 5x/week (Mon-Fri); Stories: Daily 5-7 frames; Reels: 3x/week; Live: 1x/month

Growth Tactics

Micro-influencer gifting (50/month), UGC contest ($500 prize), Email→IG funnel, Shoppable posts, Affiliate program

Top Performing Content

  • "Your 3-step morning routine" — Reel, 240K views, 18K likes, 2.3K saves, 450 profile visits
  • "Ingredient spotlight: Niacinamide" — Carousel, 45K impressions, 3.8K engagements, 1.2K saves
  • "Unboxing our new refillable packaging" — Reel, 180K views, drove 1,200 pre-orders in 48h

Key Insight

Saves = purchase intent. Educational carousels get saved 10x more than lifestyle posts. Create "reference content" people bookmark for later.

Plumbing/HVAC company serving 3-county metro area. $350 avg ticket, high LTV.

Strategy Breakdown

Platform Focus

Facebook (primary — local community), Instagram (visual proof), Google Business (reviews), TikTok (unexpected reach)

Content Pillars

Problem/solution demos 40%, Team culture 25%, Customer reviews 20%, Maintenance tips 15%

Posting Cadence

Facebook: 4x/week; Instagram: 3x/week + daily Stories; TikTok: 2x/week; GBP: Weekly updates

Growth Tactics

Review request automation, Neighborhood Facebook groups, Before/after reels, Tech takeovers, Community sponsorships

Top Performing Content

  • "Why your water heater is leaking" — Reel, 85K views, 42 calls tracked to this video
  • "Meet Mike: 20 years fixing pipes" — Photo + story, 12K reach, 890 engagements, humanized team
  • "5 things to check before calling a plumber" — Carousel, 22K impressions, 1.8K saves, positioned as helpful not salesy

Key Insight

Local businesses win with hyper-relevant, problem-solving content. "How-to" content builds trust before the sale. Track calls with unique phone numbers per platform.

Common Patterns Across All Three

What Worked Universally

  • Consistency over virality: All posted on schedule for 6+ months before hockey-stick growth
  • Platform-native formats: Reels on IG, Threads on LinkedIn, Native video on TikTok — no cross-posting same format
  • Founder/team visibility: Personal brands outperformed company pages 3-5x
  • Community-first: Replied to every comment within 2 hours; DM conversations > broadcast
  • Data-driven iteration: Weekly analytics review; doubled down on top 20% content types
  • Email capture: Every bio/link led to lead magnet; social → email → sale funnel

💡 Pro Tip

Don't copy their tactics — copy their principles. Consistency, platform-native content, personal connection, data-driven iteration, and funnel integration work in any niche.
