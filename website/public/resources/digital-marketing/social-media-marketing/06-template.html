Post Templates

Ready-to-use templates for carousel posts, Reel scripts, Story sequences, and caption formulas (PAS, AIDA, Hook-Story-Offer)

Carousel Post Outlines

Template 1: Educational Carousel (5-7 slides)

SLIDE 1: Hook + Promise Headline: "5 [Topic] Mistakes Costing You [Result]" Subtext: "Slide 3 changed everything for me" Visual: Clean title slide with your photo/logo SLIDE 2: The Problem "Most people think [common misconception]" "But the reality is [truth]" Visual: Simple illustration of the misconception SLIDE 3: The Insight (Your Unique Perspective) "Here's what [experts/top performers] do differently:" Visual: Your framework, diagram, or "aha!" moment SLIDE 4-6: Actionable Steps Step 1: [Specific action] → [Why it works] Step 2: [Specific action] → [Why it works] Step 3: [Specific action] → [Why it works] Visual: Screenshots, diagrams, or examples per step SLIDE 7: Summary + CTA "Save this for when you need it" "Follow @handle for more [topic] tips" "Comment 'GUIDE' for my free [resource]" Visual: Your photo + handle + arrow to follow button

Template 2: Process/Workflow Carousel

SLIDE 1: "How I [achieve result] in [timeframe]" Visual: Before/after or result shot SLIDE 2: "The Old Way (What I Used to Do)" • Step 1: [Inefficient action] • Step 2: [Manual work] • Step 3: [Frustration point] Visual: Messy/chaotic representation SLIDE 3: "The New Way (My Current System)" Overview of streamlined process Visual: Clean flowchart or numbered steps SLIDE 4-6: Deep Dive Each Step Step 1: [Tool/Action] — [Time saved] Step 2: [Tool/Action] — [Quality improved] Step 3: [Tool/Action] — [Result achieved] Visual: Screenshots of actual tools/workflows SLIDE 7: Results + CTA "This saves me [X hrs/week]" "Get my template: [link in bio]" "Follow for more systems"

Template 3: Myth-Busting Carousel

SLIDE 1: "Stop believing these [number] [topic] myths" Visual: Crossed-out myths or "Myth vs Reality" SLIDE 2: "Myth 1: [Common belief]" Reality: [Truth with evidence] Visual: Data point or expert quote SLIDE 3: "Myth 2: [Common belief]" Reality: [Truth with evidence] Visual: Screenshot or case study snippet SLIDE 4: "Myth 3: [Common belief]" Reality: [Truth with evidence] Visual: Before/after comparison SLIDE 5: "What to do instead" 3 actionable alternatives Visual: Checklist or framework SLIDE 6: CTA "Which myth surprised you? Comment 1, 2, or 3" "Follow for more myth-busting"

Reel/TikTok Scripts (15-60 seconds)

Script 1: Hook-Value-CTA (Educational)

[0-3s] HOOK (Visual + Audio sync) "You're probably [common mistake] — here's the fix:" [Show the mistake visually, then transition] [3-15s] VALUE (3 quick tips) Tip 1: "[Specific action]" [Demo on screen: 3s] Tip 2: "[Specific action]" [Demo on screen: 3s] Tip 3: "[Specific action]" [Demo on screen: 3s] [Text overlays for each tip] [15-20s] PROOF/RESULT "Here's what happened when I applied this:" [Show metric, screenshot, before/after] [20-25s] CTA "Save this so you don't forget" "Follow for more [topic] tips" [Point to caption/link in bio] [25-30s] LOOP BACK [Seamless transition to start for replay value]

Script 2: Storytelling (Personal/Brand)

[0-3s] HOOK "3 years ago I [struggle/failure]. Today [result]. Here's the journey:" [3-15s] THE STRUGGLE (Relatable pain) [Show old photos, screenshots, or reenactment] "I was [specific pain point]..." "Tried [failed solutions]..." "Felt [emotion]..." [15-25s] THE TURNING POINT "Then I discovered [method/tool/mindset]" [Show the discovery moment] "First attempt: [small win]" "Kept refining..." [25-40s] THE RESULT "Now: [specific measurable result]" [Show current reality] "This is possible for you too because [reason]" [40-50s] CTA "Comment 'START' for my free [resource]" "Link in bio for the full roadmap"

Script 3: Trend Adaptation (Viral Format)

[0-2s] TREND HOOK [Use trending audio/meme format] [Text: "POV: You're a [role] trying to [task]"] [2-10s] YOUR TWIST [Apply trend to your niche with humor/insight] [Show the relatable struggle specific to your audience] [10-20s] THE PAYOFF [Your expert solution or perspective] "Actually, the pro move is [your method]" [20-25s] CTA "Steal this workflow: [link]" "Follow for more [niche] reality checks"

Story Sequences (5-7 frames)

Sequence 1: Problem → Solution (Educational)

Frame 1: Poll/Question sticker "Struggling with [problem]?" [Yes / No] Frame 2: Agitate "Most people try [wrong solution]" [But it fails because...] Frame 3: Your Solution "Here's what actually works:" [1-sentence core solution] Frame 4: How-To (2-3 frames) Step 1: [Action] → [Visual] Step 2: [Action] → [Visual] Step 3: [Action] → [Visual] Frame 5: Result "Result: [Specific outcome]" [Screenshot/proof] Frame 6: CTA + Link "Want the full guide?" [Link sticker: "Get it free"]

Sequence 2: Behind-the-Scenes (Connection)

Frame 1: "A day in the life of [role]" [Photo/video of workspace/morning] Frame 2: "Current project: [name]" [Screenshot + 1-sentence excitement] Frame 3: "Challenge I'm solving today" [Problem + your approach] Frame 4: "Tool I can't live without" [Tool demo + why] Frame 5: "Lunch break / walk / reset" [Personal humanizing moment] Frame 6: "Wrap up: [win from today]" [Result + tomorrow's focus] Frame 7: "Questions? Ask below!" [Question sticker]

Caption Formulas

PAS (Problem-Agitate-Solution)

Problem: "Struggling with [specific pain point]? You're not alone." Agitate: "It's frustrating when [elaborate on consequences — wasted time, lost money, stress]. Most advice says [common bad advice] but that only makes it worse because [reason]." Solution: "Here's what actually works: [your method in 2-3 sentences]. I've seen [result] with [proof: clients, self, data]." CTA: "Want the step-by-step? Comment '[keyword]' and I'll DM you my free guide." Hashtags: [3-5 relevant tags]

AIDA (Attention-Interest-Desire-Action)

Attention: "[Shocking stat or bold statement] — [topic] is broken." Interest: "Here's why: [2-3 sentences explaining the gap/opportunity]. Most people don't realize [insight]." Desire: "Imagine [desired future state]. That's exactly what [method/product] delivers. [Social proof: client result, personal result, data]." Action: "I'm opening [spots/access] for [offer]. Link in bio to [next step]." Hashtags: [3-5 relevant tags]

Hook-Story-Offer (Direct Response)

Hook: "Stop [common action] if you want [desired result]." Story: "I learned this the hard way when [personal story: struggle, discovery, transformation]. The key insight was [one sentence]." Offer: "That's why I created [offer]. It gives you [benefit 1], [benefit 2], [benefit 3]. [Social proof]." CTA: "Only [number] spots at [price/bonus]. Link in bio — '[anchor text]'." Hashtags: [3-5 relevant tags]

💡 Pro Tip

Create a "swipe file" — save high-performing posts from competitors and creators you admire. Adapt their structures, don't copy content. Test one formula for 2 weeks, track results, then iterate.
