Official Google Tools

  • Google Keyword Planner
    Free keyword research: search volume, competition, CPC estimates, forecasts, seasonal trends. Requires Google Ads account.
    Keyword Planner →
    FreeOfficialEssential
  • Google Trends
    Search interest over time, related topics/queries, geographic interest, real-time trends. Great for seasonality and content planning.
    trends.google.com →
    FreeTrendsSeasonality
  • Google Ads Editor
    Desktop app for bulk editing, offline work, copy/paste across accounts, advanced search/replace. Essential for scale.
    Google Ads Editor →
    FreeBulk EditOffline
  • Google Tag Manager (GTM)
    Tag management without code changes. Deploy Google Ads, GA4, conversion pixels, custom events. Version control, preview mode.
    tagmanager.google.com →
    FreeTag ManagementEssential
  • Google Merchant Center
    Product feed management for Shopping ads. Feed rules, diagnostics, performance, promotions, local inventory.
    merchants.google.com →
    ShoppingFeedFree
  • Looker Studio (Data Studio)
    Free dashboards. Connect Google Ads, GA4, Sheets, BigQuery, SQL. Automated client reporting. Templates available.
    lookerstudio.google.com →
    FreeDashboardsAutomation
