Case Study 1: E-Commerce Funnel — "StyleBox"

Fashion subscription: Fixed 3 funnel leaks, revenue +40% in 60 days

$39/mo subscription, 45% churn, 1.2% landing page CVR.

Leak 1: High Bounce (68%)

Hotjar showed hero image mismatched ad messaging. Fixed: Aligned headline with ad copy. Bounce dropped to 48%.

Leak 2: Cart Abandonment (82%)

Session recordings revealed unexpected shipping cost at checkout. Fixed: Showed free shipping on product page. Abandonment dropped to 61%.

Leak 3: Trial-to-Paid (12%)

Cohort analysis: users completing style quiz had 45% conversion. Fixed: Forced quiz on Day 1. Trial-to-paid jumped to 28%.

System

GA4 + Hotjar + Mixpanel. Weekly funnel review. Monthly cohort analysis. 2 A/B tests per month. Tool cost: $0 (free tiers).

Key Insight

Biggest funnel leaks are invisible without analytics. Fix the three biggest leaks first — don't optimize micro-details yet.

Case Study 2: SaaS Onboarding — "TaskFlow"

Project management tool: Activation rate 18% to 52%, retention doubled

Free trial, $29/mo paid. Data showed users creating first project within 24h had 4x higher retention.

North Star Discovery

Amplitude cohorts: users creating a project within 24h had 4x 30-day retention. This became the activation event to optimize for.

Onboarding Redesign

Replaced 12-step tour with 3-step guided setup. Added progress bar. Pre-populated sample project. Time-to-value dropped from 48h to 2h.

Trigger-Based Emails

Amplitude events triggered emails: no project after 6h = nudge. Project created = celebration + next step. 7 days inactive = re-engagement.

Results After 90 Days

Activation: 18% to 52%. 30-day retention: 22% to 44%. MRR grew 65%. Support tickets dropped 35% because onboarding answered questions proactively.

Key Insight

Find your activation event (the one action that predicts retention) and optimize everything toward getting users to that moment as fast as possible.

Case Study 3: Content Funnel — "GrowthKit"

SaaS content marketing: Blog to free tool to paid conversion, 8% visitor-to-trial

Developer tools SaaS. Used content as top-of-funnel, free tool as mid-funnel, trial as bottom-funnel.

Top: SEO Content

50 blog posts targeting developer pain points. Each post links to free tool. Organic traffic: 80K/mo. Bounce rate: 38%. Avg 2.8 pages/session.

Mid: Free Tool

Free API testing tool. Captures email on use. 40% of blog visitors try free tool. 35% of free tool users sign up for trial. Lead-to-trial: 8%.

Bottom: Email Nurture

7-day drip after free tool signup. Day 1: Quick win. Day 3: Advanced feature. Day 5: Case study. Day 7: Trial offer with deadline. 15% conversion.

Attribution & Optimization

GA4 + PostHog. Content attribution: blog post → free tool → trial → paid. Optimized: free tool onboarding (biggest drop-off). CAC from content: $42 vs $180 from paid ads.

Key Insight

Content funnels work best with a free tool in the middle. It gives visitors a reason to share their email and experience your product before paying. The free tool is your best conversion lever.

Case Study 4: Paid Acquisition — "FitTrack"

Fitness app: CAC from $85 to $38 via funnel analytics and creative testing

$9.99/mo subscription. Running Facebook + Google ads. High CAC was killing unit economics.

Funnel Analysis

GA4 funnel: Ad click → LP visit (65%) → Account creation (22%) → Trial start (40%) → Paid (12%). Biggest leak: LP to account creation (78% drop-off).

Landing Page Fix

A/B tested 5 LP variants. Winner: Social proof above fold, 1-sentence value prop, single CTA (Google sign-in). CVR improved from 22% to 38%.

Creative Testing

Tested 30 ad creatives. Winners: UGC testimonials (3x CTR), before/after results (2.5x), founder story (2x). Paused all generic stock-image ads.

Bid Strategy Optimization

Switched from Max Clicks to Target CPA ($40). Added offline conversion import (trial-to-paid event). Algorithm learned to find high-value users. CPA dropped from $85 to $38.

Key Insight

Paid acquisition optimization is 80% funnel, 20% ads. Fix your landing page and onboarding before spending more on ads. A 2x improvement in LP conversion is worth more than a 2x budget increase.

Pro Tip

All four cases share: (1) They measured before optimizing, (2) They found the biggest leak and fixed it first, (3) They used free analytics tools, (4) They tested hypotheses with data, not opinions. Analytics without action is just reporting.
