Growth Dashboard Layout

Weekly Growth Dashboard Structure

ROW 1: NORTH STAR METRICS ├── MRR / Revenue ($ and % change WoW) ├── Active Users (DAU/WAU/MAU) ├── Conversion Rate (visitor to customer) └── NPS Score ROW 2: ACQUISITION ├── Traffic by Source (organic, paid, direct, referral) ├── New Users (and % change) ├── CAC by Channel └── Top Landing Pages + CVR ROW 3: ACTIVATION ├── Activation Rate (% completing key action) ├── Time to Value (avg time to first success) ├── Onboarding Completion Rate └── Free-to-Paid Conversion Rate ROW 4: RETENTION ├── Day 1 / Day 7 / Day 30 Retention ├── Churn Rate (monthly) ├── Cohort Retention Curves (last 4 cohorts) └── NPS Trend (last 4 weeks) ROW 5: REVENUE ├── LTV by Cohort ├── ARPU (Avg Revenue Per User) ├── Expansion Revenue % └── Refund Rate

Pro Tip

These templates work best in Google Sheets (free, collaborative) or Looker Studio (free, visual). Start with the Weekly Growth Report — it forces you to look at data every week. The dashboard and funnel templates become natural additions.
