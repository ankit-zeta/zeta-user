ConvertKit, Gumroad, Stripe, Teespring, Shopify, and launch tools

Email Marketing

ConvertKit

Built for creators. Landing pages, email sequences, automation, commerce. Best for digital products and courses.

Free up to 1K subs / $29/mo Creator

Mailchimp

Popular all-in-one. Email, landing pages, social, ads. Good for beginners. Less creator-focused than ConvertKit.

Free up to 500 contacts / $13/mo Essentials

Beehiiv

Newsletter-first platform. Great for building and monetizing newsletters. Ad network built in.

Free up to 2.5K subs / $39/mo Scale

Substack

Simple newsletter platform. Free to use, 10% cut of paid subscriptions. Zero setup required.

Free (10% of paid subs)

Course & Product Platforms

Teachable

Course hosting platform. Video hosting, quizzes, certificates, payments. All-in-one for courses.

$39/mo Basic / $99/mo Pro

Gumroad

Sell anything: ebooks, courses, templates, membership. Simple setup. Built-in audience discovery.

10% transaction fee

Podia

All-in-one: courses, downloads, webinars, memberships. No transaction fees. Simple pricing.

$39/mo Mover / $89/mo Shaker

Lemon Squeezy

Sell digital products. Global tax handling, license keys, affiliate program. Developer-friendly.

5% + 50c per transaction

Payment Processing

Stripe

Industry standard. Online payments, subscriptions, invoicing. Integrates with everything. Best for SaaS.

2.9% + 30c per transaction

PayPal

Widely trusted. Buyer protection. Good for physical products and international sales.

2.99% + 49c per transaction

LemonSqueezy

Handles global tax, compliance, payments. Great for digital products. Merchant of record.

5% + 50c per transaction

Paddle

Merchant of record for SaaS. Handles tax, compliance, payments. Best for subscription software.

5% + 50c per transaction

Landing Page Builders

Carrd

One-page websites. Beautiful templates. Fast setup. Perfect for landing pages and MVPs.

Free / $19/yr Pro

Typedream

Notion-like page builder. Clean, fast, easy. Good for simple landing pages and docs.

Free / $12/mo Pro

Framer

Design-first website builder. Beautiful animations. More advanced than Carrd.

Free / $15/mo Mini

Webflow

Professional website builder. Full design control. Steeper learning curve, most powerful.

Free / $14/mo Basic

Pro Tip

Start with the simplest tools: Gumroad for selling, ConvertKit for email, Carrd for landing page. Total cost: under $20/mo. Upgrade only after you have paying customers. The best tool is the one you'll actually use — don't spend weeks setting up tools before launching.
