Capstone Project Checklist

Validate, build, launch, optimize, and scale phase checklists

Phase 1: Validate (Week 1-2)

Before You Build Anything

Idea Clarity: Write one sentence describing your product. If you can't, the idea isn't clear enough.
Audience Defined: Who specifically is this for? "Everyone" is not an answer. One specific audience.
Problem Validated: Talk to 10 people in your target audience. 7+ should say "I'd pay for this."
Competitor Research: Check if similar products exist. If yes: proof of demand. If no: why not?
Landing Page: Create simple page describing your product. Drive 100 visitors. Target: 5%+ email signups.
Pre-Sell: Offer before building. Collect payment (even $1). Target: 10+ pre-sales. If not, pivot.
MVP Scope Defined: What's the simplest version? What's excluded? Write it down. Don't build everything.

Phase 2: Build (Week 3-4)

Create Your MVP

Core Content: Create the essential content/deliverable. Don't perfectionism — get it 80% right.
Tools Setup: Hosting, payment processing, email tool. Keep it simple. Free or cheap tools.
Landing Page (Final): Professional page with benefits, social proof, clear CTA, pricing.
Payment System: Stripe or PayPal. Test purchase flow. Make it easy to buy (fewer clicks = better).
Email Sequence: Welcome email, delivery email, follow-up sequence. Automate everything.
Test Purchase: Buy your own product. Fix any issues. Ask a friend to test too.

Phase 3: Launch (Week 5-8)

Go Live and Get Customers

Soft Launch: Share with 20-50 people first. Fix issues. Get testimonials. Then go bigger.
Email Blast: Announce to your full list. Include social proof, clear CTA, urgency (if applicable).
Social Media: Post on all channels. LinkedIn, Twitter, Facebook groups. Share value, not just pitch.
Paid Ads (Optional): $200-500 test budget. Facebook/Google. Target broad, narrow based on data.
Outreach: DM 50 people in your network. Ask for shares. Offer affiliate commission (20-30%).
Collect Feedback: Survey every buyer. Ask: What almost stopped you? What's missing? What surprised you?
Testimonials: Ask happy customers for testimonials. Add to landing page immediately.

Phase 4: Optimize (Week 9-10)

Improve Based on Data

Analyze Data: Traffic sources, conversion rates, email open rates, refund rate. Know your numbers.
Fix Leaks: Where are people dropping off? Landing page? Checkout? Follow-up? Fix the biggest leak first.
A/B Test: Headlines, pricing, CTAs. One change at a time. Let it run for 100+ conversions.
Add Social Proof: More testimonials, case studies, user counts. Every piece reduces purchase anxiety.
Email Optimization: Improve open rates (subject lines), click rates (content), conversion (offers).

Phase 5: Scale (Week 11-13)

Grow What's Working

Double Down: Put more budget into highest-performing channel. If Facebook ads work, increase spend.
Systemize: Document everything. Create SOPs. If you got hit by a bus, could someone else run this?
Automate: Email sequences, reporting, customer onboarding. Remove yourself from repetitive tasks.
Plan Next Phase: What's the next product/feature? Upsell existing customers? New audience? Document plan.
Celebrate: You launched a real product. Most people never do. Acknowledge the win.

Pro Tip

The checklist order is critical: validate before building, build before launching, launch before optimizing, optimize before scaling. Skipping validation is the #1 reason products fail. Spend 2 weeks validating — it saves 2 months of building something nobody wants.
