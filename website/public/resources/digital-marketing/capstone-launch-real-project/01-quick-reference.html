90-Day Launch Timeline

Days 1-14: Foundation

Idea validation, audience research, MVP scope, landing page, offer creation

Days 15-30: Build

MVP creation, content setup, ad campaigns, email sequences, launch assets

Days 31-60: Launch

Go live, paid traffic, content marketing, outreach, collect feedback, iterate

Days 61-75: Optimize

Analyze data, optimize funnel, A/B test, improve conversion, reduce costs

Days 76-90: Scale

Double down on what works, systemize, document, plan next phase, celebrate

MVP Framework

What's an MVP?

Minimum Viable Product: the simplest version that solves one problem for one audience. NOT a full product. It's a test. Goal: validate demand before investing heavily.

MVP by Type

Course: 3-5 lessons, one module
Ebook: 20-30 pages, focused topic
Service: One offering, defined scope
Tool: Core feature only
SaaS: One use case, basic UI

MVP Timeline

Week 1: Define problem + audience
Week 2: Create core content
Week 3: Build landing page + payment
Week 4: Soft launch to 50 people
Week 5: Collect feedback, iterate

MVP Budget

Domain: $12/year
Hosting: $5-20/month
Email: Free (ConvertKit)
Ads: $200-500 test budget
Total: Under $600 to validate

Launch Strategy Options

Soft Launch

Quiet release to small audience
Test everything, fix bugs
Gather testimonials
Low risk, low pressure
Best for: first-time creators

Hard Launch

Big push with deadline/scarcity
Email list + paid ads + PR
Limited-time pricing
High energy, high conversion
Best for: proven concepts

Beta Launch

Offer to 20-50 people at discount
Collect feedback during
Build testimonials
Iterate based on usage
Best for: products needing polish

Evergreen Launch

Always available, no urgency
Steady traffic via SEO/ads
Lower conversion rates
Passive income model
Best for: proven, mature products

Pro Tip

The capstone project is NOT about building a perfect product. It's about launching something real, learning from the market, and iterating. Done is better than perfect. Launch in 30 days, improve in the next 60. Speed of execution is your unfair advantage.
