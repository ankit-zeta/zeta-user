Pricing Formulas

Cost-Plus Pricing
(Cost to Create + Desired Profit) ÷ Units = Minimum Price
Value-Based Pricing
Customer's Revenue Increase × Your % (10-30%) = Price
Competitor-Based Pricing
Competitor Average ± Differentiation Adjustment = Price
Break-Even Point
Total Launch Costs ÷ (Price - Variable Cost per Unit) = Units to Break Even
Revenue Target
Monthly Revenue Goal ÷ Average Order Value = Customers Needed

Pro Tip

Focus on ONE growth lever at a time. Trying to improve traffic, conversion, AOV, and retention simultaneously spreads you thin. Pick the biggest bottleneck (usually traffic or conversion), fix it, then move to the next. One lever at a time compounds faster.
