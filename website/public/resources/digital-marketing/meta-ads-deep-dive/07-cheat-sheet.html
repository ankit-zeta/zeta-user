Scaling Cheat Sheet

When to scale, horizontal vs vertical scaling, budget increase rules, audience expansion, creative refresh, winning ad indicators

Scaling Readiness Checklist

✅ Stable Performance (7-14 days)

CPA/ROAS within 10% of target
Daily spend consistent (no wild swings)
Frequency < 2.5
No "Learning Limited" warnings

✅ Sufficient Volume

> 50 conversions/week per ad set
> $500+ weekly spend per ad set
Statistical significance achieved
Creative fatigue not present

✅ Tracking Verified

CAPI + Pixel both firing
Event Match Quality > 8/10
UTM data clean in GA4
Attribution window appropriate

✅ Creative Pipeline Ready

3+ new concepts in production
Winning creative identified
UGC/creator content scheduled
Refresh calendar blocked

Horizontal Scaling (Expand Reach)

New Audiences

• Lookalike 2-3% → 5-10%
• New interest clusters (test 5 at a time)
• Advantage+ with new suggestions
• Broad targeting (no interests, let AI find)
• International expansion (localize creative)

New Placements

• Add Reels if not using
• Add Stories if Feed only
• Test Audience Network (monitor quality)
• Messenger/Inbox for lead gen
• Facebook Reels (separate from IG)

New Creatives

• New hooks (test 5 per concept)
• New formats (static → video, carousel → collection)
• UGC/creator variations
• Localized creative for new geos
• Seasonal/holiday variants

New Campaigns

• Separate campaign per major audience
• Separate campaign per product line
• Separate campaign per funnel stage
• Advantage+ Shopping Campaigns (ASC)
• Advantage+ Catalog Ads

Vertical Scaling (Increase Budget)

20% Rule (Daily Budget)

Increase daily budget ≤ 20% every 3-4 days
Example: $100 → $120 → $144 → $173
Monitor CPA for 48h after each increase
If CPA degrades > 15%: roll back

Lifetime Budget Scaling

Extend end date + add budget proportionally
Or: Duplicate campaign with higher lifetime budget
Better for fixed-duration promotions
Avoids daily volatility

Bid Strategy Adjustments

Cost Cap → increase cap 10-15%
ROAS Goal → lower target slightly (3.0 → 2.8)
Highest Volume → just increase budget
Bid Cap → raise cap incrementally

When NOT to Vertical Scale

• Frequency > 3
• CPA trending up > 7 days
• Creative fatigue signals
• "Learning Limited" status
• Attribution issues (iOS 14+)

Winning Ad Indicators (Scale These)

Primary Signals

• ROAS > target (consistent 7+ days)
• CPA < target (consistent 7+ days)
• High CTR (> 1.5% Feed, > 0.8% Stories)
• High Hook Rate (> 30% 3s video play)
• High Hold Rate (> 25% ThruPlay)

Secondary Signals

• Low CPM (efficient reach)
• High Engagement Rate (> 3%)
• High Save/Share ratio (viral potential)
• Positive comments/sentiment
• High landing page CVR (> 3%)

Creative Attributes of Winners

• Strong hook in first 1-2 seconds
• Clear value prop + social proof
• Native format (looks organic)
• Authentic / UGC style
• Single focused message

Scale Winners By

• Duplicating to new ad sets/campaigns
• Increasing budget on winning ad set
• Creating variations (new hook, new CTA)
• Whitelisting (if creator content)
• Adding to Dynamic Creative

Creative Refresh Schedule

Weekly

• Review creative breakdowns
• Pause ads with CPA > 2x target
• Launch 1-2 new ad variations
• Check frequency per ad

Bi-Weekly

• Full creative audit
• Identify winning concepts
• Plan 3-5 new concepts
• Brief creative team

Monthly

• Major creative refresh (50%+ new)
• Seasonal/trend alignment
• Competitive creative audit
• UGC/creator collabs scheduled

Quarterly

• Complete creative overhaul
• New brand campaigns
• Platform feature adoption
• Strategy review with stakeholders

Troubleshooting Scale Issues

CPM Rising

• Expand audience (horizontal)
• Refresh creative (lower relevance cost)
• Check audience overlap
• Test Advantage+ Placements
• Reduce bid cap (if using)

Frequency Too High

• Expand audience size
• Add exclusion (recent converters)
• Frequency cap (if available)
• Rotate creative more aggressively
• Reduce daily budget temporarily

ROAS Dropping

• Check attribution (iOS, CAPI)
• Audit landing page (speed, offer)
• Test new offers/bundles
• Segment by placement (cut losers)
• Verify pixel/event firing

Audience Saturation

• First-time impression ratio < 40%
• Expand to broader lookalikes
• Test broad targeting (no interests)
• New geos / languages
• New product angles

💡 Pro Tip

Scale horizontally FIRST (new audiences, creatives, campaigns). Vertical scaling hits diminishing returns fast. Horizontal builds a diversified portfolio that's resilient to creative fatigue and audience saturation.
