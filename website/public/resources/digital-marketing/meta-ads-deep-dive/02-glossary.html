Meta Ads Glossary

Essential terminology for Facebook, Instagram, and Meta advertising platform

Core Metrics

CPM
Cost Per Mille (thousand impressions). Standard pricing for awareness campaigns. CPM = (Spend ÷ Impressions) × 1000
CPC
Cost Per Click. Price paid for each link click. CPC = Spend ÷ Link Clicks. Varies by objective, audience, creative.
CTR
Click-Through Rate. (Link Clicks ÷ Impressions) × 100. Measures ad relevance and creative strength. Benchmark: 1-2% (Feed), 0.5-1% (Stories).
ROAS
Return on Ad Spend. Revenue ÷ Ad Spend. Expressed as ratio (3.0 = 3:1) or percentage (300%). Target varies by margin.
CPA / CPL
Cost Per Acquisition / Cost Per Lead. Spend ÷ Conversions (or Leads). Primary efficiency metric for conversion campaigns.
Frequency
Average times each person saw your ad. Impressions ÷ Reach. >3 risks ad fatigue; >5 usually needs creative refresh.
Relevance Score (Deprecated)
Replaced by Quality Ranking, Engagement Rate Ranking, Conversion Rate Ranking (three diagnostic metrics).
Quality Ranking
Perceived quality vs competing ads for same audience. Below Average / Average / Above Average.

Tracking & Measurement

Meta Pixel
JavaScript code on website tracking visitor actions (page views, purchases, leads). Enables conversion optimization, retargeting, attribution.
Conversions API (CAPI)
Server-side tracking sending events directly to Meta. More reliable than Pixel (browsers block cookies). Use both for maximum coverage.
Standard Events
Predefined Pixel events: Purchase, Lead, CompleteRegistration, AddToCart, InitiateCheckout, Contact, Subscribe, etc.
Custom Events
User-defined events for unique actions. Use sparingly; standard events preferred for optimization.
Event Match Quality
Score (0-10) showing how well customer data matches Meta profiles. Higher = better attribution. Improve with email, phone, name, location.
Attribution Window
Timeframe for crediting conversions: 1-day click, 7-day click, 1-day view, 7-day click + 1-day view (default).
Aggregated Event Measurement (AEM)
iOS 14+ privacy framework. Limits to 8 conversion events per domain. Prioritize events in Events Manager.

Audience Terms

Custom Audience
Audience from your data: website visitors, customer lists, app users, engagement, offline events. Min 100 people to create.
Lookalike Audience
New audience similar to source Custom Audience. Percentage = similarity vs reach tradeoff. 1% = most similar.
Advantage+ Audience
AI-powered targeting. Provide suggestions (interests, demographics), Meta finds converters. Replaces detailed targeting expansion.
Detailed Targeting
Manual interest/behavior/demographic selection. Being phased out in favor of Advantage+. Still works for specific niches.
Retargeting / Remarketing
Showing ads to people who previously interacted. Source: website visitors, video watchers, engagers, customer list.
Exclusion Audience
Audience to EXCLUDE from targeting (e.g., existing customers, employees, converters). Prevents waste and annoyance.
Audience Overlap
When same people in multiple ad sets. Causes auction competition, higher costs. Check in Audience Overlap tool.
Audience Saturation
When most people in audience have seen ads. First-time impression ratio drops. Signal to expand or refresh.

Creative & Testing

Dynamic Creative
Upload multiple images, headlines, texts, CTAs → Meta auto-combines best permutations. Good for testing many combos.
Creative Testing
Controlled A/B test: identical ad sets, different creatives. Statistical significance reported. Use for major creative tests.
Ad Fatigue / Creative Fatigue
Performance decline from overexposure. Signs: rising CPM, falling CTR/ROAS, frequency >3. Fix: new creative, expand audience, pause.
Hook
First 3 seconds of video / first line of copy. Stops scroll. Test multiple hooks per creative concept.
UGC (User-Generated Content)
Content from real customers/creators. Higher trust, lower production cost. Source: reviews, ambassador programs, contests.
Whitelisting / Partnership Ads
Run ads from creator's handle (not brand). Higher trust, access to creator's audience. Set up in Meta Business Suite.
Spark Ads (TikTok term, similar concept)
Boost organic post as ad. On Meta: "Boost Post" or Partnership Ads with creator permission.

Campaign Management

CBO (Campaign Budget Optimization)
Budget set at campaign level. Meta allocates to best-performing ad sets in real-time. Default for new campaigns.
ABO (Ad Set Budget Optimization)
Budget set at ad set level. Manual control. Disable CBO to use. Good for testing specific audiences.
Learning Phase
Period after launch/edit where Meta explores (50 optimization events needed). Performance unstable. Avoid edits during.
Learning Limited
Ad set unlikely to exit learning (too small audience/budget). May not optimize well. Fix: expand audience, increase budget, consolidate.
Automated Rules
If/then rules for auto-pause, budget changes, notifications. E.g., "If CPA > $50 for 3 days → pause ad set."
Breakdown Reports
Segment performance by: Age, Gender, Placement, Device, Region, Time, Creative. Find winning segments.
Ad Scheduling
Run ads only specific days/hours. Lifetime budget only. Use for B2B (business hours), time-sensitive offers.
Bid Multipliers
Adjust bids for specific placements/devices. E.g., +20% for Instagram Stories, -30% for Audience Network.
