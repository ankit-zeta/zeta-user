Campaign Structure Template

Standard 3-Tier Setup
CAMPAIGN: [Campaign Name] ├── Objective: Sales / Leads / Traffic ├── Budget: ₹____/day (Campaign Budget Optimization: ON) │ ├── AD SET 1: Cold - Broad │ ├── Audience: Broad (no interests, let Meta optimize) │ ├── Placements: Advantage+ (automatic) │ ├── Budget: 60% of total │ └── Ads: 3-5 creatives (different formats) │ ├── AD SET 2: Warm - Retargeting │ ├── Audience: Website visitors (30 days) + Engaged (60 days) │ ├── Placements: Feed + Stories only │ ├── Budget: 25% of total │ └── Ads: 2-3 creatives (social proof, urgency) │ └── AD SET 3: Hot - Cart Abandoners ├── Audience: Added to cart but didn't purchase (14 days) ├── Placements: Feed + Stories + Reels ├── Budget: 15% of total └── Ads: 1-2 creatives (offer, reminder)

Ad Copy Formulas

PAS Formula (Problem-Agitate-Solution)
Hook: "Still struggling with [PROBLEM]?" Agitate: "Most people waste [TIME/MONEY] trying [COMMON MISTAKE]. It's frustrating when [PAIN POINT]." Solution: "[PRODUCT] solves this by [BENEFIT]. [SOCIAL PROOF]." CTA: "[ACTION] now — [URGENCY]."
