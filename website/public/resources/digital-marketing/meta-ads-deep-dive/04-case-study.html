Real-world Meta Ads campaigns across industries with budgets, strategies, and results

Case Study 1: E-commerce Fashion Brand

StyleHub IndiaE-commerce
Challenge: New D2C fashion brand. No audience. ₹20,000 monthly budget. Needed profitable ROAS within 30 days.
Strategy: Broad targeting (18-35, fashion interests) → 3 ad creatives (lifestyle, product flat-lay, UGC testimonial) → Advantage+ Shopping Campaign → Retargeting (viewed/cart abandoned). Budget split: 60% prospecting, 40% retargeting.
Results: Month 1: ROAS 1.8x (loss). Month 2: ROAS 3.2x (profitable). Month 3: ROAS 4.7x. Total revenue: ₹1.8L from ₹60K ad spend. CPA dropped from ₹450 to ₹180.
Key Insight: Broad targeting + good creative beats detailed targeting every time. Let Meta's algorithm find buyers.

Case Study 2: SaaS Lead Generation

TaskFlow SaaSSaaS
Challenge: B2B project management tool. Needed qualified trial signups. ₹30,000 monthly budget. Target: CAC under ₹500.
Strategy: LinkedIn-style targeting on Meta (job titles: managers, founders). Lead magnet: "Free Project Management Template." Lead form → email sequence → trial. Lookalike audience from existing users.
Results: 340 trial signups in 60 days. CAC: ₹380. 12% trial-to-paid conversion. 41 paying customers. LTV: ₹15,000. ROI: 39x on ad spend.
Key Insight: Lead magnets with immediate value convert 3x better than "Start Free Trial" CTAs for B2B.

Case Study 3: Local Restaurant Chain

Spice GardenLocal Business
Challenge: 3-outlet restaurant. Foot traffic declining. ₹15,000 monthly budget. Wanted table reservations and takeaway orders.
Strategy: Location targeting (5km radius per outlet). Carousel ads with menu items. Offer: "15% off first order via WhatsApp." Retargeting: People who engaged but didn't order. Day-parting: Ads only during meal times (11am-2pm, 6pm-10pm).
Results: 280 WhatsApp orders/month. Average order value: ₹650. Revenue: ₹1.82L/month from ₹15K ad spend. ROAS: 12x. Foot traffic up 35%.
Key Insight: Local businesses benefit enormously from day-parting and radius targeting. Don't waste budget showing ads at 3am.

Case Study 4: Online Course Launch

Digital Skills AcademyEducation
Challenge: Launching ₹4,999 digital marketing course. No audience. Needed 100 enrollments in 14-day launch window. Budget: ₹50,000 total.
Strategy: Phase 1 (Days 1-7): Awareness — Video views campaign with free masterclass. Phase 2 (Days 8-14): Conversion — Retarget video viewers + lookalike with enrollment offer. Urgency: "Early bird price ends Sunday." Social proof: Live enrollment counter.
Results: 127 enrollments. Revenue: ₹6.35L. Ad spend: ₹48,000. ROAS: 13.2x. CPA: ₹378. Email list grew by 2,400 subscribers from masterclass signups.
Key Insight: Two-phase launches (awareness → conversion) outperform single-phase conversion campaigns by 2-3x for high-ticket items.

💡 Pro Tip

Study these patterns, not the specific numbers. Your industry, audience, and product will have different benchmarks. The strategy frameworks (broad targeting, retargeting, urgency, social proof) transfer across all verticals.
