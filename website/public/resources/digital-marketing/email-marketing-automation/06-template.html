Welcome Email Template

Welcome Email (Email 1 of Welcome Sequence)

Subject: Welcome! Here's your [Lead Magnet Name] Preview: Download your [resource] inside + what to expect Hi {{first_name}}, Welcome to [Brand Name]! I'm [Your Name], and I'm thrilled you're here. Here's your [Lead Magnet Name]: [DOWNLOAD BUTTON] Here's what you'll get from being on this list: - [Benefit 1: e.g. Weekly growth tactics] - [Benefit 2: e.g. Exclusive templates] - [Benefit 3: e.g. Behind-the-scenes insights] One quick ask: Hit reply and tell me: 1. What's your biggest challenge with [topic]? 2. What would you most like to learn? I read every reply (yes, really). Talk soon, [Your Name] [Brand Name] P.S. Keep an eye on your inbox. Tomorrow I'm sharing [teaser of next email value].

Cart Abandonment Templates

Cart Abandonment Email 1 (1 hour delay)

Subject: You left something in your cart Preview: Your [Product Name] is still waiting Hi {{first_name}}, I noticed you were checking out [Product Name] but didn't complete your order. No worries — I saved your cart: [PRODUCT IMAGE] [PRODUCT NAME] [PRICE] [COMPLETE YOUR ORDER BUTTON] If you had any questions, just reply to this email. I'm happy to help. [Your Name]

Cart Abandonment Email 2 (24 hours)

Subject: Still thinking about [Product Name]? Preview: Here's what other customers are saying Hi {{first_name}}, Your [Product Name] is still in your cart. Here's what [Customer Name] said after buying: "[Testimonial quote about the product]" — [Customer Name], [Title/Role] [Product Name] has helped [number] customers [achieve result]. [COMPLETE YOUR ORDER BUTTON] [Your Name]

Cart Abandonment Email 3 (72 hours)

Subject: 10% off to complete your order Preview: Use code SAVE10 — expires in 48 hours Hi {{first_name}}, Your [Product Name] is still waiting for you. To help you decide, here's 10% off: Code: SAVE10 Discount: 10% off your order Expires: 48 hours [COMPLETE ORDER WITH 10% OFF BUTTON] This is the last email about your cart. After this, your cart will expire. [Your Name]

Newsletter Template

Weekly Newsletter Structure

Subject: [Topic]: [Specific Benefit] (Issue #47) Preview: [One compelling sentence about this issue] Hi {{first_name}}, [Personal intro — 1-2 sentences about your week or a quick observation] --- THIS WEEK'S TACTIC: [Tactic Name] [2-3 paragraphs explaining the tactic with specific, actionable details] Key takeaway: [One sentence summary] --- TOOL OF THE WEEK: [Tool Name] [What it does, why it's useful, who it's for] [Try Tool Name →] (affiliate link if applicable) --- QUICK WINS: • [Tip 1 — one sentence] • [Tip 2 — one sentence] • [Tip 3 — one sentence] --- QUESTION OF THE WEEK: What's your biggest challenge with [topic]? Hit reply and tell me — I'll feature the best answers next week. Until next week, [Your Name] P.S. If you found this useful, forward this email to a friend who'd benefit.

Launch Announcement Template

Product Launch Email

Subject: It's live! [Product Name] is here Preview: [Key benefit] — early bird pricing for 48 hours Hi {{first_name}}, After [X months] of building, [Product Name] is officially live. What it does: [One sentence value proposition] Why I built it: [1-2 sentences about the problem] What's included: • [Feature/Benefit 1] • [Feature/Benefit 2] • [Feature/Benefit 3] Early bird pricing: $[X] (regular $[Y]) Expires: [Date/Time] [GET EARLY BIRD PRICE →] This early bird price won't last. After [deadline], it goes to regular pricing. [Your Name] P.S. [Social proof: "X people already signed up" or testimonial]

Pro Tip

These templates are starting points — customize them with your voice and brand. The best emails feel personal, not corporate. Use first names, tell stories, and write like you're emailing a friend. That's what gets opens and clicks.
