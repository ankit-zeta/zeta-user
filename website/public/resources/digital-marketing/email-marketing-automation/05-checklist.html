Email Marketing Checklist

Tool setup, list building, sequence creation, deliverability, testing, and optimization checklists

Setup: Tool & Configuration

  • Email platform selected and configured
    ConvertKit, Mailchimp, or Beehiiv. Account settings, business info, sender name, reply-to email.
  • Custom sending domain configured
    DNS records added (SPF, DKIM, DMARC). Domain verified. Not using free ESP domain.
  • Sender reputation established
    Warm-up period complete (2-4 weeks). Initial sends to engaged contacts only. Monitor sender score.
  • Consent and compliance
    Double opt-in enabled (if required). Privacy policy updated. Unsubscribe link in every email. CAN-SPAM/GDPR compliant.
  • Analytics and tracking
    UTM parameters on all links. GA4 goals for email conversions. Revenue tracking connected.

Build: Sequences & Segments

  • Lead magnet created and landing page live
    Valuable resource that solves a specific problem. Landing page with clear CTA. Tested and converting.
  • Welcome sequence built (5-7 emails)
    Delivers lead magnet, sets expectations, builds trust, introduces brand. Automation triggered on signup.
  • Cart/abandon sequence built (if e-commerce)
    3-4 emails over 72 hours. Product images, social proof, incentive on last email. 1-24-72 hour cadence.
  • Post-purchase sequence built
    Thank you, onboarding, usage tips, feedback request, review request. Reduces refunds and churn.
  • Segments defined and populated
    At minimum: engaged, unengaged, buyers, non-buyers. Tags applied based on behavior.

Optimize: Tests & Deliverability

  • Subject line A/B tests running
    Test: length, personalization, curiosity, urgency. Minimum 1,000 sends per variation. 95%+ confidence.
  • Unengaged subscribers cleaned
    Remove: no opens in 90+ days. Re-engagement sequence before removal. List hygiene monthly.
  • Send time optimization tested
    Test different days and times. Segment by timezone. Use platform's send-time optimization if available.
  • Deliverability monitored
    Bounce rate < 2%. Spam complaint rate < 0.1%. Inbox placement tested (mail-tester.com). Monthly check.
  • Revenue per email tracked
    Email-attributed revenue documented. Compare: automation vs broadcast. Optimize highest-revenue sequences first.

Pro Tip

Complete the Setup section before sending anything. Then Build your welcome sequence (most important). Then Build cart abandonment (if e-commerce). Then Optimize. Most people try to do everything at once and end up with poor deliverability and low engagement.
