List Building Strategies

Lead Magnets (High Conversion)

Checklists, templates, cheat sheets
Ebooks, guides, toolkits
Free tools, calculators
Video courses, webinars
Discount codes (e-commerce)
Target: 20-40% landing page CVR

Content Upgrades

Blog post + related resource
"Download the template from this post"
"Get the checklist version"
"Access the spreadsheet"
Target: 5-15% of blog readers
Works: Within blog posts, exit popups

Opt-in Placement

Header bar (always visible)
Exit-intent popup (last chance)
In-content forms (within blog)
Footer signup (persistent)
Slide-in (scroll-triggered)
Homepage hero section

Referral & Viral Growth

"Share with a friend, get X"
Refer 3 friends = bonus
Social sharing incentives
Referral program with rewards
Target: K-factor > 0.5
Best: After user achieves value

Deliverability Quick Guide

Authentication (Set Up Once)

SPF: Authorizes your sending servers
DKIM: Signs emails to verify identity
DMARC: Policy for failed authentication
Custom domain: Not free domain emails
Verify: Use mail-tester.com to check

Reputation Management

Clean list monthly (remove bounces)
Segment by engagement (suppress cold)
Warm new IPs (start with engaged)
Monitor sender score (above 90)
Avoid spam traps (never buy lists)

Content Best Practices

Subject line: 30-50 characters
Preheader: 40-100 characters
Text-to-image ratio: 80:20
Single clear CTA per email
Mobile-first design (60%+ open on mobile)

Send Time Optimization

B2B: Tuesday-Thursday, 9-11 AM
B2C: Saturday morning, weekday evenings
Test: Your audience may differ
Use: Send-time optimization features
Avoid: Monday morning, Friday afternoon

Pro Tip

Email marketing ROI is $36 for every $1 spent. Focus on list quality over quantity. 1,000 engaged subscribers outperform 100,000 inactive ones. Start with a welcome series and newsletter — add automation as you grow.
