No-Code AI Products

Custom GPTs

OpenAI's no-code builder. Upload docs, set instructions, share publicly. Free to create, $20/mo to use. Monetize via GPT Store.

Chatbase

Train chatbot on your data. Website, PDF, API. Custom actions. Embed on site. $19/mo. Great for customer support bots.

Voiceflow

Visual chatbot builder. Drag-and-drop flows. Integrations, variables, logic. Free tier. Best for complex conversation design.

Bolt.new / Lovable

AI full-stack app builders. Describe what you want → get working app. $20-50/mo. Great for MVPs and prototypes.

Code-First AI Products

Vercel AI SDK

React/Next.js AI toolkit. Streaming, tool calls, generative UI. OpenAI, Anthropic, Google adapters. Free. Best for AI apps.

LangChain

AI framework: chains, agents, RAG, memory. Python + JS. Complex workflows. Free. Enterprise: LangSmith ($39/mo).

CrewAI

Multi-agent framework. Agents collaborate on tasks. Roles, goals, tools. Python. Free. Great for research, content pipelines.

OpenAI API

Direct API access. GPT-4o, DALL-E, Whisper, TTS. Pay per token. Developer playground. Essential for custom integrations.
