Retention Strategy Playbook

Keep customers coming back — because retention is 5x cheaper than acquisition

🔄 Customer Lifecycle

Stage Goal Strategy
New (0-30 days) Onboard successfully Welcome sequence, quick wins, support check-in
Active (30-90 days) Build habit Regular content, community, progress tracking
Engaged (90-180 days) Deepen relationship Exclusive content, VIP access, personal touch
Loyal (180+ days) Turn into advocates Referral program, ambassador program, co-creation
At-Risk (inactive 30+ days) Re-engage Re-engagement campaign, win-back offer, survey

🎯 Retention Tactics

Quick Win Strategies

• Send personalized check-in emails • Create exclusive community (WhatsApp/Telegram) • Offer loyalty rewards/discounts • Host monthly live sessions/Q&As • Send handwritten thank-you notes (for high-value customers) • Create "surprise and delight" moments

💡 Retention Tips

• Track churn rate — if >5% monthly, you have a problem
• Reach out to at-risk customers BEFORE they leave
• Make it easy to stay (reduce friction)
• Build community — people stay for belonging
• Always over-deliver on expectations
