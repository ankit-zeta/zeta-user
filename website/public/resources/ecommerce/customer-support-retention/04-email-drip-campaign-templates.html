Email Drip Campaign Templates

Automate engagement — because you can't manually email every customer forever

📧 Onboarding Drip (7 emails)

Days 1-7 Sequence

DAY 1: Welcome + Quick Start Guide "Here's how to get the most from [Product]" DAY 2: First Win "Complete this one task and see results" DAY 3: Feature Deep Dive "Most people miss this powerful feature" DAY 5: Social Proof "See how [Customer] achieved [result]" DAY 7: Check-in "How's everything going? Any questions?" DAY 10: Advanced Tip "Ready for the next level? Try this" DAY 14: Upsell/Cross-sell "Now that you've seen the value, here's what's next"

🔄 Re-engagement Drip (5 emails)

Win-Back Sequence

DAY 1: "We miss you" "Here's what's new since you've been away" DAY 3: Value Reminder "Remember why you signed up? Here's what you've achieved" DAY 7: Special Offer "Come back and get [discount/bonus]" DAY 14: Last Chance "This offer expires in 48 hours" DAY 30: Goodbye (soft) "We'll stop emailing, but you can always come back"

💡 Drip Tips

• Personalize with customer name and behavior data
• Test send times (Tuesday-Thursday, 10 AM works well)
• Include clear CTA in every email
• Don't overwhelm — 1 email per 3-5 days max
• Monitor unsubscribe rate — if >0.5%, adjust frequency
