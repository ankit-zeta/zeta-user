Launch Roadmap

From idea to first sale in 30 days — because overthinking kills businesses

🚀 30-Day Launch Plan

Week 1: Research & Validate (Days 1-7)

Day 1-2: List 5 niche ideas Day 3: Quick Google Trends check Day 4-5: Talk to 10 potential customers Day 6: Competitor analysis Day 7: Pick your niche (just pick one!)

Week 2: Build MVP (Days 8-14)

Day 8-9: Create your first product/service Day 10-11: Set up payment (Razorpay/Stripe) Day 12-13: Build landing page (Carrd/WordPress) Day 14: Set up social media profiles

Week 3: Test & Iterate (Days 15-21)

Day 15-16: Soft launch to friends/family Day 17-18: Get feedback, fix issues Day 19-20: Start content marketing Day 21: Set up email list

Week 4: Launch & Scale (Days 22-30)

Day 22-23: Official launch Day 24-25: Run small ads (₹500/day) Day 26-27: Content creation spree Day 28-29: Partnership outreach Day 30: Review, celebrate, plan next month

💡 Launch Tips

• Done is better than perfect — launch ugly, improve later
• Your first product won't be your best — that's okay
• Get 10 paying customers before anything else
• Focus on ONE channel for first 30 days
• Track everything from day 1
