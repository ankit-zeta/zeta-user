Abandoned Cart Email Sequence

Recover 10-15% of lost sales with these automated emails

📧 3-Email Recovery Sequence

Email 1: Hour 1 — Gentle Reminder

Subject: You left something behind 👋 Hi [Name], I noticed you were checking out [Product Name] but didn't complete your order. No worries — it happens! Your cart is saved and ready when you are. [Product Image] [Product Name] [Price] Complete your order: [CTA Button] Questions? Reply to this email — I'm here to help! [Your Name]

Email 2: Hour 24 — Social Proof

Subject: [X] people bought this yesterday Hi [Name], Quick update — [Product Name] is selling fast! [X] customers bought it in the last 24 hours. Here's what they're saying: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ "[Customer review]" — [Customer Name] Don't miss out. Your cart is waiting: [CTA Button] [Your Name]

Email 3: Hour 48 — Incentive

Subject: Last chance — 10% off your cart Hi [Name], I want to make sure you don't miss out on [Product Name]. Here's a special 10% discount to complete your order: Code: COMEBACK10 Expires: [Date - 48 hours] [CTA Button] This is the last email about this — I'll stop bugging you after this. 🙂 [Your Name]

💡 Cart Recovery Tips

• Send within 1 hour — decay rate is 90% after 24 hours
• Include product image — visual reminder is powerful
• Keep discount small (5-10%) — protect margins
• Use urgency: "Expires in 24 hours"
• Automate this in Shopify — set it and forget it
