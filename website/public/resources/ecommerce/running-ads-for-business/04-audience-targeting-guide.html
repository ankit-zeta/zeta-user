Audience Targeting Guide

Reach the right people — because showing ads to everyone wastes money

🎯 Audience Types

Cold Audience

Never heard of you. Target by interests, demographics, behaviors. Build awareness first.

Warm Audience

Engaged with your brand. Website visitors, social followers, email subscribers. Ready for offers.

Hot Audience

High intent. Cart abandoners, product viewers, past customers. Ready to buy.

Lookalike Audience

People similar to your best customers. Platform finds them automatically. Scale tool.

🇮🇳 India-Specific Targeting

Language Targeting

Hindi, English, or both. Regional languages for tier 2/3 cities.

Location Targeting

Metro cities vs. tier 2/3. Different messaging for different markets.

Device Targeting

80%+ mobile users. Optimize for mobile first. Android dominant.

Time Targeting

Peak hours: 8-10 AM, 7-10 PM. Weekends for B2C.

💡 Targeting Tips

• Start broad, narrow down based on data
• Don't overlap audiences — it inflates costs
• Test 3-5 audience segments per campaign
• Use exclusion audiences to avoid wasted spend
• Review audience performance weekly
