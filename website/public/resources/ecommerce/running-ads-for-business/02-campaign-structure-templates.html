Meta Ads Structure

CAMPAIGN (Objective: Sales/Leads) ├── AD SET 1 (Audience: Cold - Interest) │ ├── Ad 1A (Creative: Video) │ ├── Ad 1B (Creative: Image) │ └── Ad 1C (Creative: Carousel) ├── AD SET 2 (Audience: Cold - Lookalike) │ ├── Ad 2A (Creative: Video) │ ├── Ad 2B (Creative: Image) │ └── Ad 2C (Creative: Carousel) └── AD SET 3 (Audience: Warm - Retargeting) ├── Ad 3A (Creative: Testimonial) ├── Ad 3B (Creative: Offer) └── Ad 3C (Creative: Urgency)

Google Ads Structure

CAMPAIGN (Type: Search) ├── AD GROUP 1 (Theme: Product Category) │ ├── Keyword 1: [exact match] │ ├── Keyword 2: "phrase match" │ └── Keyword 3: broad match modifier ├── AD GROUP 2 (Theme: Competitor) │ ├── Keyword 1: [competitor name] │ └── Keyword 2: [competitor alternative] └── AD GROUP 3 (Theme: Problem/Solution) ├── Keyword 1: [problem keyword] └── Keyword 2: [solution keyword]

💡 Structure Tips

• One objective per campaign — don't mix leads and sales
• Budget 70% on proven campaigns, 30% on testing
• Name campaigns consistently: [Platform]_[Objective]_[Audience]
• Review performance at ad set level, not just campaign level
• Pause underperformers after 3-5 days of data
