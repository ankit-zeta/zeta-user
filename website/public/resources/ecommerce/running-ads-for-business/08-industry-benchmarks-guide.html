Industry Benchmarks Guide

Know what's "good" — because you can't improve what you don't measure

📊 Meta Ads Benchmarks (India)

Metric Good Average Poor
CTR > 2% 1-2% < 1%
CPC < ₹10 ₹10-30 > ₹30
CPM < ₹200 ₹200-500 > ₹500
Conversion Rate > 3% 1-3% < 1%
ROAS > 4x 2-4x < 2x

📊 Google Ads Benchmarks (India)

Metric Good Average Poor
CTR (Search) > 5% 3-5% < 3%
CPC (Search) < ₹15 ₹15-50 > ₹50
Conversion Rate > 5% 2-5% < 2%
Quality Score 8-10 5-7 < 5

💡 Benchmark Tips

• These are averages — your mileage may vary by industry
• Track your own benchmarks over time
• Use benchmarks to set realistic goals
• Don't obsess over single metrics — look at the full picture
• Industry reports change quarterly — stay updated
