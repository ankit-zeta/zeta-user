Performance Diagnosis Flowchart

Fix underperforming ads — because not every ad will be a winner

🔍 Diagnosis Flowchart

Problem Likely Cause Fix
Low Impressions Budget too low, audience too narrow, bid too low Increase budget, broaden audience, raise bids
Low CTR (<1%) Weak creative, wrong audience, poor ad copy Test new creatives, refine targeting, rewrite copy
High CPC Poor quality score, low relevance, competition Improve ad relevance, refine keywords, test new audiences
Low Conversions Weak landing page, wrong offer, poor user experience Optimize landing page, test new offers, improve UX
High Cart Abandonment Unexpected costs, complicated checkout, trust issues Transparent pricing, simplify checkout, add trust badges
Low ROAS Wrong audience, poor targeting, weak creative Refine targeting, test new creatives, adjust bidding

🛠️ Quick Fixes

Emergency Actions

If CPC > ₹50: → Pause ad, check targeting If CTR < 0.5%: → New creative immediately If ROAS < 2x after 7 days: → Pause campaign, analyze data If conversions < 5/day: → Broaden audience, increase budget

💡 Diagnosis Tips

• Don't panic — ads take 3-7 days to optimize
• Change one thing at a time
• Compare against industry benchmarks
• Document what works and what doesn't
• Ask: "Is this a targeting, creative, or landing page issue?"
