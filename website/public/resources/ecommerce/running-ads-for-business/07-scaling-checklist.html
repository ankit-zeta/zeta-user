Scaling Checklist

Scale winning campaigns safely — because more spend ≠ more problems

📈 Pre-Scale Checklist

  • Consistent ROAS for 7+ days
    Minimum 3x ROAS before scaling. Don't scale losers.
  • Sufficient conversion data
    At least 50 conversions per week. More data = better optimization.
  • Stable frequency
    Frequency under 3. Higher = audience fatigue.
  • Landing page can handle traffic
    Test with higher traffic. Ensure fast loading.

🚀 Scaling Methods

Vertical Scaling (Budget Increase)

• Increase budget by 20% every 3-4 days • Don't increase more than 20% at once • Monitor performance closely • Pause if ROAS drops below 2x

Horizontal Scaling (New Audiences)

• Duplicate winning ad set • Test new audiences (interests, lookalikes) • Test new platforms (Google, YouTube) • Test new geographies

💡 Scaling Rules

• Never scale more than 20% at a time
• Always have a "control" campaign running
• Monitor daily for the first week after scaling
• Have a kill switch — know when to stop
• Scale winners, kill losers fast
