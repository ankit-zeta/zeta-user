Social Media Post Templates

Scroll-stopping posts — because you have 3 seconds to grab attention

📱 Instagram Templates

Educational Carousel Post

SLIDE 1: Hook title (big text, question or bold statement) SLIDE 2: The problem (pain point) SLIDE 3-6: Solution steps (one per slide) SLIDE 7: Summary/recap SLIDE 8: CTA ("Save this for later", "Follow for more") Caption: Hook + Value + CTA + Hashtags (5-10 relevant)

Engagement Post (Poll/Question)

"Which one are you? Option A: _______ Option B: _______ Option C: _______ Drop your answer below! ⬇️" Why it works: Low-friction engagement, boosts algorithm

🐦 Twitter/X Templates

Thread Template

TWEET 1 (Hook): "I spent [time] learning [topic]. Here are [number] things I wish I knew earlier:" TWEET 2-10: "One thing: [Specific insight] [Explanation in 1-2 sentences] [Example or proof]" FINAL TWEET: "If you found this helpful: • Follow me for more [topic] tips • RT the first tweet to help others • Bookmark this thread"

💡 Social Media Tips

• Post consistently (same time, same days)
• Use hashtags strategically (3-5 relevant ones)
• Engage with comments within 1 hour of posting
• Repurpose one piece of content across platforms
• Track what works — double down on winners
