Cold Call Scripts

Ready-to-use cold call scripts that get conversations started

📞 Opening Scripts

Script 1: The Referral

"Hi [Name], this is [Your Name] from [Company]. [Mutual Connection] suggested I reach out to you. They mentioned you're working on [specific project/challenge]. We've helped similar companies achieve [specific result]. I'd love to explore if we can do the same for you. Do you have 2 minutes right now, or should I call back at a better time?"

Script 2: The Value-First

"Hi [Name], I'm [Your Name] from [Company]. I'm calling because I noticed [specific observation about their business]. I have a quick idea that could help you [achieve specific result]. Would you be open to hearing it? It'll take 30 seconds."

💡 Cold Call Tips

• Smile while you talk — it changes your tone
• Stand up for more energy and confidence
• Have a script but sound natural — don't read robotically
• Goal is to book a meeting, not close the deal
• Track your metrics: calls made, conversations, meetings booked
