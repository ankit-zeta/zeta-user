Lead Scoring Spreadsheet Guide

Prioritize your leads — spend time on the ones most likely to convert

📊 Lead Scoring Model

Criteria Points How to Score
Company Size 1-10 1-10 employees: 1pt | 11-50: 5pt | 51+: 10pt
Budget Fit 1-15 No budget: 1pt | Has budget: 10pt | Pre-approved: 15pt
Decision Authority 1-10 Employee: 1pt | Manager: 5pt | Founder/CEO: 10pt
Timeline 1-10 No timeline: 1pt | 3+ months: 5pt | Within 30 days: 10pt
Engagement 1-15 Opened email: 1pt | Clicked link: 5pt | Visited pricing: 10pt | Booked call: 15pt

🎯 Score-Based Actions

Score Range Priority Action
40-60 Hot 🔥 Call immediately. These are ready to buy.
25-39 Warm 🌡️ Nurture with value. Schedule a call within 48 hours.
10-24 Cool ❄️ Add to email sequence. Re-engage in 2 weeks.
0-9 Cold 🧊 Low priority. Monthly newsletter only.

💡 Scoring Tips

• Review and adjust scores monthly based on conversion data
• Weight recent engagement higher than old activity
• Don't over-complicate — start simple, refine over time
• Use your CRM to automate scoring where possible
