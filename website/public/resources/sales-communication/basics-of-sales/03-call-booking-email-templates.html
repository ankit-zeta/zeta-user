Call Booking Email Templates

Get prospects excited to hop on a call with you

🎯 Cold Outreach Templates

Template 1: Value-First Cold Email

Subject: Quick idea for [Company Name] Hi [Name], I noticed [specific observation about their business - e.g., "you're running Google Ads but your landing page could convert better"]. I helped [Similar Company] increase their [metric] by [percentage/amount] in [timeframe]. I have a few ideas that could do the same for you. Would you be open to a 15-minute call this week? I'll share the specific strategies — no pitch, just value. Best, [Your Name] [Your Title] [Calendar Link]

Template 2: Problem-Solution Cold Email

Subject: [Company] + [Your Company] = [Benefit] Hi [Name], Most [industry] companies struggle with [specific problem]. Is that true for you too? We've developed a simple system that helps [target audience] [achieve specific result]. It worked for: • [Client 1]: [Result] • [Client 2]: [Result] • [Client 3]: [Result] Want to see if it works for [Company]? Book a quick call here: [Calendar Link] Or reply with "interested" and I'll send you a custom proposal. [Your Name]

Template 3: Social Proof Cold Email

Subject: How [Similar Company] solved [problem] Hi [Name], I work with [industry] companies like [Similar Company] to help them [achieve specific result]. Recently, we helped them: ✓ [Specific achievement 1] ✓ [Specific achievement 2] ✓ [Specific achievement 3] I'd love to explore if we can do the same for [Company]. Do you have 15 minutes this Thursday or Friday for a quick chat? [Your Name] [Calendar Link]

🔄 Follow-Up Templates

Follow-Up 1 (Day 3): Value Add

Subject: Thought you'd find this useful Hi [Name], I know you're busy, so I'll keep this brief. I came across this [resource] and thought of our conversation about [their challenge]: [Link to valuable content] Quick highlight: [1-2 sentences about why it's relevant] If you'd like to discuss how this applies to [Company], I'm happy to jump on a quick call. [Your Name]

Follow-Up 2 (Day 7): Social Proof

Subject: [Client] just hit [milestone] Hi [Name], Quick update — [Client Name] just achieved [specific result] using our approach. I remembered you were interested in [their goal]. Would a 15-minute call be helpful to explore if we can do the same for you? Here's my calendar: [Calendar Link] [Your Name]

Follow-Up 3 (Day 14): Breakup Email

Subject: Should I close your file? Hi [Name], I've reached out a few times about helping [Company] with [problem], but I haven't heard back. I'll assume the timing isn't right and close your file for now. If anything changes, feel free to reach out anytime. I'm always happy to help. All the best, [Your Name] P.S. If you're not the right person for this, could you point me to who is? Thanks!

📅 Meeting Confirmation Templates

Confirmation Email (Send Immediately After Booking)

Subject: Confirmed: Our call on [Date] at [Time] Hi [Name], Looking forward to our call! 📅 Date: [Date] 🕐 Time: [Time] ([Timezone]) 📍 Location: [Zoom/Google Meet/Phone Number] ⏱ Duration: [X] minutes Here's what we'll cover: 1. [Agenda Item 1] 2. [Agenda Item 2] 3. [Agenda Item 3] If you need to reschedule, please let me know at least 24 hours in advance. See you soon! [Your Name]

Reminder Email (1 Day Before)

Subject: Reminder: Our call tomorrow at [Time] Hi [Name], Just a friendly reminder about our call tomorrow: 📅 [Date] at [Time] ([Timezone]) 📍 [Meeting Link] I'm looking forward to learning more about [Company] and exploring how we can help. See you tomorrow! [Your Name]

💡 Pro Tips for Higher Booking Rates

• Keep emails under 150 words — busy people skim
• Use their name in the subject line (+26% open rate)
• Send emails Tuesday-Thursday, 8-10 AM their time
• Always include a clear CTA (one action only)
• Follow up 3-5 times — most sales happen after follow-up #3
