CRM & Sales Tools

  • HubSpot CRM (Free)
    Contacts, deals, pipeline, email tracking, meeting scheduler. Free forever. Best for beginners starting with CRM.
    hubspot.com/crm →
    FreePipelineEmail Tracking
  • Pipedrive
    Visual sales pipeline. Activity reminders, email sync, reporting. $14.90/user. Great for visual learners.
    pipedrive.com →
    VisualPipeline$15/mo
  • Calendly
    Scheduling links. Eliminates back-and-forth emails. Free tier available. Integrates with CRM.

💡 Pro Tip

Start with HubSpot CRM (free) + Calendly (free). Upgrade to Pipedrive only when you need advanced pipeline features. Focus on building the habit of logging every interaction before investing in tools.
