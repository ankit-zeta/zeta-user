📊 Key Metrics to Track Weekly

1. Lead Response Time

How fast you respond to new leads. Speed wins — respond within 5 minutes for 10x higher conversion.

Formula: Time from lead capture to first contact

2. Conversion Rate (Lead → Customer)

What percentage of leads become paying customers. Industry average: 2-5%.

Formula: (Customers ÷ Total Leads) × 100

3. Average Deal Size

Average revenue per closed deal. Increase by upselling and offering packages.

Formula: Total Revenue ÷ Number of Deals

4. Sales Cycle Length

Average days from first contact to closed deal. Shorter = more efficient.

Formula: Total Days to Close ÷ Number of Deals

5. Pipeline Value

Total potential revenue in your pipeline. Your "forecast" of future income.

Formula: Sum of (Deal Value × Probability %) for all active deals
