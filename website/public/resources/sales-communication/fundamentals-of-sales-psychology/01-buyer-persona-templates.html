🎯 Persona 1: The Startup Founder

🚀
Rahul, 28
Founder, Tech Startup • Mumbai

Goals

Scale quickly, raise funding, build team, reach product-market fit

Pain Points

Limited budget, wears many hats, fears burning cash, needs to prove traction

How They Buy

Research-heavy, values ROI data, needs quick wins, prefers monthly plans

What Motivates

Growth metrics, competitive advantage, investor appeal, speed to market

🎯 Persona 2: The Corporate Manager

🏢
Priya, 35
Marketing Manager, MNC • Bangalore

Goals

Meet quarterly targets, get promoted, manage team efficiently, impress leadership

Pain Points

Budget constraints, bureaucracy, needs approvals, fears making mistakes publicly

How They Buy

Needs business case, compares vendors, requires demos, prefers annual contracts

What Motivates

Career growth, team success, process improvement, industry recognition

🎯 Persona 3: The Freelancer

💻
Amit, 25
Freelance Designer • Delhi

Goals

Steady income, better clients, work-life balance, creative freedom

Pain Points

Inconsistent income, scope creep, difficult clients, isolation, no benefits

How They Buy

Price-sensitive, needs social proof, prefers pay-as-you-go, trusts peers

What Motivates

Financial stability, portfolio building, creative control, flexibility

🎯 Persona 4: The Agency Owner

📢
Vikram, 32
Owner, Digital Agency • Pune

Goals

Client retention, team productivity, profit margins, agency growth

Pain Points

Client churn, hiring challenges, cash flow, competition, burnout

How They Buy

Values efficiency, needs team features, prefers white-label, long-term contracts

What Motivates

Scalability, reputation, recurring revenue, team empowerment
