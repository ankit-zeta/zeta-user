Follow-Up Sequence Templates

5-touch follow-up sequence that converts cold leads into warm conversations

📅 The 5-Touch Sequence

Touch 1: Day 1 — Value-First Email

Subject: Quick idea for [Company Name] Hi [Name], I noticed [specific observation about their business]. I helped [Similar Company] increase their [metric] by [percentage] in [timeframe]. Would you be open to a 15-minute call? I'll share the exact strategy — no pitch, just value. Best, [Your Name]

Touch 2: Day 3 — Social Proof

Subject: How [Client] solved [their problem] Hi [Name], Quick case study: [Client Name] was struggling with [problem]. They tried [solution] but it didn't work. We helped them [specific result] in [timeframe]. Full story: [Link] Would this work for [Company]? [Your Name]

Touch 3: Day 7 — Helpful Resource

Subject: Thought you'd find this useful Hi [Name], I came across this [resource] and thought of our conversation about [their challenge]. [Link to valuable content] Key insight: [1-2 sentences] Worth a quick read. Let me know what you think! [Your Name]

Touch 4: Day 14 — Direct Question

Subject: Quick question, [Name] Hi [Name], I know you're busy, so I'll keep this brief. Are you still working on [their goal/challenge]? If yes, I have some ideas that could help. If no, no worries — just let me know. [Your Name]

Touch 5: Day 21 — Breakup Email

Subject: Should I close your file? Hi [Name], I've reached out a few times but haven't heard back. I'll assume the timing isn't right and close your file for now. If anything changes, I'm always happy to help. Just reply to this email. All the best, [Your Name] P.S. If someone else on your team handles this, could you point me to them? Thanks!

💡 Sequence Tips

• Always personalize — never send generic templates
• Vary your subject lines — they determine if your email gets opened
• Each touch should provide value, not just "checking in"
• Track opens/clicks to know who's engaged
• If they reply, stop the sequence and have a conversation
