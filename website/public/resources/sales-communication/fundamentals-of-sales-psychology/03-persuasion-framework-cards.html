🧠 Cialdini's 6 Principles of Influence

1. Reciprocity

People feel obligated to give back when they receive something first.

In Sales: Offer a free audit, valuable insight, or helpful resource BEFORE asking for anything. "I noticed your website has a few issues — here's a free audit. If you want help fixing them, I'm here."

2. Commitment & Consistency

People want to be consistent with what they've already said or done.

In Sales: Get small "yes" commitments first. "Would you agree that increasing leads would help your business?" Once they say yes, they're more likely to say yes to your solution.

3. Social Proof

People follow what others are doing, especially those similar to them.

In Sales: "We've helped 50+ agencies like yours. Here's what [Client Name] said: [testimonial]." Use case studies from similar industries.

4. Authority

People trust experts and权威 figures.

In Sales: Share credentials, certifications, media features, or speaking engagements. "I've been in this space for 10 years and helped 200+ clients."

5. Liking

People buy from people they like. Find common ground and be genuine.

In Sales: "I saw you're into cricket — me too! Who's your favorite player?" Build rapport before business. Mirror their communication style.

6. Scarcity

People want what's limited or might run out. Use ethically — never fake scarcity.

In Sales: "We only take 5 clients per month to ensure quality. I have 2 spots left this month." Real scarcity only — never lie about availability.

💡 Ethical Use Only

These principles are powerful. Use them to HELP people make decisions that benefit them, not to manipulate. If the product isn't right for them, say so. Long-term reputation > short-term sales.
