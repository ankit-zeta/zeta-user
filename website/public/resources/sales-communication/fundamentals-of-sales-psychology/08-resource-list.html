Fundamentals of Sales Psychology

Sales Psychology Resources

Books, courses, and tools for understanding buyer psychology and ethical persuasion

Must-Read Books

  • Influence: The Psychology of Persuasion (Robert Cialdini)
    6 principles: Reciprocity, Commitment, Social Proof, Authority, Liking, Scarcity. The bible of ethical persuasion.
    BookClassicEssential
  • Pre-Suasion (Robert Cialdini)
    How to prime people before your pitch. Framing, attention direction, context setting. The sequel to Influence.
    BookAdvanced
  • Thinking, Fast and Slow (Daniel Kahneman)
    System 1 (fast, emotional) vs System 2 (slow, logical). Cognitive biases. Explains why people buy irrationally.
    BookPsychologyNobel Prize
  • The Art of Negotiation (Michael Wheeler)
    Harvard Business School professor. Real negotiation psychology. Not scripts — mindset and strategy.
    BookNegotiation

Online Courses

    Practical course on buyer behavior, objection handling, closing techniques. ~$15 on sale. 4.5 stars.

💡 Pro Tip

Read "Influence" first — it covers 80% of what you need. Then practice one principle per week in real conversations. After 6 weeks, you'll naturally weave persuasion into your sales process.
