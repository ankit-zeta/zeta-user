📋 Pre-Negotiation Checklist

Know Your Numbers

My Ideal Outcome: _______________ My Walk-Away Point: _______________ My Target Price: ₹_______________ My Opening Offer: ₹_______________ Their Likely Opening Offer: ₹_______________ What's my BATNA (Best Alternative)? _______________ What's their likely BATNA? _______________

Know Their Needs

Their Top 3 Priorities: 1. _______________ 2. _______________ 3. _______________ Their Constraints: • Budget: _______________ • Timeline: _______________ • Decision makers: _______________ What can I offer that costs me little but matters to them? _______________

💡 Negotiation Prep Tips

• Always negotiate from a position of strength — know your alternatives
• Never accept the first offer — always counter
• Focus on interests, not positions — find creative solutions
• Be willing to walk away — it's your strongest negotiating tool
• Document everything — verbal agreements aren't binding
