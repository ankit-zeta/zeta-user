Next Steps (5 min) - Schedule follow-up if needed PRE-WORK: [What attendees should prepare/review before the meeting]

⏰ Time Management Rules

The 50/10 Rule

• Meetings should be 50 minutes max (not 60) • Last 10 minutes for action items + wrap-up • Or use 25/5 for shorter meetings • Start on time, end on time — always • If agenda is done early, end early

💡 Meeting Efficiency Tips

• Send agenda 24 hours before — no agenda, no meeting
• Limit attendees to essential people only
• Assign a timekeeper to keep discussions on track
• End with clear action items: Who + What + When
• Follow up within 24 hours with meeting notes
