Professional Bio Templates

Write a bio that wins clients — because first impressions matter

✍️ Bio Templates

Template 1: The Authority Bio

[Name] is a [Title] who helps [Target Audience] achieve [Specific Result]. With [X] years of experience in [Industry/Field], [Name] has worked with [Notable Clients/Companies] to [Specific Achievement]. [Name]'s work has been featured in [Publications/Media]. When not [Professional Activity], [Name] enjoys [Personal Interest]. [CTA: Book a call / Learn more / Contact]

Template 2: The Story Bio

[Name] started [Company/Career] after [Origin Story — what problem they faced or saw]. Today, they help [Target Audience] [Achieve Result] through [Method/Approach]. Key achievements: • [Achievement 1 with number] • [Achievement 2 with number] • [Achievement 3 with number] [Name] believes [Core Belief/Philosophy about their work].

Template 3: The LinkedIn Bio

[Title] | Helping [Target Audience] [Result] | [Key Credential] What I do: → [Service/Offer 1] → [Service/Offer 2] → [Service/Offer 3] Past: [Previous experience] Featured: [Media/Publications] Contact: [Email/Link] #Hashtag1 #Hashtag2 #Hashtag3

💡 Bio Writing Tips

• Lead with results, not responsibilities
• Use specific numbers — "helped 100+ clients" not "helped many"
• Keep it under 150 words for most platforms
• Update quarterly with new achievements
• Write in third person for formal contexts, first person for casual
